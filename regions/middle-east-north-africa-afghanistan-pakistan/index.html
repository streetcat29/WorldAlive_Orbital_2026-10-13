Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan population

828,025,185 people live in Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan — 10.1% of everyone alive, across 23 economies. Pakistan alone holds 31% of the region; the three largest (Pakistan, Egypt, Iran) hold 56%. Population-weighted growth is +1.82% a year, so the region adds about 15,029,594 people annually. The fastest-growing country is United Arab Emirates (+4.68%/yr); the oldest society is Malta (20.5% aged 65+).
