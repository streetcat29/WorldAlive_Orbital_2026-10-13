Sub-Saharan Africa population

1,321,654,217 people live in Sub-Saharan Africa — 16.1% of everyone alive, across 48 economies. Nigeria alone holds 18% of the region; the three largest (Nigeria, Ethiopia, DR Congo) hold 37%. Population-weighted growth is +2.43% a year, so the region adds about 32,123,330 people annually. The fastest-growing country is Chad (+3.41%/yr); the oldest society is Mauritius (14.1% aged 65+).
