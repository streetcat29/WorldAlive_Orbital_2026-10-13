East Asia & Pacific population

2,367,527,180 people live in East Asia & Pacific — 28.8% of everyone alive, across 37 economies. China alone holds 59% of the region; the three largest (China, Indonesia, Japan) hold 77%. Population-weighted growth is +0.11% a year, so the region adds about 2,650,527 people annually. The fastest-growing country is Solomon Islands (+2.35%/yr); the oldest society is Japan (30.0% aged 65+).
