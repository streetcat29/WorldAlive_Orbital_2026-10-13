North America population

383,501,065 people live in North America — 4.7% of everyone alive, across 3 economies. United States alone holds 89% of the region; the three largest (United States, Canada, Bermuda) hold 100%. Population-weighted growth is +0.57% a year, so the region adds about 2,176,791 people annually. The fastest-growing country is Canada (+0.94%/yr); the oldest society is Bermuda (22.6% aged 65+).
