Europe & Central Asia population

932,525,035 people live in Europe & Central Asia — 11.4% of everyone alive, across 58 economies. Russia alone holds 15% of the region; the three largest (Russia, Türkiye, Germany) hold 34%. Population-weighted growth is +0.43% a year, so the region adds about 3,997,183 people annually. The fastest-growing country is Georgia (+3.18%/yr); the oldest society is Monaco (36.0% aged 65+).
