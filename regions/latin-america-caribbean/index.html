Latin America & Caribbean population

666,592,570 people live in Latin America & Caribbean — 8.1% of everyone alive, across 42 economies. Brazil alone holds 32% of the region; the three largest (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia) hold 60%. Population-weighted growth is +0.66% a year, so the region adds about 4,426,722 people annually. The fastest-growing country is Honduras (+1.65%/yr); the oldest society is Puerto Rico (25.3% aged 65+).
