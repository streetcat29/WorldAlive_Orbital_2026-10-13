South Asia population

1,692,252,900 people live in South Asia — 20.6% of everyone alive, across 6 economies. India alone holds 87% of the region; the three largest (India, Bangladesh, Nepal) hold 99%. Population-weighted growth is +0.88% a year, so the region adds about 14,939,382 people annually. The fastest-growing country is Bangladesh (+1.22%/yr); the oldest society is Sri Lanka (12.5% aged 65+).
