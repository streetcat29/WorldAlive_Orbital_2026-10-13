World population history, 1960–2025

In 1960 there were 3,021,512,598 people on Earth. By 2025 there were 8,215,424,893 — 2.72× as many, an average of 79,906,343 more people every year for 65 years. Growth peaked in the late 1960s at over 2% a year and has slowed to +0.91% today, yet the world still adds about 74,867,471 people a year.

The billion milestones

Each additional billion has arrived at a similar or faster pace since the data begins: 4→5 billion took 13 years, 7→8 billion 12 years. With growth now below 1% a year, the UN expects the gap to widen again: 9 billion is not projected before the late 2030s.
