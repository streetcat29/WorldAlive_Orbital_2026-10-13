The world when you were born

Pick your birth date and World Alive shows the planet as it was that day — how many people were alive, how many babies have been born since, how many people you have shared the Earth with, which billion milestones you lived through, and how your country changed. Everything is modelled from World Bank annual data and stays on your device.

How it is calculated

Population on your birth date: exponential interpolation between mid-year anchors. Babies born since: each year's population × crude birth rate, for the fraction of the year inside your lifetime — the same for deaths. "People you have shared the planet with" = population at birth + births since. Milestones: the first instant the world series crossed each billion. "Right now" uses the same live projection as the counter at the top of the site.
