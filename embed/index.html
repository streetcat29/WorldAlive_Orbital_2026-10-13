Terms & notes

  
Free to embed on any website, newsletter web version or intranet, commercial or not, as long as the "theworldalive.com" credit inside the widget stays visible. The widget loads one file of about 400 bytes plus a flag image; it sets no cookies and sends nothing about your readers anywhere (neither the widget nor this configuration page loads analytics). Figures are continuous estimates projected from annual data (World Bank; UN World Urbanization Prospects for urban areas; national statistics offices for province-level cities) — not a census. Data refreshes when the site does. Questions or a custom widget: theworldalivecom@gmail.com.
