Contact World Alive

Corrections, accessibility problems and useful questions are welcome.

Email: theworldalivecom@gmail.com. Selecting the address opens your email application; nothing is submitted by this page.

Report a data issue

  1. Copy the page address and identify the exact figure or wording.
  2. State the place, reference year and geographic definition.
  3. Include an official source when available.

Report a usability issue

Include browser, device, day/night mode and steps that reproduce the problem. Avoid sending birth dates, private memories or identifying photos. Screenshots should be cropped to the issue.

This is a site contact, not an emergency or government service. No claim is made about response times. Read our editorial standards.
