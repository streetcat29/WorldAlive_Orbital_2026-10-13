Why the larger number is not automatically the better answer

The bundled GeoNames settlement record shows 5,638,830; the UN urban-centre estimate is 4,150,917 for 2025. The UN figure is smaller here. “Urban area” therefore must not be described as automatically larger than every administrative record.

Differences in boundaries, source vintages and source definitions can all matter. GeoNames does not supply a population reference date for this local record, so the gap cannot be attributed to recent growth or population loss.

Use a consistent definition for Sydney's comparisons, and do not add suburb values to the parent population: they are not additional people. Read the boundary explanation.
