Corrections and data quality

Found a wrong number, boundary, source date or broken interaction?

  1. Include the page address and exact figure or wording.
  2. Provide source URL, reference year and geographic definition where possible.
  3. Corrections should update the generator and the visible page.
  4. Facts that cannot be verified should be marked unknown or withdrawn—not guessed.
