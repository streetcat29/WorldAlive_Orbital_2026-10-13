Sources, definitions and calculations

Moving digits are modelled estimates, not a live census. Geographic definitions and reference years matter as much as the numbers.

Population basis

Province, administrative settlement and UN urban centre are different geographies. Each city page states which figure drives its headline, ratios and same-definition rankings. Separate figures are context, not interchangeable estimates. A settlement coordinate does not establish containment within an urban centre.

Live projection

Where explicitly used: P(t) = P0 × exp(r × elapsed years). The page states its base, model anchor and annual rate. Country growth is not a measurement of local growth. Missing dates remain unknown; a modelling anchor must not be interpreted as an observed census date.

Birthdays and stories

The birthday calculator interpolates annual mid-year population anchors; annual birth/death rates support modelled cumulative flows. Your World Story uses annual reference values, not an invented exact-day count. It marks unavailable observations rather than substituting zero. AI-authored passages are selected from a prewritten local library; numbers come from calculations, never generated text.

Projection is not destiny

UN medium projections are scenarios, not individual predictions. Life expectancy at birth describes a population and period; it does not forecast your remaining lifespan.

Known gaps

Istanbul has 39 reference-confirmed districts in the bundled list, but only 38 retained district population values; Büyükçekmece's district population is unknown. Membership coverage does not imply independently verified population coverage. Other places may have incomplete divisions or mixed-year observations.
