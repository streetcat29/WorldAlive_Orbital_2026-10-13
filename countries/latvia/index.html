How Latvia's population is changing

Latvia's population is shrinking: −0.99% a year, which works out to about −18,249 people per year, or −50 a day. Births (6.9 per 1,000) and deaths (14.3 per 1,000) alone would give −0.74% a year; net migration removes about 7,330 people a year (−0.40%). Roughly one in every 4,446 people on Earth lives in Latvia.

Population of Latvia since 1960

In 1960, Latvia had 2,120,979 people. By 2025 that was 1,847,785 — 13% smaller. The population peaked in 1989 at 2,666,955 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 11%.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
