How Timor-Leste's population is changing

Timor-Leste's population is growing: +1.27% a year, which works out to about +17,993 people per year, or +49 a day. Births (21.8 per 1,000) and deaths (7.2 per 1,000) alone would give +1.45% a year; net migration removes about 2,550 people a year (−0.18%). Roughly one in every 5,792 people on Earth lives in Timor-Leste.

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
