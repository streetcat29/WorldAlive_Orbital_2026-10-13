How Hungary's population is changing

Hungary's population is shrinking: −0.50% a year, which works out to about −47,694 people per year, or −131 a day. Births (8.2 per 1,000) and deaths (13.4 per 1,000) alone would give −0.52% a year; net migration removes about 16,331 people a year (−0.17%). Roughly one in every 863 people on Earth lives in Hungary.

Population of Hungary since 1960

In 1960, Hungary had 9,983,512 people. By 2025 that was 9,514,251 — almost unchanged. The population peaked in 1981 at 10,711,848 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1967 and 1977, when the population rose by 4%.

Hungary from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put Hungary at about 3,250,000 people in 1800; the UN estimates 9,632,286 in 2025 (3× 1800) and projects 7,460,740 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 1980 at 10,695,372 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
