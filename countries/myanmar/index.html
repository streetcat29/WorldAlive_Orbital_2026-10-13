How Myanmar's population is changing

Myanmar's population is growing: +0.64% a year, which works out to about +351,682 people per year, or +963 a day. Births (16.5 per 1,000) and deaths (9.2 per 1,000) alone would give +0.73% a year; net migration removes about 39,661 people a year (−0.07%). Roughly one in every 150 people on Earth lives in Myanmar.

Myanmar from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put Myanmar at about 6,000,000 people in 1800; the UN estimates 54,850,648 in 2025 (9× 1800) and projects 49,658,883 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 2049 at 58,633,124 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
