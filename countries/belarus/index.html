How Belarus's population is changing

Belarus's population is shrinking: −0.51% a year, which works out to about −46,519 people per year, or −127 a day. Births (7.0 per 1,000) and deaths (13.1 per 1,000) alone would give −0.61% a year; net migration removes about 3,020 people a year (−0.03%). Roughly one in every 904 people on Earth lives in Belarus.

Population of Belarus since 1960

In 1960, Belarus had 8,198,000 people. By 2025 that was 9,085,991 — 1.1× larger. The population peaked in 1993 at 10,239,050 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 10%.
