How Georgia's population is changing

Georgia's population is growing fast: +3.18% a year, which works out to about +125,219 people per year, or +343 a day. Births (11.3 per 1,000) and deaths (11.8 per 1,000) alone would give −0.05% a year; net migration adds about 1,283 people a year (+0.03%). Population of Georgia since 1960

In 1960, Georgia had 3,645,600 people. By 2025 that was 3,935,766 — 1.1× larger. The population peaked in 1993 at 4,911,100 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 13%.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
