How Nicaragua's population is changing

Nicaragua's population is growing: +1.31% a year, which works out to about +91,963 people per year, or +252 a day. Births (19.1 per 1,000) and deaths (4.6 per 1,000) alone would give +1.45% a year; net migration removes about 8,371 people a year (−0.12%). Roughly one in every 1,172 people on Earth lives in Nicaragua.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
