How Ukraine's population is changing

Ukraine's population is growing fast: +2.92% a year, which works out to about +1,136,565 people per year, or +3,112 a day. Births (5.5 per 1,000) and deaths (13.6 per 1,000) alone would give −0.81% a year; net migration adds about 1,702,358 people a year (+4.37%). Roughly one in every 211 people on Earth lives in Ukraine.

Population of Ukraine since 1960

In 1960, Ukraine had 42,824,093 people. By 2025 that was 38,980,376 — 9% smaller. The population peaked in 1993 at 52,350,126 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 10%.

Ukraine from 1800 to 2100

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
