How Palestine's population is changing

Palestine's population is growing fast: +2.33% a year, which works out to about +125,897 people per year, or +345 a day. Births (26.5 per 1,000) and deaths (5.9 per 1,000) alone would give +2.06% a year; net migration removes about 23,156 people a year (−0.43%). Roughly one in every 1,518 people on Earth lives in Palestine.
