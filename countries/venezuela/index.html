How Venezuela's population is changing

Venezuela's population is growing: +0.39% a year, which works out to about +111,571 people per year, or +305 a day. Births (15.2 per 1,000) and deaths (7.7 per 1,000) alone would give +0.76% a year; net migration removes about 100,467 people a year (−0.35%). Roughly one in every 288 people on Earth lives in Venezuela.

Population of Venezuela since 1960

In 1960, Venezuela had 8,160,090 people. By 2025 that was 28,516,896 — 3.5× larger. The population peaked in 2016 at 30,765,720 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 40%.

Venezuela from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put Venezuela at about 1,000,000 people in 1800; the UN estimates 28,516,900 in 2025 (29× 1800) and projects 28,280,554 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 2057 at 31,213,041 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
