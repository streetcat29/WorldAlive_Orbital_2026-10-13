How Lithuania's population is changing

Lithuania's population is roughly stable: +0.02% a year, which works out to about +496 people per year, or +1 a day. Births (6.6 per 1,000) and deaths (13.0 per 1,000) alone would give −0.64% a year; net migration removes about 24,618 people a year (−0.85%). Roughly one in every 2,844 people on Earth lives in Lithuania.

Population of Lithuania since 1960

In 1960, Lithuania had 2,778,550 people. By 2025 that was 2,888,774 — almost unchanged. The population peaked in 1991 at 3,704,134 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 13%.
