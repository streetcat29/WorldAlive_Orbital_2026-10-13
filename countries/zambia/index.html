How Zambia's population is changing

Zambia's population is growing fast: +2.77% a year, which works out to about +607,255 people per year, or +1,663 a day. Births (32.7 per 1,000) and deaths (5.2 per 1,000) alone would give +2.76% a year; net migration adds about 6,562 people a year (+0.03%). Roughly one in every 375 people on Earth lives in Zambia.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
