How United Arab Emirates's population is changing

United Arab Emirates's population is growing fast: +4.68% a year, which works out to about +539,180 people per year, or +1,476 a day. Births (9.8 per 1,000) and deaths (1.0 per 1,000) alone would give +0.88% a year; net migration adds about 158,634 people a year (+1.38%). Roughly one in every 714 people on Earth lives in United Arab Emirates.

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
