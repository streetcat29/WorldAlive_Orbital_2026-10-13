How Gabon's population is changing

Gabon's population is growing fast: +2.11% a year, which works out to about +54,752 people per year, or +150 a day. Births (27.1 per 1,000) and deaths (6.2 per 1,000) alone would give +2.09% a year; net migration adds about 1,116 people a year (+0.04%). Roughly one in every 3,168 people on Earth lives in Gabon.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
