How Romania's population is changing

Romania's population is roughly stable: −0.17% a year, which works out to about −31,507 people per year, or −86 a day. Births (7.5 per 1,000) and deaths (12.8 per 1,000) alone would give −0.53% a year; net migration removes about 29,041 people a year (−0.15%). Roughly one in every 432 people on Earth lives in Romania.

Population of Romania since 1960

In 1960, Romania had 18,406,905 people. By 2025 that was 19,020,271 — almost unchanged. The population peaked in 1990 at 23,201,835 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1966 and 1976, when the population rose by 12%.

Romania from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put Romania at about 5,500,000 people in 1800; the UN estimates 18,908,655 in 2025 (3× 1800) and projects 10,768,185 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 1991 at 22,947,416 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
