How Cameroon's population is changing

Cameroon's population is growing fast: +2.56% a year, which works out to about +765,311 people per year, or +2,095 a day. Births (33.3 per 1,000) and deaths (7.0 per 1,000) alone would give +2.63% a year; net migration removes about 15,379 people a year (−0.05%). Roughly one in every 275 people on Earth lives in Cameroon.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
