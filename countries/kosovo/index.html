How Kosovo's population is changing

Kosovo's population is shrinking: −1.10% a year, which works out to about −17,381 people per year, or −48 a day. Births (11.9 per 1,000) and deaths (6.0 per 1,000) alone would give +0.59% a year; net migration removes about 18,380 people a year (−1.17%). Roughly one in every 5,210 people on Earth lives in Kosovo.

Population of Kosovo since 1960

In 1960, Kosovo had 984,846 people. By 2025 that was 1,576,876 — 1.6× larger. The population peaked in 1997 at 2,197,284 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1971 and 1981, when the population rose by 29%.

Modelled estimates put Kosovo at about 383,800 people in 1800; the UN estimates 1,674,131 in 2025 (4× 1800) and projects 1,094,725 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 1997 at 2,197,286 and decline afterwards.

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement.
