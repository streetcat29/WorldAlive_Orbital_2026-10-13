How Eswatini's population is changing

Eswatini's population is growing: +1.07% a year, which works out to about +13,423 people per year, or +37 a day. Births (23.6 per 1,000) and deaths (7.7 per 1,000) alone would give +1.59% a year; net migration removes about 5,775 people a year (−0.46%). Roughly one in every 6,540 people on Earth lives in Eswatini.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
