How Kuwait's population is changing

Kuwait's population is shrinking: −0.65% a year, which works out to about −31,860 people per year, or −87 a day. Births (10.0 per 1,000) and deaths (1.5 per 1,000) alone would give +0.85% a year; net migration adds about 42,775 people a year (+0.88%). Roughly one in every 1,689 people on Earth lives in Kuwait.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
