How Thailand's population is changing

Thailand's population is roughly stable: −0.07% a year, which works out to about −48,132 people per year, or −132 a day. Births (8.1 per 1,000) and deaths (9.0 per 1,000) alone would give −0.09% a year; net migration adds about 27,509 people a year (+0.04%). Roughly one in every 115 people on Earth lives in Thailand.

Thailand from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put Thailand at about 3,000,000 people in 1800; the UN estimates 71,619,866 in 2025 (24× 1800) and projects 45,555,066 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 2022 at 71,735,321 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
