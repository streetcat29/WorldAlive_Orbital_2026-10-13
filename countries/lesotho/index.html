How Lesotho's population is changing

Lesotho's population is growing: +1.10% a year, which works out to about +26,045 people per year, or +71 a day. Births (23.8 per 1,000) and deaths (10.5 per 1,000) alone would give +1.33% a year; net migration removes about 5,192 people a year (−0.22%). Roughly one in every 3,476 people on Earth lives in Lesotho.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
