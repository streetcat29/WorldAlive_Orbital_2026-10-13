How Croatia's population is changing

Croatia's population is roughly stable: +0.26% a year, which works out to about +10,013 people per year, or +27 a day. Births (8.3 per 1,000) and deaths (13.2 per 1,000) alone would give −0.49% a year; net migration removes about 3,481 people a year (−0.09%). Roughly one in every 2,119 people on Earth lives in Croatia.

Population of Croatia since 1960

In 1960, Croatia had 4,140,181 people. By 2025 that was 3,876,200 — 6% smaller. The population peaked in 1990 at 4,777,368 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 7%.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
