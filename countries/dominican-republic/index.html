How Dominican Republic's population is changing

Dominican Republic's population is growing: +0.81% a year, which works out to about +93,307 people per year, or +255 a day. Births (17.6 per 1,000) and deaths (6.2 per 1,000) alone would give +1.14% a year; net migration removes about 35,306 people a year (−0.31%). Roughly one in every 713 people on Earth lives in Dominican Republic.
