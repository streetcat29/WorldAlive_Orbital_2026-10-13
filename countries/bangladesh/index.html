How Bangladesh's population is changing

Bangladesh's population is growing: +1.22% a year, which works out to about +2,137,485 people per year, or +5,852 a day. Births (20.0 per 1,000) and deaths (5.1 per 1,000) alone would give +1.49% a year; net migration removes about 402,100 people a year (−0.23%). Roughly one in every 47 people on Earth lives in Bangladesh.

Bangladesh from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put Bangladesh at about 19,191,243 people in 1800; the UN estimates 175,686,904 in 2025 (9× 1800) and projects 208,596,514 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 2071 at 226,129,886 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
