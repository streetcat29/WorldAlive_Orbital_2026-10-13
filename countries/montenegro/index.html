How Montenegro's population is changing

Montenegro's population is roughly stable: −0.06% a year, which works out to about −396 people per year, or −1 a day. Births (11.2 per 1,000) and deaths (10.2 per 1,000) alone would give +0.10% a year; net migration removes about 9,279 people a year (−1.49%). Roughly one in every 13,184 people on Earth lives in Montenegro.

Population of Montenegro since 1960

In 1960, Montenegro had 480,579 people. By 2025 that was 623,129 — 1.3× larger. The population peaked in 2019 at 626,753 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1972 and 1982, when the population rose by 12%.
