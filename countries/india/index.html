How India's population is changing

India's population is growing: +0.89% a year, which works out to about +12,987,174 people per year, or +35,558 a day. Births (16.0 per 1,000) and deaths (6.6 per 1,000) alone would give +0.94% a year; net migration removes about 495,753 people a year (−0.03%). Roughly one in every 6 people on Earth lives in India.

India from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put India at about 200,733,034 people in 1800; the UN estimates 1,463,865,527 in 2025 (7× 1800) and projects 1,505,251,749 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 2061 at 1,701,283,705 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
