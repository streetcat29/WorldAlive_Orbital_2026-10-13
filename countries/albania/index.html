How Albania's population is changing

Albania's population is shrinking: −1.17% a year, which works out to about −27,388 people per year, or −75 a day. Births (10.1 per 1,000) and deaths (8.5 per 1,000) alone would give +0.17% a year; net migration removes about 24,230 people a year (−1.03%). Roughly one in every 3,497 people on Earth lives in Albania.

Population of Albania since 1960

In 1960, Albania had 1,608,800 people. By 2025 that was 2,349,580 — 1.5× larger. The population peaked in 1990 at 3,286,542 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 33%.
