How Mozambique's population is changing

Mozambique's population is growing fast: +2.85% a year, which works out to about +1,014,184 people per year, or +2,777 a day. Births (37.0 per 1,000) and deaths (6.9 per 1,000) alone would give +3.01% a year; net migration removes about 65,573 people a year (−0.18%). Roughly one in every 231 people on Earth lives in Mozambique.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
