How Bhutan's population is changing

Bhutan's population is growing: +0.65% a year, which works out to about +5,175 people per year, or +14 a day. Births (12.5 per 1,000) and deaths (6.1 per 1,000) alone would give +0.65% a year; net migration adds about 455 people a year (+0.06%). Roughly one in every 10,312 people on Earth lives in Bhutan.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
