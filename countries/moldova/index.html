How Moldova's population is changing

Moldova's population is shrinking: −1.75% a year, which works out to about −41,414 people per year, or −113 a day. Births (10.6 per 1,000) and deaths (13.8 per 1,000) alone would give −0.32% a year; net migration removes about 29,766 people a year (−1.26%). Roughly one in every 3,480 people on Earth lives in Moldova.

Population of Moldova since 1960

In 1960, Moldova had 2,058,020 people. By 2025 that was 2,360,527 — 1.1× larger. The population peaked in 1991 at 2,981,566 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 18%.
