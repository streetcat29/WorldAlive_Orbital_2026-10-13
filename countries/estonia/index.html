How Estonia's population is changing

Estonia's population is shrinking: −0.43% a year, which works out to about −5,853 people per year, or −16 a day. Births (7.1 per 1,000) and deaths (11.5 per 1,000) alone would give −0.44% a year; net migration removes about 13,350 people a year (−0.98%). Roughly one in every 6,012 people on Earth lives in Estonia.

Population of Estonia since 1960

In 1960, Estonia had 1,211,537 people. By 2025 that was 1,366,475 — 1.1× larger. The population peaked in 1990 at 1,569,174 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1961 and 1971, when the population rose by 12%.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
