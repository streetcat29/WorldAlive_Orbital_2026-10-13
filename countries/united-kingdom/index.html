How United Kingdom's population is changing

United Kingdom's population is roughly stable: +0.30% a year, which works out to about +206,306 people per year, or +565 a day. Births (9.9 per 1,000) and deaths (9.7 per 1,000) alone would give +0.02% a year; net migration adds about 389,911 people a year (+0.56%). Roughly one in every 118 people on Earth lives in United Kingdom.

United Kingdom from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put United Kingdom at about 10,750,000 people in 1800; the UN estimates 69,551,334 in 2025 (6× 1800) and projects 74,305,411 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 2072 at 76,068,380 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
