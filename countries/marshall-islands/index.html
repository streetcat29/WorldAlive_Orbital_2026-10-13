Population of Marshall Islands since 1960

In 1960, Marshall Islands had 14,703 people. By 2025 that was 36,282 — 2.5× larger. The population peaked in 2010 at 52,044 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1961 and 1971, when the population rose by 54%.
