How Uruguay's population is changing

Uruguay's population is roughly stable: −0.06% a year, which works out to about −1,899 people per year, or −5 a day. Births (9.8 per 1,000) and deaths (9.9 per 1,000) alone would give −0.01% a year; net migration removes about 1,338 people a year (−0.04%). Roughly one in every 2,427 people on Earth lives in Uruguay.
