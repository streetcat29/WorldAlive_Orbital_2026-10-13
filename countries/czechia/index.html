How Czechia's population is changing

Czechia's population is roughly stable: −0.17% a year, which works out to about −18,135 people per year, or −50 a day. Births (7.7 per 1,000) and deaths (10.3 per 1,000) alone would give −0.26% a year; net migration removes about 112,188 people a year (−1.03%). Roughly one in every 755 people on Earth lives in Czechia.
