How Trinidad and Tobago's population is changing

Trinidad and Tobago's population is roughly stable: −0.04% a year, which works out to about −569 people per year, or −2 a day. Births (10.5 per 1,000) and deaths (8.7 per 1,000) alone would give +0.18% a year; net migration adds about 691 people a year (+0.05%). Roughly one in every 6,006 people on Earth lives in Trinidad and Tobago.

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement.
