How Slovenia's population is changing

Slovenia's population is roughly stable: +0.17% a year, which works out to about +3,589 people per year, or +10 a day. Births (7.9 per 1,000) and deaths (10.1 per 1,000) alone would give −0.22% a year; net migration adds about 2,711 people a year (+0.13%). Roughly one in every 3,855 people on Earth lives in Slovenia.

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement.
