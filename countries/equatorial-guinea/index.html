How Equatorial Guinea's population is changing

Equatorial Guinea's population is growing fast: +2.40% a year, which works out to about +46,468 people per year, or +127 a day. Births (30.0 per 1,000) and deaths (7.9 per 1,000) alone would give +2.21% a year; net migration adds about 3,812 people a year (+0.20%). Roughly one in every 4,238 people on Earth lives in Equatorial Guinea.
