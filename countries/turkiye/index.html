How Türkiye's population is changing

Türkiye's population is growing: +0.42% a year, which works out to about +360,651 people per year, or +987 a day. Births (11.0 per 1,000) and deaths (5.7 per 1,000) alone would give +0.53% a year; net migration removes about 258,205 people a year (−0.30%). Roughly one in every 96 people on Earth lives in Türkiye.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.

Read this country carefully

A growing population can coexist with low fertility

The bundled World Bank population series rises from 85,518,661 in 2024 to 85,878,556 in 2025. A fertility estimate of 1.48 children per woman refers to 2024, not a forecast of next year's population.

Growth also depends on the existing age structure, deaths and migration. The birth/death rates and migration observations on this page have different reference years, so they cannot be assembled into a precise single-year balance without aligned data.

To investigate a trend, compare consistent annual population observations first; use the other indicators as dated context. See the worked Türkiye example.
