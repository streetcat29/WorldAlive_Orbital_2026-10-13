How Papua New Guinea's population is changing

Papua New Guinea's population is growing fast: +1.75% a year, which works out to about +187,947 people per year, or +515 a day. Births (24.3 per 1,000) and deaths (6.6 per 1,000) alone would give +1.77% a year; net migration removes about 284 people a year (−0.00%). Roughly one in every 763 people on Earth lives in Papua New Guinea.
