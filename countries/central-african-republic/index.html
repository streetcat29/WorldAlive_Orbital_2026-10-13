How Central African Republic's population is changing

Central African Republic's population is growing fast: +3.37% a year, which works out to about +185,684 people per year, or +508 a day. Births (46.2 per 1,000) and deaths (9.4 per 1,000) alone would give +3.68% a year; net migration removes about 14,693 people a year (−0.27%). Roughly one in every 1,490 people on Earth lives in Central African Republic.

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement.
