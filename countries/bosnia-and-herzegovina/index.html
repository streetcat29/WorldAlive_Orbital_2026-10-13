How Bosnia and Herzegovina's population is changing

Bosnia and Herzegovina's population is shrinking: −0.77% a year, which works out to about −24,066 people per year, or −66 a day. Births (7.7 per 1,000) and deaths (13.5 per 1,000) alone would give −0.58% a year; net migration removes about 6,994 people a year (−0.22%). Roughly one in every 2,616 people on Earth lives in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Population of Bosnia and Herzegovina since 1960

In 1960, Bosnia and Herzegovina had 3,281,381 people. By 2025 that was 3,140,095 — almost unchanged. The population peaked in 1991 at 4,458,343 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 15%.
