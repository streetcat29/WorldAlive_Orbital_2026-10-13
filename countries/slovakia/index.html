How Slovakia's population is changing

Slovakia's population is roughly stable: −0.15% a year, which works out to about −8,250 people per year, or −23 a day. Births (8.5 per 1,000) and deaths (9.9 per 1,000) alone would give −0.14% a year; net migration removes about 31,377 people a year (−0.58%). Roughly one in every 1,517 people on Earth lives in Slovakia.

Population of Slovakia since 1960

In 1960, Slovakia had 4,068,095 people. By 2025 that was 5,413,813 — 1.3× larger. The population peaked in 2020 at 5,458,827 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 12%.
