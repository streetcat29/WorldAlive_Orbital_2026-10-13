How Comoros's population is changing

Comoros's population is growing fast: +1.85% a year, which works out to about +16,370 people per year, or +45 a day. Births (28.2 per 1,000) and deaths (7.1 per 1,000) alone would give +2.11% a year; net migration removes about 2,127 people a year (−0.24%). Roughly one in every 9,306 people on Earth lives in Comoros.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
