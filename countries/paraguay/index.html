How Paraguay's population is changing

Paraguay's population is growing: +1.20% a year, which works out to about +84,431 people per year, or +231 a day. Births (19.7 per 1,000) and deaths (5.7 per 1,000) alone would give +1.40% a year; net migration removes about 12,423 people a year (−0.18%). Roughly one in every 1,171 people on Earth lives in Paraguay.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement.
