How Cyprus's population is changing

Cyprus's population is growing: +0.91% a year, which works out to about +12,529 people per year, or +34 a day. Births (10.5 per 1,000) and deaths (7.0 per 1,000) alone would give +0.35% a year; net migration adds about 7,749 people a year (+0.57%). Roughly one in every 5,993 people on Earth lives in Cyprus.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
