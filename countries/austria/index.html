How Austria's population is changing

Austria's population is growing: +0.33% a year, which works out to about +30,231 people per year, or +83 a day. Births (8.4 per 1,000) and deaths (9.6 per 1,000) alone would give −0.12% a year; net migration adds about 11,254 people a year (+0.12%). Roughly one in every 892 people on Earth lives in Austria.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
