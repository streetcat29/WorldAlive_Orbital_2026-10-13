How Mauritius's population is changing

Mauritius's population is roughly stable: −0.16% a year, which works out to about −2,036 people per year, or −6 a day. Births (10.3 per 1,000) and deaths (10.0 per 1,000) alone would give +0.03% a year; net migration removes about 2,784 people a year (−0.22%). Roughly one in every 6,605 people on Earth lives in Mauritius.

Population of Mauritius since 1960

In 1960, Mauritius had 680,331 people. By 2025 that was 1,243,741 — 1.8× larger. The population peaked in 2021 at 1,266,334 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1961 and 1971, when the population rose by 23%.

Mauritius from 1800 to 2100

Modelled estimates put Mauritius at about 59,000 people in 1800; the UN estimates 1,268,285 in 2025 (21× 1800) and projects 581,719 for 2100 in its medium scenario. The population is projected to peak around 2015 at 1,292,283 and decline afterwards.

About these numbers

Population, growth, birth and death rates, migration, density, age structure, life expectancy, fertility and GDP per capita come from the World Bank Indicators API (latest available year shown beside each figure). The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
