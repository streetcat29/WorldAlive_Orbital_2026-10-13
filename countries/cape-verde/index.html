How Cape Verde's population is changing

Cape Verde's population is growing: +0.47% a year, which works out to about +2,455 people per year, or +7 a day. Births (12.2 per 1,000) and deaths (5.1 per 1,000) alone would give +0.71% a year; net migration removes about 1,284 people a year (−0.24%). Roughly one in every 15,579 people on Earth lives in Cape Verde.
