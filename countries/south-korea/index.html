How South Korea's population is changing

South Korea's population is roughly stable: −0.13% a year, which works out to about −66,458 people per year, or −182 a day. Births (4.7 per 1,000) and deaths (7.0 per 1,000) alone would give −0.23% a year; net migration adds about 65,731 people a year (+0.13%). Roughly one in every 159 people on Earth lives in South Korea.

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement.
