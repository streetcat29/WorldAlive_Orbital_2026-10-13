How Saudi Arabia's population is changing

Saudi Arabia's population is growing fast: +4.63% a year, which works out to about +1,712,320 people per year, or +4,688 a day. Births (16.5 per 1,000) and deaths (2.3 per 1,000) alone would give +1.42% a year; net migration adds about 119,738 people a year (+0.32%). Roughly one in every 222 people on Earth lives in Saudi Arabia.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
