How Suriname's population is changing

Suriname's population is growing: +0.85% a year, which works out to about +5,442 people per year, or +15 a day. Births (17.2 per 1,000) and deaths (6.8 per 1,000) alone would give +1.03% a year; net migration removes about 967 people a year (−0.15%). Roughly one in every 12,840 people on Earth lives in Suriname.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement.
