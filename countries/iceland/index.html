How Iceland's population is changing

Iceland's population is growing fast: +1.51% a year, which works out to about +5,943 people per year, or +16 a day. Births (11.2 per 1,000) and deaths (6.8 per 1,000) alone would give +0.44% a year; net migration adds about 2,709 people a year (+0.69%). Roughly one in every 20,936 people on Earth lives in Iceland.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
