How Armenia's population is changing

Armenia's population is growing fast: +1.74% a year, which works out to about +53,664 people per year, or +147 a day. Births (11.1 per 1,000) and deaths (8.4 per 1,000) alone would give +0.27% a year; net migration removes about 29,013 people a year (−0.94%). Roughly one in every 2,662 people on Earth lives in Armenia.

Population of Armenia since 1960

In 1960, Armenia had 1,863,705 people. By 2025 that was 3,086,700 — 1.7× larger. The population peaked in 1991 at 3,613,977 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 1960 and 1970, when the population rose by 33%.
