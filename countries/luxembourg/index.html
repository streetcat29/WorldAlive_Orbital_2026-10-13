How Luxembourg's population is changing

Luxembourg's population is growing: +1.46% a year, which works out to about +10,031 people per year, or +27 a day. Births (9.5 per 1,000) and deaths (6.6 per 1,000) alone would give +0.29% a year; net migration adds about 5,237 people a year (+0.76%). Roughly one in every 11,959 people on Earth lives in Luxembourg.
