How Turkmenistan's population is changing

Turkmenistan's population is growing fast: +1.65% a year, which works out to about +125,375 people per year, or +343 a day. Births (20.9 per 1,000) and deaths (5.8 per 1,000) alone would give +1.51% a year; net migration adds about 13,268 people a year (+0.17%). Roughly one in every 1,078 people on Earth lives in Turkmenistan.
