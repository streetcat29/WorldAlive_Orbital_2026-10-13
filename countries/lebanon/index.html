How Lebanon's population is changing

Lebanon's population is growing: +0.75% a year, which works out to about +43,621 people per year, or +119 a day. Births (16.0 per 1,000) and deaths (6.0 per 1,000) alone would give +0.99% a year; net migration removes about 10,230 people a year (−0.17%). Roughly one in every 1,404 people on Earth lives in Lebanon.

Population of Lebanon since 1960

In 1960, Lebanon had 1,794,406 people. By 2025 that was 5,849,421 — 3.3× larger. The population peaked in 2015 at 6,472,469 and has declined since. The fastest growth came between 2005 and 2015, when the population rose by 39%.

About these numbers

The live counter applies the annual growth rate continuously from 1 July 2025 — a model, not a measurement. City populations are the latest census or official estimate held by GeoNames and are not projected. Sub-city units are folded into their parent city.
