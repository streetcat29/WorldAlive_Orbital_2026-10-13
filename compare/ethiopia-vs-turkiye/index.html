Ethiopia vs Türkiye

Ethiopia has 1.6× the population of Türkiye: 135,472,051 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 49.6 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 21.0%). People in Türkiye live 9.8 years longer on average (77.4 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 19.9× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
