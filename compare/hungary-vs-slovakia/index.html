Hungary has 1.8× the population of Slovakia: 9,514,251 vs 5,413,813, a gap of 4.1 million. Slovakia is shrinking more slowly (−0.15% vs −0.50% a year), adding about -8,250 people a year against −47,694. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Slovakia would overtake Hungary in about 162 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2187) — a projection, not a forecast. Hungary is the older society: 21.2% aged 65+ against 19.0% in Slovakia, while Slovakia has more children (15.6% under 15 vs 14.4%). People in Slovakia live 1.6 years longer on average (78.4 vs 76.7).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Hungary) and 2025 (Slovakia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
