Canada has 7% more people than Angola: 41,651,653 vs 39,040,039, a gap of 2.6 million. Angola is growing faster (+3.00% vs +0.94% a year), adding about 1,171,595 people a year against +391,155. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Angola would overtake Canada in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Canada is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 2.9% in Angola, while Angola has more children (44.1% under 15 vs 15.0%). Angola is 6.5× as densely populated (29.5 vs 4.6 people per km²). People in Canada live 17.3 years longer on average (82.1 vs 64.8). GDP per capita is 17.8× higher in Canada ($55,698 vs $3,129).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Angola) and 2025 (Canada); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
