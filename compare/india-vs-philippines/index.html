India has 12.5× the population of Philippines: 1,463,865,525 vs 116,786,962, a gap of 1.35 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.81% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +947,122. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 24.2%). People in India live 2.3 years longer on average (72.2 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Philippines ($4,171 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Philippines); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
