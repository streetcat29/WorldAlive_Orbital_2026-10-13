Brazil has 3.1× the population of United Kingdom: 212,812,405 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 143.3 million. Brazil is growing faster (+0.38% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 815,392 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 11.5% in Brazil, while Brazil has more children (19.4% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 11.2× as densely populated (283.2 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 5.4 years longer on average (81.4 vs 76.0). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $10,713).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
