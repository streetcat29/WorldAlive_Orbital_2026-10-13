Guatemala has 3% more people than Netherlands: 18,687,881 vs 18,087,633, a gap of 600,248. Guatemala is growing faster (+1.52% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 283,664 people a year against +94,394. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Netherlands is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Guatemala, while Guatemala has more children (31.0% under 15 vs 14.9%). Netherlands is 3.1× as densely populated (531.0 vs 169.1 people per km²). People in Netherlands live 9.2 years longer on average (82.0 vs 72.7). GDP per capita is 11.2× higher in Netherlands ($73,684 vs $6,598).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Guatemala) and 2025 (Netherlands); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
