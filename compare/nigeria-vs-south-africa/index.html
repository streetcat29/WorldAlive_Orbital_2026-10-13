Nigeria vs South Africa

Nigeria has 3.7× the population of South Africa: 237,527,782 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 172.8 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +1.15% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +744,395. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Africa is the older society: 6.9% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 25.7%). Nigeria is 4.8× as densely populated (250.2 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in South Africa live 11.7 years longer on average (66.3 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nigeria) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
