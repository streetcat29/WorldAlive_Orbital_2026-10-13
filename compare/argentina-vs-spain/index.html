Spain has 8% more people than Argentina: 49,355,143 vs 45,851,378, a gap of 3.5 million. Spain is growing faster (+1.03% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 508,918 people a year against +155,482. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Spain is the older society: 21.6% aged 65+ against 12.6% in Argentina, while Argentina has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 12.6%). Spain is 5.8× as densely populated (96.8 vs 16.6 people per km²). People in Spain live 6.3 years longer on average (83.9 vs 77.5). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Spain ($38,627 vs $14,898).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Argentina) and 2025 (Spain); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
