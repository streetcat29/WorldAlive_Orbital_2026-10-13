China has 26.3× the population of Colombia: 1,406,585,000 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 1.35 billion. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 3.2× as densely populated (150.3 vs 47.2 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in China ($13,862 vs $8,562).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Colombia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
