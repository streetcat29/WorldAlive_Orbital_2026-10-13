Ukraine has 5% more people than Saudi Arabia: 38,980,376 vs 36,973,555, a gap of 2.0 million. Saudi Arabia is growing faster (+4.63% vs +2.92% a year), adding about 1,712,320 people a year against +1,136,565. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Saudi Arabia would overtake Ukraine in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Ukraine is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Saudi Arabia, while Saudi Arabia has more children (23.6% under 15 vs 13.7%). Ukraine is 4.2× as densely populated (65.1 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Saudi Arabia live 4.3 years longer on average (79.0 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 5.9× higher in Saudi Arabia ($34,537 vs $5,866).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Saudi Arabia) and 2025 (Ukraine); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
