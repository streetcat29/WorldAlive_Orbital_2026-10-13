Nigeria has 1.8× the population of Ethiopia: 237,527,782 vs 135,472,051, a gap of 102.1 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +2.06% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +4,898,468. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ethiopia would overtake Nigeria in about 115 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2140) — a projection, not a forecast. Nigeria is 2.2× as densely populated (250.2 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in Ethiopia live 13.0 years longer on average (67.6 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Nigeria ($1,224 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
