Bulgaria has 5% more people than Singapore: 6,433,302 vs 6,111,175, a gap of 322,127. Singapore is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 74,771 people a year against −8,114. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Singapore would overtake Bulgaria in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Bulgaria is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 14.2% in Singapore. Singapore is 138.8× as densely populated (8,241.8 vs 59.4 people per km²). People in Singapore live 7.6 years longer on average (83.3 vs 75.8). GDP per capita is 4.9× higher in Singapore ($98,814 vs $20,328).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bulgaria) and 2025 (Singapore); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
