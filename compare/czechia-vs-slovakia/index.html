Czechia has 2.0× the population of Slovakia: 10,886,878 vs 5,413,813, a gap of 5.5 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (−0.17% vs −0.15% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Czechia is the older society: 21.2% aged 65+ against 19.0% in Slovakia, while Slovakia has more children (15.6% under 15 vs 15.1%). People in Czechia live 1.6 years longer on average (80.0 vs 78.4). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Czechia ($35,917 vs $28,544).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Czechia) and 2025 (Slovakia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
