Madagascar has about the same population as Ivory Coast: 32,740,678 vs 32,711,547, a gap of 29,131. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+2.40% vs +2.40% a year). Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ivory Coast would overtake Madagascar in about 12 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2037) — a projection, not a forecast. Ivory Coast is 1.8× as densely populated (98.0 vs 53.6 people per km²). People in Madagascar live 1.7 years longer on average (63.8 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 5.1× higher in Ivory Coast ($3,050 vs $599).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ivory Coast) and 2025 (Madagascar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
