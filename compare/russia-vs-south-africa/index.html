Russia has 2.2× the population of South Africa: 143,513,328 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 78.8 million. South Africa is growing faster (+1.15% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 744,395 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, South Africa would overtake Russia in about 63 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2088) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 6.9% in South Africa, while South Africa has more children (25.7% under 15 vs 17.0%). South Africa is 5.9× as densely populated (52.1 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 7.1 years longer on average (73.4 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $6,598).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Russia) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
