Czechia has about the same population as Portugal: 10,886,878 vs 10,804,871, a gap of 82,007. Portugal is growing faster (+1.03% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 110,756 people a year against −18,135. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Portugal would overtake Czechia in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast.
