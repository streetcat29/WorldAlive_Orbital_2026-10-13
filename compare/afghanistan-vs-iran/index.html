Iran has 2.1× the population of Afghanistan: 92,417,681 vs 43,844,111, a gap of 48.6 million. Afghanistan is growing faster (+2.77% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 1,213,252 people a year against +853,875. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Afghanistan would overtake Iran in about 40 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2065) — a projection, not a forecast. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 2.4% in Afghanistan, while Afghanistan has more children (42.6% under 15 vs 22.0%). People in Iran live 11.6 years longer on average (77.9 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 9.4× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $417).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Afghanistan) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
