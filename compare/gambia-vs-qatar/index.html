Qatar has 5% more people than Gambia: 2,972,215 vs 2,822,093, a gap of 150,122. Qatar is growing faster (+3.92% vs +2.23% a year), adding about 116,653 people a year against +62,799. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Gambia is the older society: 3.2% aged 65+ against 1.7% in Qatar. People in Qatar live 16.5 years longer on average (82.5 vs 66.1). GDP per capita is 78.9× higher in Qatar ($72,525 vs $919).
