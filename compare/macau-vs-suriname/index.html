Macau has 7% more people than Suriname: 685,900 vs 639,850, a gap of 46,050. Suriname is growing faster (+0.85% vs −0.16% a year), adding about 5,442 people a year against −1,099. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Suriname would overtake Macau in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Macau is the older society: 15.1% aged 65+ against 8.2% in Suriname, while Suriname has more children (25.3% under 15 vs 13.7%). Macau is 5249.9× as densely populated (20,569.7 vs 3.9 people per km²). People in Macau live 9.6 years longer on average (83.3 vs 73.8). GDP per capita is 10.7× higher in Macau ($75,902 vs $7,070).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Macau) and 2025 (Suriname); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
