Venezuela has 3% more people than Australia: 28,516,896 vs 27,614,411, a gap of 902,485. Australia is growing faster (+1.53% vs +0.39% a year), adding about 423,354 people a year against +111,571. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Australia would overtake Venezuela in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Australia is the older society: 18.1% aged 65+ against 10.0% in Venezuela, while Venezuela has more children (24.9% under 15 vs 17.7%). Venezuela is 9.3× as densely populated (32.1 vs 3.5 people per km²). People in Australia live 10.4 years longer on average (83.1 vs 72.7). GDP per capita is 18.6× higher in Australia ($65,130 vs $3,495).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Australia) and 2025 (Venezuela); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
