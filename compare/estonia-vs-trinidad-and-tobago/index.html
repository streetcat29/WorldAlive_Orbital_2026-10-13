Trinidad and Tobago has about the same population as Estonia: 1,367,764 vs 1,366,475, a gap of 1,289. Trinidad and Tobago is shrinking more slowly (−0.04% vs −0.43% a year), adding about -569 people a year against −5,853. Estonia is the older society: 21.8% aged 65+ against 12.9% in Trinidad and Tobago, while Trinidad and Tobago has more children (17.5% under 15 vs 15.3%). Trinidad and Tobago is 8.3× as densely populated (266.6 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Estonia live 5.7 years longer on average (79.3 vs 73.6). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in Estonia ($34,418 vs $18,967).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Estonia) and 2025 (Trinidad and Tobago); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
