China has 27.2× the population of Sudan: 1,406,585,000 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 1.35 billion. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake China in about 130 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2155) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 5.6× as densely populated (150.3 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in China live 11.5 years longer on average (78.0 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 11.9× higher in China ($13,862 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
