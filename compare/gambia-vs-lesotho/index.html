Gambia has 1.2× the population of Lesotho: 2,822,093 vs 2,363,325, a gap of 458,768. Gambia is growing faster (+2.23% vs +1.10% a year), adding about 62,799 people a year against +26,045. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Gambia is 3.5× as densely populated (266.6 vs 76.1 people per km²). People in Gambia live 8.3 years longer on average (66.1 vs 57.8).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Gambia) and 2025 (Lesotho); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
