Albania has 1.2× the population of Equatorial Guinea: 2,349,580 vs 1,938,431, a gap of 411,149. Equatorial Guinea is growing faster (+2.40% vs −1.17% a year), adding about 46,468 people a year against −27,388. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Equatorial Guinea would overtake Albania in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Albania is the older society: 17.6% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Equatorial Guinea, while Equatorial Guinea has more children (37.1% under 15 vs 16.6%). Albania is 1.3× as densely populated (88.0 vs 65.9 people per km²). People in Albania live 15.9 years longer on average (79.8 vs 63.9). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Albania ($12,998 vs $6,615).
