Belgium has about the same population as Haiti: 11,941,781 vs 11,906,095, a gap of 35,686. Haiti is growing faster (+1.13% vs +0.70% a year), adding about 134,293 people a year against +83,462. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Haiti would overtake Belgium in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Belgium is the older society: 21.0% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Haiti, while Haiti has more children (30.8% under 15 vs 15.7%). People in Belgium live 17.2 years longer on average (82.3 vs 65.1). GDP per capita is 22.5× higher in Belgium ($60,750 vs $2,694).
