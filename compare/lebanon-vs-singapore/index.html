Singapore has 4% more people than Lebanon: 6,111,175 vs 5,849,421, a gap of 261,754. Singapore is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.75% a year), adding about 74,771 people a year against +43,621. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Singapore is the older society: 14.2% aged 65+ against 10.4% in Lebanon, while Lebanon has more children (25.6% under 15 vs 11.7%). Singapore is 14.6× as densely populated (8,241.8 vs 564.4 people per km²). People in Singapore live 5.4 years longer on average (83.3 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 22.1× higher in Singapore ($98,814 vs $4,473).
