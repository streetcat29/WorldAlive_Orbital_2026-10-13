Chile has 4% more people than Romania: 19,859,921 vs 19,020,271, a gap of 839,650. Chile is growing faster (+0.48% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 95,379 people a year against −31,507. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Romania is the older society: 20.1% aged 65+ against 14.6% in Chile, while Chile has more children (16.5% under 15 vs 15.6%). Romania is 3.1× as densely populated (82.8 vs 26.5 people per km²). People in Chile live 4.9 years longer on average (81.4 vs 76.5). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Romania ($22,538 vs $17,995).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chile) and 2025 (Romania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
