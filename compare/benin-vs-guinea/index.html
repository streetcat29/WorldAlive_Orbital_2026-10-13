Guinea has about the same population as Benin: 15,099,727 vs 14,814,460, a gap of 285,267. Benin is growing faster (+2.40% vs +2.31% a year), adding about 355,979 people a year against +348,943. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Benin would overtake Guinea in about 21 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2046) — a projection, not a forecast. Benin is 2.1× as densely populated (125.1 vs 58.6 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Benin) and 2025 (Guinea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
