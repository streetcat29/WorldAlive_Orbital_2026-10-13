Greece has 9% more people than Hungary: 10,413,962 vs 9,514,251, a gap of 899,711. Greece is growing faster (+0.08% vs −0.50% a year), adding about 8,832 people a year against −47,694. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Greece is the older society: 24.4% aged 65+ against 21.2% in Hungary, while Hungary has more children (14.4% under 15 vs 12.9%). Hungary is 1.3× as densely populated (105.1 vs 80.7 people per km²). People in Greece live 5.1 years longer on average (81.8 vs 76.7).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Greece) and 2025 (Hungary); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
