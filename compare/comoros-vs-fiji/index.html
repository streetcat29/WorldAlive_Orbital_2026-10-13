Fiji has 6% more people than Comoros: 933,154 vs 882,847, a gap of 50,307. Comoros is growing faster (+1.85% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 16,370 people a year against +4,380. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Comoros would overtake Fiji in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Fiji is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 4.5% in Comoros, while Comoros has more children (36.9% under 15 vs 26.8%). Comoros is 9.0× as densely populated (457.0 vs 50.6 people per km²). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Fiji ($6,642 vs $2,056).
