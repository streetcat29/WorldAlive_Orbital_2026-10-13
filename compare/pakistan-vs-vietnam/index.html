Pakistan has 2.5× the population of Vietnam: 255,219,554 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 153.6 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +612,685. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 22.9%). People in Vietnam live 6.9 years longer on average (74.7 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Pakistan) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
