Pakistan has 3.7× the population of France: 255,219,554 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 186.5 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 16.2%). Pakistan is 2.5× as densely populated (321.1 vs 126.9 people per km²). People in France live 15.2 years longer on average (83.0 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 30.7× higher in France ($48,986 vs $1,596).
