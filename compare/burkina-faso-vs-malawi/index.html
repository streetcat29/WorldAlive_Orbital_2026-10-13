Burkina Faso has 8% more people than Malawi: 24,074,580 vs 22,216,120, a gap of 1.9 million. Malawi is growing faster (+2.56% vs +2.21% a year), adding about 568,034 people a year against +531,626. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Malawi would overtake Burkina Faso in about 23 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2048) — a projection, not a forecast. Malawi is 2.7× as densely populated (223.8 vs 84.2 people per km²). People in Malawi live 6.3 years longer on average (67.6 vs 61.3). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Burkina Faso ($1,148 vs $672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Burkina Faso) and 2025 (Malawi); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
