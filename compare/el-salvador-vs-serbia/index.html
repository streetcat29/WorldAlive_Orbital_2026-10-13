Serbia has about the same population as El Salvador: 6,549,143 vs 6,365,503, a gap of 183,640. El Salvador is growing faster (+0.43% vs −0.57% a year), adding about 27,369 people a year against −37,227. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, El Salvador would overtake Serbia in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Serbia is the older society: 23.1% aged 65+ against 8.3% in El Salvador, while El Salvador has more children (24.4% under 15 vs 14.3%). El Salvador is 3.9× as densely populated (304.5 vs 78.8 people per km²). People in Serbia live 3.7 years longer on average (76.0 vs 72.3). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Serbia ($15,262 vs $5,767).
