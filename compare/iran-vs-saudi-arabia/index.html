Iran has 2.5× the population of Saudi Arabia: 92,417,681 vs 36,973,555, a gap of 55.4 million. Saudi Arabia is growing faster (+4.63% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 1,712,320 people a year against +853,875. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Saudi Arabia would overtake Iran in about 25 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2050) — a projection, not a forecast. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Saudi Arabia, while Saudi Arabia has more children (23.6% under 15 vs 22.0%). Iran is 3.6× as densely populated (55.8 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Saudi Arabia live 1.1 years longer on average (79.0 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 8.8× higher in Saudi Arabia ($34,537 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Saudi Arabia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
