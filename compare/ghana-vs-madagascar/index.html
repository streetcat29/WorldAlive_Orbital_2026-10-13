Ghana has 7% more people than Madagascar: 35,064,272 vs 32,740,678, a gap of 2.3 million. Madagascar is growing faster (+2.40% vs +1.83% a year), adding about 785,059 people a year against +642,712. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Madagascar would overtake Ghana in about 12 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2037) — a projection, not a forecast. Ghana is 2.8× as densely populated (148.5 vs 53.6 people per km²). People in Ghana live 1.9 years longer on average (65.7 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in Ghana ($3,257 vs $599).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ghana) and 2025 (Madagascar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
