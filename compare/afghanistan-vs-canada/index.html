Afghanistan has 5% more people than Canada: 43,844,111 vs 41,651,653, a gap of 2.2 million. Afghanistan is growing faster (+2.77% vs +0.94% a year), adding about 1,213,252 people a year against +391,155. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Canada is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 2.4% in Afghanistan, while Afghanistan has more children (42.6% under 15 vs 15.0%). Afghanistan is 13.9× as densely populated (63.6 vs 4.6 people per km²). People in Canada live 15.8 years longer on average (82.1 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 133.6× higher in Canada ($55,698 vs $417).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Afghanistan) and 2025 (Canada); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
