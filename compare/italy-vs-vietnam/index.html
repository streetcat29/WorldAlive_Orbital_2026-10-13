Vietnam has 1.7× the population of Italy: 101,598,527 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 42.7 million. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 11.7%). Vietnam is 1.6× as densely populated (320.2 vs 199.5 people per km²). People in Italy live 9.2 years longer on average (84.0 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 8.5× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $5,066).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
