Ecuador has about the same population as Cambodia: 18,289,896 vs 17,847,982, a gap of 441,914. Cambodia is growing faster (+1.18% vs +0.85% a year), adding about 210,416 people a year against +155,074. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cambodia would overtake Ecuador in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Ecuador is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 6.4% in Cambodia, while Cambodia has more children (29.5% under 15 vs 23.9%). Cambodia is 1.4× as densely populated (98.7 vs 72.4 people per km²). People in Ecuador live 6.8 years longer on average (77.6 vs 70.8). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Ecuador ($7,125 vs $2,872).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cambodia) and 2025 (Ecuador); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
