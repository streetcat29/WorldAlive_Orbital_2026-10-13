San Marino has 2.8× the population of Nauru: 34,109 vs 12,025, a gap of 22,084. Nauru is growing faster (+0.65% vs +0.39% a year), adding about 78 people a year against +132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. San Marino is the older society: 23.1% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Nauru, while Nauru has more children (37.2% under 15 vs 11.9%). People in San Marino live 23.5 years longer on average (85.8 vs 62.3). GDP per capita is 4.1× higher in San Marino ($59,871 vs $14,640).
