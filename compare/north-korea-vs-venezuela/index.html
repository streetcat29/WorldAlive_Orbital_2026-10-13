Venezuela has 7% more people than North Korea: 28,516,896 vs 26,571,036, a gap of 1.9 million. Venezuela is growing faster (+0.39% vs +0.27% a year), adding about 111,571 people a year against +72,311. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. North Korea is the older society: 12.7% aged 65+ against 10.0% in Venezuela, while Venezuela has more children (24.9% under 15 vs 18.9%). North Korea is 6.8× as densely populated (219.4 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in North Korea live 1.1 years longer on average (73.7 vs 72.7).
