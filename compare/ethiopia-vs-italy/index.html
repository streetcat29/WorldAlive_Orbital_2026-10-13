Ethiopia has 2.3× the population of Italy: 135,472,051 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 76.6 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 1.7× as densely populated (199.5 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in Italy live 16.3 years longer on average (84.0 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 46.4× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Italy); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
