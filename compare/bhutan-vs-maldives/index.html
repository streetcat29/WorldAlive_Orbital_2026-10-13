Bhutan has 1.5× the population of Maldives: 796,682 vs 529,676, a gap of 267,006. Bhutan is growing faster (+0.65% vs +0.35% a year), adding about 5,175 people a year against +1,880. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bhutan is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Maldives. Maldives is 85.6× as densely populated (1,765.1 vs 20.6 people per km²). People in Maldives live 8.0 years longer on average (81.3 vs 73.3). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Maldives ($14,615 vs $4,493).
