Russia has 2.7× the population of Colombia: 143,513,328 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 90.1 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake Russia in about 88 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2113) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 17.0%). Colombia is 5.4× as densely populated (47.2 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Colombia live 4.5 years longer on average (77.9 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $8,562).
