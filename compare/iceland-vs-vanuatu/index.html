Iceland has 1.2× the population of Vanuatu: 392,404 vs 335,169, a gap of 57,235. Vanuatu is growing faster (+2.23% vs +1.51% a year), adding about 7,475 people a year against +5,943. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Vanuatu would overtake Iceland in about 22 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2047) — a projection, not a forecast. Iceland is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Vanuatu, while Vanuatu has more children (38.0% under 15 vs 17.5%). Vanuatu is 6.9× as densely populated (26.3 vs 3.8 people per km²). People in Iceland live 11.2 years longer on average (82.8 vs 71.7). GDP per capita is 24.3× higher in Iceland ($98,323 vs $4,039).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iceland) and 2025 (Vanuatu); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
