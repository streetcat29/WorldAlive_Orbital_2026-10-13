DR Congo has 1.2× the population of Iran: 112,832,473 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 20.4 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +853,875. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 22.0%). People in Iran live 15.8 years longer on average (77.9 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 4.9× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
