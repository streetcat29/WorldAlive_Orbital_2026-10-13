Aruba has 5% more people than Tonga: 108,785 vs 103,742, a gap of 5,043. Aruba is growing faster (+0.73% vs −0.42% a year), adding about 793 people a year against −432. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Aruba is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 6.9% in Tonga, while Tonga has more children (34.9% under 15 vs 16.5%). Aruba is 4.1× as densely populated (596.4 vs 145.3 people per km²). People in Aruba live 3.4 years longer on average (76.5 vs 73.1). GDP per capita is 5.9× higher in Aruba ($38,591 vs $6,547).
