Timor-Leste has 4% more people than Trinidad and Tobago: 1,418,517 vs 1,367,764, a gap of 50,753. Timor-Leste is growing faster (+1.27% vs −0.04% a year), adding about 17,993 people a year against −569. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Trinidad and Tobago is the older society: 12.9% aged 65+ against 5.2% in Timor-Leste, while Timor-Leste has more children (33.1% under 15 vs 17.5%). Trinidad and Tobago is 2.9× as densely populated (266.6 vs 93.1 people per km²). People in Trinidad and Tobago live 5.7 years longer on average (73.6 vs 67.9). GDP per capita is 14.1× higher in Trinidad and Tobago ($18,967 vs $1,341).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Timor-Leste) and 2025 (Trinidad and Tobago); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
