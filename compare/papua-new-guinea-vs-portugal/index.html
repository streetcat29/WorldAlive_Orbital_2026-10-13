Portugal has about the same population as Papua New Guinea: 10,804,871 vs 10,762,817, a gap of 42,054. Papua New Guinea is growing faster (+1.75% vs +1.03% a year), adding about 187,947 people a year against +110,756. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Papua New Guinea would overtake Portugal in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Portugal is the older society: 24.9% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Papua New Guinea, while Papua New Guinea has more children (33.1% under 15 vs 12.7%). Portugal is 5.0× as densely populated (115.5 vs 22.9 people per km²). People in Portugal live 16.1 years longer on average (82.4 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 10.6× higher in Portugal ($32,082 vs $3,020).
