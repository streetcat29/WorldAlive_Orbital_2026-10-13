Zimbabwe has 14% more people than Benin: 16,950,795 vs 14,814,460, a gap of 2.1 million. Benin is growing faster (+2.40% vs +1.88% a year), adding about 355,979 people a year against +319,413. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Benin would overtake Zimbabwe in about 26 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2051) — a projection, not a forecast. Benin is 3.0× as densely populated (125.1 vs 42.2 people per km²). People in Zimbabwe live 2.1 years longer on average (63.1 vs 61.0). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in Zimbabwe ($3,021 vs $1,658).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Benin) and 2025 (Zimbabwe); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
