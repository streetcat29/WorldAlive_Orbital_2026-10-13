Pakistan has 2.3× the population of DR Congo: 255,219,554 vs 112,832,473, a gap of 142.4 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +3,981,282. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, DR Congo would overtake Pakistan in about 50 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2075) — a projection, not a forecast. Pakistan is the older society: 4.4% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 36.2%). Pakistan is 6.9× as densely populated (321.1 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Pakistan live 5.7 years longer on average (67.8 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Pakistan ($1,596 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
