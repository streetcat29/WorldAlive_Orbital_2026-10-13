Pakistan has 1.9× the population of Mexico: 255,219,554 vs 131,946,900, a gap of 123.3 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +1,090,386. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 24.1%). Pakistan is 4.8× as densely populated (321.1 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Mexico live 7.5 years longer on average (75.3 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 8.7× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
