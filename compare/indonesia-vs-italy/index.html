Indonesia has 4.8× the population of Italy: 285,721,236 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 226.8 million. Indonesia is growing faster (+0.78% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 2,242,079 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Indonesia, while Indonesia has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 1.3× as densely populated (199.5 vs 148.6 people per km²). People in Italy live 12.7 years longer on average (84.0 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 8.6× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $5,060).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Italy); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
