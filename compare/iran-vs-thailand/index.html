Iran has 1.3× the population of Thailand: 92,417,681 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 20.8 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 2.5× as densely populated (140.3 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 1.3 years longer on average (77.9 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
