Equatorial Guinea has 6% more people than North Macedonia: 1,938,431 vs 1,820,909, a gap of 117,522. Equatorial Guinea is growing faster (+2.40% vs −0.19% a year), adding about 46,468 people a year against −3,447. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. North Macedonia is the older society: 18.5% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Equatorial Guinea, while Equatorial Guinea has more children (37.1% under 15 vs 16.5%). People in North Macedonia live 12.7 years longer on average (76.6 vs 63.9). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in North Macedonia ($10,490 vs $6,615).
