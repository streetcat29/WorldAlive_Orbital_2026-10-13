Russia has 2.8× the population of South Korea: 143,513,328 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 91.8 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (−0.11% vs −0.13% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 17.8% in Russia, while Russia has more children (17.0% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 60.3× as densely populated (529.8 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in South Korea live 10.2 years longer on average (83.6 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $17,547).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Russia) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
