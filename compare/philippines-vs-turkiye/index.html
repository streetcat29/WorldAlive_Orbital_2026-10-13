Philippines has 1.4× the population of Türkiye: 116,786,962 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 30.9 million. Philippines is growing faster (+0.81% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 947,122 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 21.0%). Philippines is 3.5× as densely populated (385.3 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 7.5 years longer on average (77.4 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 4.5× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $4,171).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Philippines) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
