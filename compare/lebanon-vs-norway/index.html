Lebanon has 4% more people than Norway: 5,849,421 vs 5,610,870, a gap of 238,551. Lebanon is growing faster (+0.75% vs +0.69% a year), adding about 43,621 people a year against +38,724. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Norway is the older society: 19.1% aged 65+ against 10.4% in Lebanon, while Lebanon has more children (25.6% under 15 vs 15.9%). Lebanon is 37.2× as densely populated (564.4 vs 15.2 people per km²). People in Norway live 5.2 years longer on average (83.2 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 21.1× higher in Norway ($94,594 vs $4,473).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Lebanon) and 2025 (Norway); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
