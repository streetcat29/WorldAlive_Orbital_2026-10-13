Mexico has 1.9× the population of France: 131,946,900 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 63.2 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 16.2%). France is 1.9× as densely populated (126.9 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in France live 7.7 years longer on average (83.0 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in France ($48,986 vs $13,889).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (France) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
