Armenia has 9% more people than Jamaica: 3,086,700 vs 2,837,077, a gap of 249,623. Armenia is growing faster (+1.74% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 53,664 people a year against −2,097. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Armenia is the older society: 14.4% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Jamaica. Jamaica is 2.5× as densely populated (262.2 vs 105.1 people per km²). People in Armenia live 6.7 years longer on average (78.3 vs 71.6).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Armenia) and 2025 (Jamaica); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
