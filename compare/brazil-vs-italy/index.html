Brazil has 3.6× the population of Italy: 212,812,405 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 153.9 million. Brazil is growing faster (+0.38% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 815,392 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 11.5% in Brazil, while Brazil has more children (19.4% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 7.9× as densely populated (199.5 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Italy live 7.9 years longer on average (84.0 vs 76.0). GDP per capita is 4.0× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $10,713).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Italy); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
