Tanzania has 1.2× the population of Kenya: 70,545,865 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 13.0 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +1.93% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +1,110,192. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. People in Tanzania live 3.4 years longer on average (67.2 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in Kenya ($2,363 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
