Bangladesh has 3.2× the population of Myanmar: 175,686,899 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 120.8 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bangladesh is 15.9× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 7.8 years longer on average (74.9 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Bangladesh ($2,597 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
