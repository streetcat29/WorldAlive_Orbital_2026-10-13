Tanzania has about the same population as United Kingdom: 70,545,865 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 1.1 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +206,306. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 3.8× as densely populated (283.2 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 14.2 years longer on average (81.4 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 43.7× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Tanzania) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
