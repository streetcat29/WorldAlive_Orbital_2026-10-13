United States has 1.9× the population of Bangladesh: 341,784,857 vs 175,686,899, a gap of 166.1 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +1,785,717. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Bangladesh would overtake United States in about 96 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2121) — a projection, not a forecast. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 17.1%). Bangladesh is 35.8× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 4.0 years longer on average (78.9 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 34.7× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
