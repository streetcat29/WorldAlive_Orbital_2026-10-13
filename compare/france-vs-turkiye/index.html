Türkiye has 1.2× the population of France: 85,878,556 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 17.2 million. Türkiye is growing faster (+0.42% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 360,651 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 16.2%). People in France live 5.6 years longer on average (83.0 vs 77.4). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in France ($48,986 vs $18,599).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (France) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
