Russia has 2.5× the population of Kenya: 143,513,328 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 86.0 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Russia in about 45 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2070) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 17.0%). Kenya is 10.8× as densely populated (95.3 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 9.6 years longer on average (73.4 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 7.4× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
