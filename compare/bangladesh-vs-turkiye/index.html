Bangladesh has 2.0× the population of Türkiye: 175,686,899 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 89.8 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 21.0%). Bangladesh is 11.9× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 2.5 years longer on average (77.4 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 7.2× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
