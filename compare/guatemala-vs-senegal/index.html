Senegal has about the same population as Guatemala: 18,931,966 vs 18,687,881, a gap of 244,085. Senegal is growing faster (+2.30% vs +1.52% a year), adding about 434,940 people a year against +283,664. Guatemala is the older society: 5.0% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Senegal, while Senegal has more children (37.7% under 15 vs 31.0%). Guatemala is 1.8× as densely populated (169.1 vs 93.9 people per km²). People in Guatemala live 3.8 years longer on average (72.7 vs 68.9). GDP per capita is 3.4× higher in Guatemala ($6,598 vs $1,955).
