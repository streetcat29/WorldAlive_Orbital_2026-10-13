Benin has about the same population as Rwanda: 14,814,460 vs 14,569,341, a gap of 245,119. Benin is growing faster (+2.40% vs +2.17% a year), adding about 355,979 people a year against +316,180. Rwanda is 4.5× as densely populated (565.6 vs 125.1 people per km²). People in Rwanda live 7.1 years longer on average (68.0 vs 61.0). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Benin ($1,658 vs $1,124).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Benin) and 2025 (Rwanda); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
