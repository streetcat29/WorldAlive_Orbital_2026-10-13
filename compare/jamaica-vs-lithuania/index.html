Lithuania has about the same population as Jamaica: 2,888,774 vs 2,837,077, a gap of 51,697. Lithuania is growing faster (+0.02% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 496 people a year against −2,097. Lithuania is the older society: 20.7% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Jamaica, while Jamaica has more children (18.3% under 15 vs 14.5%). Jamaica is 5.7× as densely populated (262.2 vs 45.9 people per km²). People in Lithuania live 5.6 years longer on average (77.2 vs 71.6). GDP per capita is 4.1× higher in Lithuania ($32,959 vs $8,003).
