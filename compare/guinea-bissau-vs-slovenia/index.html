Guinea-Bissau has 6% more people than Slovenia: 2,249,515 vs 2,130,986, a gap of 118,529. Guinea-Bissau is growing faster (+2.16% vs +0.17% a year), adding about 48,686 people a year against +3,589. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Slovenia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Guinea-Bissau, while Guinea-Bissau has more children (38.3% under 15 vs 14.5%). Slovenia is 1.4× as densely populated (105.3 vs 76.6 people per km²). People in Slovenia live 18.0 years longer on average (82.3 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 33.3× higher in Slovenia ($37,376 vs $1,124).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Guinea-Bissau) and 2025 (Slovenia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
