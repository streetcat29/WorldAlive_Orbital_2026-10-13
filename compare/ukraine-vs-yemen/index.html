Yemen has 7% more people than Ukraine: 41,773,878 vs 38,980,376, a gap of 2.8 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+2.92% vs +2.89% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Ukraine is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 2.5% in Yemen, while Yemen has more children (41.0% under 15 vs 13.7%). People in Ukraine live 5.3 years longer on average (74.7 vs 69.4). GDP per capita is 9.3× higher in Ukraine ($5,866 vs $634).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ukraine) and 2025 (Yemen); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
