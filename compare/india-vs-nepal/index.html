India has 49.4× the population of Nepal: 1,463,865,525 vs 29,618,118, a gap of 1.43 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −32,918. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. India is 2.3× as densely populated (483.7 vs 207.1 people per km²). People in India live 1.6 years longer on average (72.2 vs 70.6). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in India ($2,702 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Nepal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
