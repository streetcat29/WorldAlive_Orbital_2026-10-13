Egypt vs Türkiye

Egypt has 1.4× the population of Türkiye: 118,365,995 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 32.5 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 21.0%). People in Türkiye live 5.6 years longer on average (77.4 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 6.0× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
