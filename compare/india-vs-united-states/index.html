India has 4.3× the population of United States: 1,463,865,525 vs 341,784,857, a gap of 1.12 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +1,785,717. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 17.1%). India is 13.1× as densely populated (483.7 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 6.7 years longer on average (78.9 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 33.3× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
