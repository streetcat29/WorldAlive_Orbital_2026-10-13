Bolivia has 3% more people than South Sudan: 12,581,843 vs 12,188,788, a gap of 393,055. South Sudan is growing faster (+2.03% vs +1.35% a year), adding about 247,884 people a year against +169,667. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, South Sudan would overtake Bolivia in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Bolivia is the older society: 5.7% aged 65+ against 3.1% in South Sudan, while South Sudan has more children (38.0% under 15 vs 29.4%). South Sudan is 1.6× as densely populated (18.2 vs 11.3 people per km²). People in Bolivia live 11.0 years longer on average (68.7 vs 57.7). GDP per capita is 4.8× higher in Bolivia ($5,148 vs $1,080).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bolivia) and 2025 (South Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
