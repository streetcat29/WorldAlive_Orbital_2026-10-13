Bangladesh has 2.5× the population of Thailand: 175,686,899 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 104.1 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 14.4%). Bangladesh is 9.4× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 140.3 people per km²). People in Thailand live 1.6 years longer on average (76.6 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
