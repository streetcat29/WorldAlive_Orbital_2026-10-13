Pakistan has 4.7× the population of Myanmar: 255,219,554 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 200.4 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Myanmar is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 24.1%). Pakistan is 3.9× as densely populated (321.1 vs 82.9 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
