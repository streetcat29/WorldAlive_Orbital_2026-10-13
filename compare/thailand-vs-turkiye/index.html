Türkiye has 1.2× the population of Thailand: 85,878,556 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 14.3 million. Türkiye is growing faster (+0.42% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 360,651 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 14.4%). GDP per capita is 2.3× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Thailand) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
