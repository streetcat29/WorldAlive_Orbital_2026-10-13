Colombia has 3% more people than South Korea: 53,425,635 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 1.7 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 11.2× as densely populated (529.8 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in South Korea live 5.7 years longer on average (83.6 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 4.2× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $8,562).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
