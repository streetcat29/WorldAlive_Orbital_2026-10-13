Ukraine has 5% more people than Uzbekistan: 38,980,376 vs 37,053,428, a gap of 1.9 million. Ukraine is growing faster (+2.92% vs +1.88% a year), adding about 1,136,565 people a year against +698,104. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Ukraine is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 6.1% in Uzbekistan, while Uzbekistan has more children (31.3% under 15 vs 13.7%). People in Ukraine live 2.2 years longer on average (74.7 vs 72.5). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Ukraine ($5,866 vs $3,968).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ukraine) and 2025 (Uzbekistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
