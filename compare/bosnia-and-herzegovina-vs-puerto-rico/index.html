Puerto Rico has about the same population as Bosnia and Herzegovina: 3,184,835 vs 3,140,095, a gap of 44,740. Puerto Rico is shrinking more slowly (−0.55% vs −0.77% a year), adding about -17,637 people a year against −24,066. Puerto Rico is the older society: 25.3% aged 65+ against 22.9% in Bosnia and Herzegovina, while Bosnia and Herzegovina has more children (12.8% under 15 vs 11.2%). Puerto Rico is 5.8× as densely populated (361.2 vs 62.2 people per km²). People in Puerto Rico live 3.9 years longer on average (81.9 vs 78.0). GDP per capita is 3.9× higher in Puerto Rico ($40,620 vs $10,382).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bosnia and Herzegovina) and 2025 (Puerto Rico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
