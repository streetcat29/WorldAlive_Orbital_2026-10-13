Azerbaijan has 11% more people than Austria: 10,246,996 vs 9,208,163, a gap of 1.0 million. Azerbaijan is growing faster (+0.43% vs +0.33% a year), adding about 44,261 people a year against +30,231. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Austria is the older society: 21.1% aged 65+ against 9.2% in Azerbaijan, while Azerbaijan has more children (21.2% under 15 vs 14.1%). People in Austria live 7.4 years longer on average (82.0 vs 74.6). GDP per capita is 8.5× higher in Austria ($62,930 vs $7,411).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Austria) and 2025 (Azerbaijan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
