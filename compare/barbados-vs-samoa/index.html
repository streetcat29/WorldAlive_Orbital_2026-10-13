Barbados has 1.3× the population of Samoa: 282,623 vs 219,306, a gap of 63,317. Samoa is growing faster (+0.59% vs +0.06% a year), adding about 1,291 people a year against +156. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Samoa would overtake Barbados in about 48 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2073) — a projection, not a forecast. Barbados is the older society: 17.1% aged 65+ against 6.1% in Samoa, while Samoa has more children (38.1% under 15 vs 17.1%). Barbados is 8.4× as densely populated (656.6 vs 77.9 people per km²). People in Barbados live 4.5 years longer on average (76.3 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 4.8× higher in Barbados ($28,365 vs $5,873).
