Pakistan has 4.9× the population of Sudan: 255,219,554 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 203.6 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +3,981,282. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Pakistan in about 196 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2221) — a projection, not a forecast. Pakistan is 12.0× as densely populated (321.1 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Pakistan live 1.3 years longer on average (67.8 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Pakistan ($1,596 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Pakistan) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
