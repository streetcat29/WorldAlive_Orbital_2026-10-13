Afghanistan has 5% more people than Yemen: 43,844,111 vs 41,773,878, a gap of 2.1 million. Yemen is growing faster (+2.89% vs +2.77% a year), adding about 1,208,013 people a year against +1,213,252. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Yemen would overtake Afghanistan in about 39 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2064) — a projection, not a forecast. People in Yemen live 3.1 years longer on average (69.4 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Yemen ($634 vs $417).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Afghanistan) and 2025 (Yemen); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
