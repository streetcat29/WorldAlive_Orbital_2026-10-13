Mexico has 2.2× the population of Italy: 131,946,900 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 73.0 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 3.0× as densely populated (199.5 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Italy live 8.7 years longer on average (84.0 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $13,889).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
