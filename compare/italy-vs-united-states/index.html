United States has 5.8× the population of Italy: 341,784,857 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 282.9 million. United States is growing faster (+0.52% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 1,785,717 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 18.4% in United States, while United States has more children (17.1% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 5.4× as densely populated (199.5 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in Italy live 5.1 years longer on average (84.0 vs 78.9). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $43,309).
