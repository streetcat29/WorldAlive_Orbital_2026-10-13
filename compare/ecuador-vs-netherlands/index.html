Ecuador has about the same population as Netherlands: 18,289,896 vs 18,087,633, a gap of 202,263. Ecuador is growing faster (+0.85% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 155,074 people a year against +94,394. Netherlands is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Ecuador, while Ecuador has more children (23.9% under 15 vs 14.9%). Netherlands is 7.3× as densely populated (531.0 vs 72.4 people per km²). People in Netherlands live 4.4 years longer on average (82.0 vs 77.6). GDP per capita is 10.3× higher in Netherlands ($73,684 vs $7,125).
