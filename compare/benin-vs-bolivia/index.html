Benin has 1.2× the population of Bolivia: 14,814,460 vs 12,581,843, a gap of 2.2 million. Benin is growing faster (+2.40% vs +1.35% a year), adding about 355,979 people a year against +169,667. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bolivia is the older society: 5.7% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Benin, while Benin has more children (41.3% under 15 vs 29.4%). Benin is 11.1× as densely populated (125.1 vs 11.3 people per km²). People in Bolivia live 7.8 years longer on average (68.7 vs 61.0). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in Bolivia ($5,148 vs $1,658).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Benin) and 2025 (Bolivia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
