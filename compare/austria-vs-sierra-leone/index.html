Austria has 4% more people than Sierra Leone: 9,208,163 vs 8,819,794, a gap of 388,369. Sierra Leone is growing faster (+2.04% vs +0.33% a year), adding about 179,588 people a year against +30,231. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sierra Leone would overtake Austria in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Austria is the older society: 21.1% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Sierra Leone, while Sierra Leone has more children (37.7% under 15 vs 14.1%). People in Austria live 20.0 years longer on average (82.0 vs 62.0). GDP per capita is 74.4× higher in Austria ($62,930 vs $846).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Austria) and 2025 (Sierra Leone); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
