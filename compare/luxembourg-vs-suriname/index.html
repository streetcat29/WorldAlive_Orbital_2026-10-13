Luxembourg has 7% more people than Suriname: 686,970 vs 639,850, a gap of 47,120. Luxembourg is growing faster (+1.46% vs +0.85% a year), adding about 10,031 people a year against +5,442. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Luxembourg is the older society: 15.8% aged 65+ against 8.2% in Suriname, while Suriname has more children (25.3% under 15 vs 15.7%). Luxembourg is 66.1× as densely populated (258.9 vs 3.9 people per km²). People in Luxembourg live 9.4 years longer on average (83.2 vs 73.8). GDP per capita is 20.8× higher in Luxembourg ($147,252 vs $7,070).
