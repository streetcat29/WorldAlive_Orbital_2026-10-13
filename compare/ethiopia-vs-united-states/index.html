United States has 2.5× the population of Ethiopia: 341,784,857 vs 135,472,051, a gap of 206.3 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +1,785,717. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ethiopia would overtake United States in about 46 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2071) — a projection, not a forecast. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 17.1%). Ethiopia is 3.1× as densely populated (114.0 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 11.3 years longer on average (78.9 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 96.5× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
