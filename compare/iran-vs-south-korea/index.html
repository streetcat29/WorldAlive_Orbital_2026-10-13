Iran has 1.8× the population of South Korea: 92,417,681 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 40.7 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 9.5× as densely populated (529.8 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in South Korea live 5.8 years longer on average (83.6 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 9.2× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
