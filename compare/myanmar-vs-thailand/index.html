Thailand has 1.3× the population of Myanmar: 71,619,863 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 16.8 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against −48,132. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Myanmar would overtake Thailand in about 38 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2063) — a projection, not a forecast. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 1.7× as densely populated (140.3 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Thailand live 9.5 years longer on average (76.6 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
