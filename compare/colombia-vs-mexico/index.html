Mexico has 2.5× the population of Colombia: 131,946,900 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 78.5 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +1,090,386. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 20.0%). Mexico is 1.4× as densely populated (66.7 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 2.6 years longer on average (77.9 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $8,562).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
