Cambodia has 1.2× the population of Guinea: 17,847,982 vs 15,099,727, a gap of 2.7 million. Guinea is growing faster (+2.31% vs +1.18% a year), adding about 348,943 people a year against +210,416. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guinea would overtake Cambodia in about 15 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2040) — a projection, not a forecast. Cambodia is the older society: 6.4% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Guinea, while Guinea has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 29.5%). Cambodia is 1.7× as densely populated (98.7 vs 58.6 people per km²). People in Cambodia live 9.9 years longer on average (70.8 vs 60.9). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Cambodia ($2,872 vs $1,877).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cambodia) and 2025 (Guinea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
