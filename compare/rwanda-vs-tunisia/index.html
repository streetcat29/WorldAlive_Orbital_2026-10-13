Rwanda has 1.2× the population of Tunisia: 14,569,341 vs 12,348,573, a gap of 2.2 million. Rwanda is growing faster (+2.17% vs +0.58% a year), adding about 316,180 people a year against +71,672. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Tunisia is the older society: 9.9% aged 65+ against 4.0% in Rwanda, while Rwanda has more children (37.0% under 15 vs 23.6%). Rwanda is 7.2× as densely populated (565.6 vs 78.5 people per km²). People in Tunisia live 8.7 years longer on average (76.7 vs 68.0). GDP per capita is 4.1× higher in Tunisia ($4,657 vs $1,124).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Rwanda) and 2025 (Tunisia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
