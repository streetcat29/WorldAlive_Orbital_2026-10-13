Belarus has 3% more people than Sierra Leone: 9,085,991 vs 8,819,794, a gap of 266,197. Sierra Leone is growing faster (+2.04% vs −0.51% a year), adding about 179,588 people a year against −46,519. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sierra Leone would overtake Belarus in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Belarus is the older society: 18.3% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Sierra Leone, while Sierra Leone has more children (37.7% under 15 vs 15.9%). Sierra Leone is 2.6× as densely populated (117.2 vs 45.2 people per km²). People in Belarus live 12.4 years longer on average (74.4 vs 62.0). GDP per capita is 12.1× higher in Belarus ($10,279 vs $846).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Belarus) and 2025 (Sierra Leone); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
