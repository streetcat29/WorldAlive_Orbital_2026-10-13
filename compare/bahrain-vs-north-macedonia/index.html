North Macedonia has 14% more people than Bahrain: 1,820,909 vs 1,600,366, a gap of 220,543. Bahrain is growing faster (+0.73% vs −0.19% a year), adding about 11,739 people a year against −3,447. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Bahrain would overtake North Macedonia in about 14 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2039) — a projection, not a forecast. North Macedonia is the older society: 18.5% aged 65+ against 4.0% in Bahrain, while Bahrain has more children (18.4% under 15 vs 16.5%). Bahrain is 27.2× as densely populated (1,971.3 vs 72.5 people per km²). People in Bahrain live 4.8 years longer on average (81.4 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 2.9× higher in Bahrain ($30,597 vs $10,490).
