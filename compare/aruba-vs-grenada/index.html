Grenada has 8% more people than Aruba: 117,303 vs 108,785, a gap of 8,518. Aruba is growing faster (+0.73% vs +0.08% a year), adding about 793 people a year against +96. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Aruba would overtake Grenada in about 12 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2037) — a projection, not a forecast. Aruba is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 12.6% in Grenada, while Grenada has more children (19.0% under 15 vs 16.5%). Aruba is 1.7× as densely populated (596.4 vs 344.4 people per km²). People in Aruba live 1.1 years longer on average (76.5 vs 75.4). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Aruba ($38,591 vs $12,107).
