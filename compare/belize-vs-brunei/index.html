Brunei has 10% more people than Belize: 466,330 vs 422,924, a gap of 43,406. Belize is growing faster (+1.39% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 5,893 people a year against +3,623. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Belize would overtake Brunei in about 16 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2041) — a projection, not a forecast. Brunei is the older society: 7.2% aged 65+ against 5.2% in Belize, while Belize has more children (26.3% under 15 vs 20.6%). Brunei is 4.8× as densely populated (87.1 vs 18.0 people per km²). People in Brunei live 1.8 years longer on average (75.5 vs 73.7). GDP per capita is 4.1× higher in Brunei ($32,235 vs $7,865).
