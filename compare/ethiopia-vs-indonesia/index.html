Ethiopia vs Indonesia

Indonesia has 2.1× the population of Ethiopia: 285,721,236 vs 135,472,051, a gap of 150.2 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +2,242,079. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ethiopia would overtake Indonesia in about 42 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2067) — a projection, not a forecast. Indonesia is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 24.2%). Indonesia is 1.3× as densely populated (148.6 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in Indonesia live 3.7 years longer on average (71.3 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in Indonesia ($5,060 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Indonesia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
