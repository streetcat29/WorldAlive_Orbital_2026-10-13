Nigeria has 4.4× the population of Colombia: 237,527,782 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 184.1 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +1.01% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +542,012. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 20.0%). Nigeria is 5.3× as densely populated (250.2 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 23.3 years longer on average (77.9 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 7.0× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
