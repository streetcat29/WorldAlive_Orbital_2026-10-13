Mali has 15% more people than Zambia: 25,198,821 vs 21,913,874, a gap of 3.3 million. Mali is growing faster (+2.90% vs +2.77% a year), adding about 730,719 people a year against +607,255. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Zambia is 1.4× as densely populated (27.9 vs 19.5 people per km²). People in Zambia live 5.9 years longer on average (66.5 vs 60.7).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mali) and 2025 (Zambia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
