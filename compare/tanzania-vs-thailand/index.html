Thailand has about the same population as Tanzania: 71,619,863 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 1.1 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against −48,132. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Thailand in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 1.9× as densely populated (140.3 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in Thailand live 9.4 years longer on average (76.6 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 6.1× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $1,319).
