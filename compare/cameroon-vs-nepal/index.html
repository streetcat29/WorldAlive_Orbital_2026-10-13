Cameroon has about the same population as Nepal: 29,879,337 vs 29,618,118, a gap of 261,219. Cameroon is growing faster (+2.56% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 765,311 people a year against −32,918. Nepal is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 2.8% in Cameroon, while Cameroon has more children (41.1% under 15 vs 28.1%). Nepal is 3.5× as densely populated (207.1 vs 60.0 people per km²). People in Nepal live 6.7 years longer on average (70.6 vs 64.0). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Cameroon ($1,972 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cameroon) and 2025 (Nepal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
