Egypt has 2.3× the population of Sudan: 118,365,995 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 66.7 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +1,841,995. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Egypt in about 101 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2126) — a projection, not a forecast. Egypt is the older society: 5.3% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 31.6%). Egypt is 4.3× as densely populated (115.1 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Egypt live 5.3 years longer on average (71.8 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Egypt ($3,086 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
