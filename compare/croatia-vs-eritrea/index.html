Croatia has 7% more people than Eritrea: 3,876,200 vs 3,607,003, a gap of 269,197. Eritrea is growing faster (+2.00% vs +0.26% a year), adding about 72,116 people a year against +10,013. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Eritrea would overtake Croatia in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Croatia is the older society: 23.6% aged 65+ against 4.3% in Eritrea, while Eritrea has more children (37.6% under 15 vs 13.7%). Croatia is 2.4× as densely populated (69.0 vs 28.6 people per km²). People in Croatia live 10.0 years longer on average (78.9 vs 68.9). GDP per capita is 39.4× higher in Croatia ($27,104 vs $689).
