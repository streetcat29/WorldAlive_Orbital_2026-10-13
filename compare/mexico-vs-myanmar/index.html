Mexico has 2.4× the population of Myanmar: 131,946,900 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 77.1 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. People in Mexico live 8.2 years longer on average (75.3 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 9.3× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
