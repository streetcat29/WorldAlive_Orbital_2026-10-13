Kenya has 11% more people than South Korea: 57,532,493 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 5.8 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 5.6× as densely populated (529.8 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in South Korea live 19.8 years longer on average (83.6 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 15.3× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
