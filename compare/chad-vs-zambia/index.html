Zambia has 4% more people than Chad: 21,913,874 vs 21,003,705, a gap of 910,169. Chad is growing faster (+3.41% vs +2.77% a year), adding about 716,671 people a year against +607,255. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Chad would overtake Zambia in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Zambia is 1.8× as densely populated (27.9 vs 15.3 people per km²). People in Zambia live 11.3 years longer on average (66.5 vs 55.2). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Zambia ($1,318 vs $1,022).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chad) and 2025 (Zambia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
