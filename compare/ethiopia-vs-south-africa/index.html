Ethiopia vs South Africa

Ethiopia has 2.1× the population of South Africa: 135,472,051 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 70.7 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +1.15% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +744,395. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Africa is the older society: 6.9% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 25.7%). Ethiopia is 2.2× as densely populated (114.0 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in Ethiopia live 1.3 years longer on average (67.6 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
