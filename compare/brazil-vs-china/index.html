China has 6.6× the population of Brazil: 1,406,585,000 vs 212,812,405, a gap of 1.19 billion. Brazil is growing faster (+0.38% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 815,392 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 11.5% in Brazil, while Brazil has more children (19.4% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 5.9× as densely populated (150.3 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in China live 2.0 years longer on average (78.0 vs 76.0). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in China ($13,862 vs $10,713).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (China); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
