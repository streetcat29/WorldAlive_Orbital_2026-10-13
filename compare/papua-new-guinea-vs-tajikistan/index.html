Tajikistan has about the same population as Papua New Guinea: 10,786,734 vs 10,762,817, a gap of 23,917. Tajikistan is growing faster (+1.83% vs +1.75% a year), adding about 197,606 people a year against +187,947. Tajikistan is 3.3× as densely populated (74.9 vs 22.9 people per km²). People in Tajikistan live 5.7 years longer on average (71.9 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in Papua New Guinea ($3,020 vs $1,637).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Papua New Guinea) and 2025 (Tajikistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
