Indonesia vs Türkiye

Indonesia has 3.3× the population of Türkiye: 285,721,236 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 199.8 million. Indonesia is growing faster (+0.78% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 2,242,079 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Indonesia, while Indonesia has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 21.0%). Indonesia is 1.3× as densely populated (148.6 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 6.1 years longer on average (77.4 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 3.7× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $5,060).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
