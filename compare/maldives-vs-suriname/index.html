Suriname has 1.2× the population of Maldives: 639,850 vs 529,676, a gap of 110,174. Suriname is growing faster (+0.85% vs +0.35% a year), adding about 5,442 people a year against +1,880. GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Maldives ($14,615 vs $7,070).
