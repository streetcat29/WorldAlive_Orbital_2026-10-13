Faroe Islands has 1.2× the population of Saint Kitts and Nevis: 54,900 vs 46,922, a gap of 7,978. Faroe Islands is growing faster (+0.47% vs +0.17% a year), adding about 261 people a year against +79. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Faroe Islands is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 11.7% in Saint Kitts and Nevis. Saint Kitts and Nevis is 4.5× as densely populated (179.8 vs 39.7 people per km²). People in Faroe Islands live 10.8 years longer on average (83.1 vs 72.3). GDP per capita is 2.9× higher in Faroe Islands ($74,175 vs $25,223).
