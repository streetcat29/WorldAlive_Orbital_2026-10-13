South Africa has 1.3× the population of South Korea: 64,747,319 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 13.1 million. South Africa is growing faster (+1.15% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 744,395 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 6.9% in South Africa, while South Africa has more children (25.7% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 10.2× as densely populated (529.8 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in South Korea live 17.3 years longer on average (83.6 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 5.5× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $6,598).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Africa) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
