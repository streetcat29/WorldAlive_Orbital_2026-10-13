India has 10.2× the population of Russia: 1,463,865,525 vs 143,513,328, a gap of 1.32 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −156,235. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 17.0%). India is 55.1× as densely populated (483.7 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 1.2 years longer on average (73.4 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 6.5× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
