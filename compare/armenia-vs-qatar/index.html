Armenia has 4% more people than Qatar: 3,086,700 vs 2,972,215, a gap of 114,485. Qatar is growing faster (+3.92% vs +1.74% a year), adding about 116,653 people a year against +53,664. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Qatar would overtake Armenia in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Armenia is the older society: 14.4% aged 65+ against 1.7% in Qatar. Qatar is 2.2× as densely populated (231.2 vs 105.1 people per km²). People in Qatar live 4.2 years longer on average (82.5 vs 78.3). GDP per capita is 7.7× higher in Qatar ($72,525 vs $9,474).
