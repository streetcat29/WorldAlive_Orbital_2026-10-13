Mauritania has 1.2× the population of Panama: 5,315,065 vs 4,571,189, a gap of 743,876. Mauritania is growing faster (+2.78% vs +1.22% a year), adding about 147,703 people a year against +55,953. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Panama is the older society: 9.7% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Mauritania, while Mauritania has more children (42.4% under 15 vs 24.5%). Panama is 12.3× as densely populated (60.1 vs 4.9 people per km²). People in Panama live 11.1 years longer on average (79.8 vs 68.7). GDP per capita is 9.0× higher in Panama ($19,790 vs $2,198).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mauritania) and 2025 (Panama); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
