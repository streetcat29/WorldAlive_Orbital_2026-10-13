China has 19.9× the population of Tanzania: 1,406,585,000 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 1.34 billion. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake China in about 99 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2124) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 2.0× as densely populated (150.3 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in China live 10.8 years longer on average (78.0 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 10.5× higher in China ($13,862 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
