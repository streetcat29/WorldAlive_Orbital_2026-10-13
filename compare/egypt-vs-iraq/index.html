Egypt has 2.5× the population of Iraq: 118,365,995 vs 47,020,774, a gap of 71.3 million. Iraq is growing faster (+2.10% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 989,089 people a year against +1,841,995. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iraq would overtake Egypt in about 169 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2194) — a projection, not a forecast. Egypt is the older society: 5.3% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Iraq, while Iraq has more children (36.0% under 15 vs 31.6%). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in Iraq ($5,410 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Iraq); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
