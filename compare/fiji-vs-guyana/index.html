Fiji has 12% more people than Guyana: 933,154 vs 835,986, a gap of 97,168. Guyana is growing faster (+0.59% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 4,913 people a year against +4,380. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guyana would overtake Fiji in about 93 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2118) — a projection, not a forecast. Fiji is 12.9× as densely populated (50.6 vs 3.9 people per km²). People in Guyana live 2.9 years longer on average (70.3 vs 67.5). GDP per capita is 4.9× higher in Guyana ($32,414 vs $6,642).
