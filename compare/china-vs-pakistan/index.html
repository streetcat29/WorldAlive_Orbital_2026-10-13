China has 5.5× the population of Pakistan: 1,406,585,000 vs 255,219,554, a gap of 1.15 billion. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Pakistan would overtake China in about 99 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2124) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 15.4%). Pakistan is 2.1× as densely populated (321.1 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in China live 10.2 years longer on average (78.0 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 8.7× higher in China ($13,862 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
