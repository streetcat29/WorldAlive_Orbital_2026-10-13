Egypt has 1.4× the population of Germany: 118,365,995 vs 83,491,249, a gap of 34.9 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against −25,340. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 13.9%). Germany is 2.1× as densely populated (238.4 vs 115.1 people per km²). People in Germany live 9.0 years longer on average (80.8 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 19.6× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Germany); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
