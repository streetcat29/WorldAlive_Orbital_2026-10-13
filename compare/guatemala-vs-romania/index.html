Romania has about the same population as Guatemala: 19,020,271 vs 18,687,881, a gap of 332,390. Guatemala is growing faster (+1.52% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 283,664 people a year against −31,507. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guatemala would overtake Romania in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Romania is the older society: 20.1% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Guatemala, while Guatemala has more children (31.0% under 15 vs 15.6%). Guatemala is 2.0× as densely populated (169.1 vs 82.8 people per km²). People in Romania live 3.7 years longer on average (76.5 vs 72.7). GDP per capita is 3.4× higher in Romania ($22,538 vs $6,598).
