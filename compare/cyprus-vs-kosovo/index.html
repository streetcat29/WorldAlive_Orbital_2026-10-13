Kosovo has 1.2× the population of Cyprus: 1,576,876 vs 1,370,754, a gap of 206,122. Cyprus is growing faster (+0.91% vs −1.10% a year), adding about 12,529 people a year against −17,381. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cyprus would overtake Kosovo in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Cyprus is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 10.5% in Kosovo, while Kosovo has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 16.1%). People in Cyprus live 3.6 years longer on average (81.8 vs 78.2). GDP per capita is 5.3× higher in Cyprus ($41,783 vs $7,899).
