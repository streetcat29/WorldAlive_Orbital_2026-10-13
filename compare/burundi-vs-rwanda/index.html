Rwanda has about the same population as Burundi: 14,569,341 vs 14,390,003, a gap of 179,338. Burundi is growing faster (+2.41% vs +2.17% a year), adding about 346,352 people a year against +316,180. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Burundi would overtake Rwanda in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Rwanda is the older society: 4.0% aged 65+ against 2.5% in Burundi, while Burundi has more children (44.0% under 15 vs 37.0%). People in Rwanda live 4.2 years longer on average (68.0 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 4.8× higher in Rwanda ($1,124 vs $234).
