Qatar has about the same population as Lithuania: 2,972,215 vs 2,888,774, a gap of 83,441. Qatar is growing faster (+3.92% vs +0.02% a year), adding about 116,653 people a year against +496. Lithuania is the older society: 20.7% aged 65+ against 1.7% in Qatar, while Qatar has more children (15.0% under 15 vs 14.5%). Qatar is 5.0× as densely populated (231.2 vs 45.9 people per km²). People in Qatar live 5.3 years longer on average (82.5 vs 77.2). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Qatar ($72,525 vs $32,959).
