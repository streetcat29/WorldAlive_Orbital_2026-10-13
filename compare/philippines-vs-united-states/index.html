United States has 2.9× the population of Philippines: 341,784,857 vs 116,786,962, a gap of 225.0 million. Philippines is growing faster (+0.81% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 947,122 people a year against +1,785,717. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 17.1%). Philippines is 10.5× as densely populated (385.3 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 8.9 years longer on average (78.9 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 21.6× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $4,171).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Philippines) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
