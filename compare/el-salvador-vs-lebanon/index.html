El Salvador has 9% more people than Lebanon: 6,365,503 vs 5,849,421, a gap of 516,082. Lebanon is growing faster (+0.75% vs +0.43% a year), adding about 43,621 people a year against +27,369. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Lebanon would overtake El Salvador in about 27 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2052) — a projection, not a forecast. Lebanon is the older society: 10.4% aged 65+ against 8.3% in El Salvador. Lebanon is 1.9× as densely populated (564.4 vs 304.5 people per km²). People in Lebanon live 5.6 years longer on average (77.9 vs 72.3). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in El Salvador ($5,767 vs $4,473).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (El Salvador) and 2025 (Lebanon); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
