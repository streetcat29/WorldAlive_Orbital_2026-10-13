Iran has 2.0× the population of Iraq: 92,417,681 vs 47,020,774, a gap of 45.4 million. Iraq is growing faster (+2.10% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 989,089 people a year against +853,875. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iraq would overtake Iran in about 57 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2082) — a projection, not a forecast. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Iraq, while Iraq has more children (36.0% under 15 vs 22.0%). Iraq is 1.9× as densely populated (103.8 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 5.4 years longer on average (77.9 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Iraq ($5,410 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Iraq); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
