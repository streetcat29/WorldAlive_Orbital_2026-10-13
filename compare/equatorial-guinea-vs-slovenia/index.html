Slovenia has 10% more people than Equatorial Guinea: 2,130,986 vs 1,938,431, a gap of 192,555. Equatorial Guinea is growing faster (+2.40% vs +0.17% a year), adding about 46,468 people a year against +3,589. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Equatorial Guinea would overtake Slovenia in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Slovenia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Equatorial Guinea, while Equatorial Guinea has more children (37.1% under 15 vs 14.5%). Slovenia is 1.6× as densely populated (105.3 vs 65.9 people per km²). People in Slovenia live 18.4 years longer on average (82.3 vs 63.9). GDP per capita is 5.7× higher in Slovenia ($37,376 vs $6,615).
