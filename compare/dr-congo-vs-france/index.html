DR Congo has 1.6× the population of France: 112,832,473 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 44.1 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 16.2%). France is 2.7× as densely populated (126.9 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in France live 20.9 years longer on average (83.0 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 60.7× higher in France ($48,986 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (France); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
