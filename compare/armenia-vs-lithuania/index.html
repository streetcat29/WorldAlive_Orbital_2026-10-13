Armenia has 7% more people than Lithuania: 3,086,700 vs 2,888,774, a gap of 197,926. Armenia is growing faster (+1.74% vs +0.02% a year), adding about 53,664 people a year against +496. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Lithuania is the older society: 20.7% aged 65+ against 14.4% in Armenia, while Armenia has more children (19.0% under 15 vs 14.5%). Armenia is 2.3× as densely populated (105.1 vs 45.9 people per km²). People in Armenia live 1.1 years longer on average (78.3 vs 77.2). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in Lithuania ($32,959 vs $9,474).
