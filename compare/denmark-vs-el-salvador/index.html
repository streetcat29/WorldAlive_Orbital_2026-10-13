El Salvador has 6% more people than Denmark: 6,365,503 vs 6,009,169, a gap of 356,334. Denmark is growing faster (+0.54% vs +0.43% a year), adding about 32,263 people a year against +27,369. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Denmark would overtake El Salvador in about 54 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2079) — a projection, not a forecast. Denmark is the older society: 21.1% aged 65+ against 8.3% in El Salvador, while El Salvador has more children (24.4% under 15 vs 15.6%). El Salvador is 2.0× as densely populated (304.5 vs 148.7 people per km²). People in Denmark live 10.0 years longer on average (82.3 vs 72.3). GDP per capita is 13.3× higher in Denmark ($76,970 vs $5,767).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Denmark) and 2025 (El Salvador); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
