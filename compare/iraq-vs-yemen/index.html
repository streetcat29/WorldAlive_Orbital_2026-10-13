Iraq has 13% more people than Yemen: 47,020,774 vs 41,773,878, a gap of 5.2 million. Yemen is growing faster (+2.89% vs +2.10% a year), adding about 1,208,013 people a year against +989,089. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Yemen would overtake Iraq in about 15 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2040) — a projection, not a forecast. Iraq is 1.4× as densely populated (103.8 vs 74.6 people per km²). People in Iraq live 3.0 years longer on average (72.4 vs 69.4). GDP per capita is 8.5× higher in Iraq ($5,410 vs $634).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iraq) and 2025 (Yemen); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
