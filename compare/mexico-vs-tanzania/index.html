Mexico has 1.9× the population of Tanzania: 131,946,900 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 61.4 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +1,090,386. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Mexico in about 31 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2056) — a projection, not a forecast. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 24.1%). People in Mexico live 8.1 years longer on average (75.3 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 10.5× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
