Mauritius has 1.4× the population of Comoros: 1,243,741 vs 882,847, a gap of 360,894. Comoros is growing faster (+1.85% vs −0.16% a year), adding about 16,370 people a year against −2,036. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Comoros would overtake Mauritius in about 17 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2042) — a projection, not a forecast. Mauritius is the older society: 14.1% aged 65+ against 4.5% in Comoros, while Comoros has more children (36.9% under 15 vs 14.5%). Mauritius is 1.4× as densely populated (625.3 vs 457.0 people per km²). People in Mauritius live 6.8 years longer on average (73.8 vs 67.0). GDP per capita is 6.3× higher in Mauritius ($12,991 vs $2,056).
