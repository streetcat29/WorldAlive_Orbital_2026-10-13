Cuba has about the same population as Portugal: 10,937,203 vs 10,804,871, a gap of 132,332. Portugal is growing faster (+1.03% vs −0.39% a year), adding about 110,756 people a year against −42,497. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Portugal would overtake Cuba in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast.
