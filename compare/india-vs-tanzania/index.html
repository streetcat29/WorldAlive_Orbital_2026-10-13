India has 20.8× the population of Tanzania: 1,463,865,525 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 1.39 billion. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +12,987,174. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake India in about 154 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2179) — a projection, not a forecast. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 24.2%). India is 6.4× as densely populated (483.7 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in India live 5.0 years longer on average (72.2 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in India ($2,702 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
