Yemen has about the same population as Canada: 41,773,878 vs 41,651,653, a gap of 122,225. Yemen is growing faster (+2.89% vs +0.94% a year), adding about 1,208,013 people a year against +391,155. Canada is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 2.5% in Yemen, while Yemen has more children (41.0% under 15 vs 15.0%). Yemen is 16.4× as densely populated (74.6 vs 4.6 people per km²). People in Canada live 12.7 years longer on average (82.1 vs 69.4). GDP per capita is 87.9× higher in Canada ($55,698 vs $634).
