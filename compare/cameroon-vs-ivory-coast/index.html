Ivory Coast has 9% more people than Cameroon: 32,711,547 vs 29,879,337, a gap of 2.8 million. Cameroon is growing faster (+2.56% vs +2.40% a year), adding about 765,311 people a year against +786,702. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cameroon would overtake Ivory Coast in about 58 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2083) — a projection, not a forecast. Ivory Coast is 1.6× as densely populated (98.0 vs 60.0 people per km²). People in Cameroon live 1.9 years longer on average (64.0 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Ivory Coast ($3,050 vs $1,972).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cameroon) and 2025 (Ivory Coast); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
