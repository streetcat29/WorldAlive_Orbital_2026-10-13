Pakistan has 2.2× the population of Philippines: 255,219,554 vs 116,786,962, a gap of 138.4 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.81% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +947,122. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Philippines is the older society: 5.7% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 27.1%). People in Philippines live 2.1 years longer on average (69.9 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Philippines ($4,171 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Pakistan) and 2025 (Philippines); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
