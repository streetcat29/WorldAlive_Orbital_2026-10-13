Türkiye has 1.3× the population of South Africa: 85,878,556 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 21.1 million. South Africa is growing faster (+1.15% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 744,395 people a year against +360,651. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, South Africa would overtake Türkiye in about 39 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2064) — a projection, not a forecast. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 6.9% in South Africa, while South Africa has more children (25.7% under 15 vs 21.0%). Türkiye is 2.1× as densely populated (110.9 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 11.1 years longer on average (77.4 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 2.8× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $6,598).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Africa) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
