Angola has about the same population as Ukraine: 39,040,039 vs 38,980,376, a gap of 59,663. Angola is growing faster (+3.00% vs +2.92% a year), adding about 1,171,595 people a year against +1,136,565. Ukraine is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 2.9% in Angola, while Angola has more children (44.1% under 15 vs 13.7%). Ukraine is 2.2× as densely populated (65.1 vs 29.5 people per km²). People in Ukraine live 9.9 years longer on average (74.7 vs 64.8). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Ukraine ($5,866 vs $3,129).
