India has 21.1× the population of United Kingdom: 1,463,865,525 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 1.39 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 17.0%). India is 1.7× as densely populated (483.7 vs 283.2 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 9.2 years longer on average (81.4 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 21.3× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
