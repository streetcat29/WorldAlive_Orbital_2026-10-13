Russia has 2.0× the population of Tanzania: 143,513,328 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 73.0 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Russia in about 24 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2049) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 17.0%). Tanzania is 8.6× as densely populated (75.2 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 6.2 years longer on average (73.4 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 13.3× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Russia) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
