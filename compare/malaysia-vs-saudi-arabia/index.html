Saudi Arabia has about the same population as Malaysia: 36,973,555 vs 35,977,838, a gap of 995,717. Saudi Arabia is growing faster (+4.63% vs +1.17% a year), adding about 1,712,320 people a year against +422,638. Malaysia is the older society: 8.0% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Saudi Arabia, while Saudi Arabia has more children (23.6% under 15 vs 21.3%). Malaysia is 6.8× as densely populated (106.9 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Saudi Arabia live 2.2 years longer on average (79.0 vs 76.8). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Saudi Arabia ($34,537 vs $13,125).
