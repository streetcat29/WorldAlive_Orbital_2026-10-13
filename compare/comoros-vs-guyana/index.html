Comoros has 6% more people than Guyana: 882,847 vs 835,986, a gap of 46,861. Comoros is growing faster (+1.85% vs +0.59% a year), adding about 16,370 people a year against +4,913. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Guyana is the older society: 7.0% aged 65+ against 4.5% in Comoros, while Comoros has more children (36.9% under 15 vs 28.9%). Comoros is 116.8× as densely populated (457.0 vs 3.9 people per km²). People in Guyana live 3.3 years longer on average (70.3 vs 67.0). GDP per capita is 15.8× higher in Guyana ($32,414 vs $2,056).
