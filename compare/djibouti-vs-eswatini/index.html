Eswatini has 6% more people than Djibouti: 1,256,174 vs 1,184,076, a gap of 72,098. Djibouti is growing faster (+1.31% vs +1.07% a year), adding about 15,454 people a year against +13,423. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Djibouti would overtake Eswatini in about 25 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2050) — a projection, not a forecast. Eswatini is 1.4× as densely populated (71.5 vs 49.7 people per km²). People in Djibouti live 1.9 years longer on average (66.2 vs 64.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Djibouti) and 2025 (Eswatini); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
