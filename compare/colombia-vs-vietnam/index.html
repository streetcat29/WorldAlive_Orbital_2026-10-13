Vietnam has 1.9× the population of Colombia: 101,598,527 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 48.2 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +612,685. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake Vietnam in about 156 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2181) — a projection, not a forecast. Vietnam is 6.8× as densely populated (320.2 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 3.2 years longer on average (77.9 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $5,066).
