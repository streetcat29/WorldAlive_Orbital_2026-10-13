Bangladesh has 5.9× the population of Nepal: 175,686,899 vs 29,618,118, a gap of 146.1 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against −32,918. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bangladesh is 6.4× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 207.1 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 4.3 years longer on average (74.9 vs 70.6). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Bangladesh ($2,597 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Nepal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
