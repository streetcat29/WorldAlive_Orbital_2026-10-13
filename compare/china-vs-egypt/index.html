China has 11.9× the population of Egypt: 1,406,585,000 vs 118,365,995, a gap of 1.29 billion. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Egypt would overtake China in about 143 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2168) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 1.3× as densely populated (150.3 vs 115.1 people per km²). People in China live 6.2 years longer on average (78.0 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 4.5× higher in China ($13,862 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Egypt); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
