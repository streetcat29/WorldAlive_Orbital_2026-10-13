Nigeria has 1.8× the population of Mexico: 237,527,782 vs 131,946,900, a gap of 105.6 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +1,090,386. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 24.1%). Nigeria is 3.7× as densely populated (250.2 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Mexico live 20.6 years longer on average (75.3 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 11.3× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
