DR Congo has 1.3× the population of Türkiye: 112,832,473 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 27.0 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 21.0%). Türkiye is 2.4× as densely populated (110.9 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 15.3 years longer on average (77.4 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 23.1× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
