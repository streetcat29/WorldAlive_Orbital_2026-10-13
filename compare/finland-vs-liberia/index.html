Liberia has about the same population as Finland: 5,731,206 vs 5,646,436, a gap of 84,770. Liberia is growing faster (+2.09% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 119,629 people a year against +26,587. Finland is the older society: 24.2% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Liberia, while Liberia has more children (39.1% under 15 vs 14.3%). Liberia is 3.1× as densely populated (57.0 vs 18.4 people per km²). People in Finland live 20.0 years longer on average (82.3 vs 62.3). GDP per capita is 61.3× higher in Finland ($56,149 vs $915).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Finland) and 2025 (Liberia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
