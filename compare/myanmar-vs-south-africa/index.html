South Africa has 1.2× the population of Myanmar: 64,747,319 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 9.9 million. South Africa is growing faster (+1.15% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 744,395 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Myanmar is 1.6× as densely populated (82.9 vs 52.1 people per km²). GDP per capita is 4.4× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
