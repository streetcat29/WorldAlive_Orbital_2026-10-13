Indonesia has 4.1× the population of Tanzania: 285,721,236 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 215.2 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +2,242,079. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Indonesia in about 68 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2093) — a projection, not a forecast. Indonesia is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 24.2%). Indonesia is 2.0× as densely populated (148.6 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in Indonesia live 4.1 years longer on average (71.3 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 3.8× higher in Indonesia ($5,060 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
