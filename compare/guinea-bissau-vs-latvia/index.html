Guinea-Bissau has 1.2× the population of Latvia: 2,249,515 vs 1,847,785, a gap of 401,730. Guinea-Bissau is growing faster (+2.16% vs −0.99% a year), adding about 48,686 people a year against −18,249. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Latvia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Guinea-Bissau, while Guinea-Bissau has more children (38.3% under 15 vs 15.2%). Guinea-Bissau is 2.5× as densely populated (76.6 vs 30.3 people per km²). People in Latvia live 12.2 years longer on average (76.4 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 23.4× higher in Latvia ($26,312 vs $1,124).
