Brazil has 1.5× the population of Russia: 212,812,405 vs 143,513,328, a gap of 69.3 million. Brazil is growing faster (+0.38% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 815,392 people a year against −156,235. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 11.5% in Brazil, while Brazil has more children (19.4% under 15 vs 17.0%). Brazil is 2.9× as densely populated (25.3 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Brazil live 2.6 years longer on average (76.0 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $10,713).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
