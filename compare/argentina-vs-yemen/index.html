Argentina has 10% more people than Yemen: 45,851,378 vs 41,773,878, a gap of 4.1 million. Yemen is growing faster (+2.89% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 1,208,013 people a year against +155,482. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Yemen would overtake Argentina in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Argentina is the older society: 12.6% aged 65+ against 2.5% in Yemen, while Yemen has more children (41.0% under 15 vs 21.0%). Yemen is 4.5× as densely populated (74.6 vs 16.6 people per km²). People in Argentina live 8.1 years longer on average (77.5 vs 69.4). GDP per capita is 23.5× higher in Argentina ($14,898 vs $634).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Argentina) and 2025 (Yemen); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
