Türkiye has 8.2× the population of Greece: 85,878,556 vs 10,413,962, a gap of 75.5 million. Türkiye is growing faster (+0.42% vs +0.08% a year), adding about 360,651 people a year against +8,832. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Greece is the older society: 24.4% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 12.9%). Türkiye is 1.4× as densely populated (110.9 vs 80.7 people per km²). People in Greece live 4.4 years longer on average (81.8 vs 77.4). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Greece ($26,948 vs $18,599).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Greece) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
