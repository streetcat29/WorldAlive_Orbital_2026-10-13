New Zealand has 3% more people than Costa Rica: 5,324,700 vs 5,152,950, a gap of 171,750. New Zealand is growing faster (+0.65% vs +0.45% a year), adding about 34,814 people a year against +23,092. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. New Zealand is the older society: 17.6% aged 65+ against 12.8% in Costa Rica, while Costa Rica has more children (18.3% under 15 vs 17.9%). Costa Rica is 5.1× as densely populated (100.0 vs 19.7 people per km²). People in New Zealand live 1.0 years longer on average (82.0 vs 81.0). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in New Zealand ($49,591 vs $19,970).
