Myanmar vs Russia

Russia has 2.6× the population of Myanmar: 143,513,328 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 88.7 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Myanmar would overtake Russia in about 128 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2153) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 17.0%). Myanmar is 9.4× as densely populated (82.9 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 6.3 years longer on average (73.4 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 11.8× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
