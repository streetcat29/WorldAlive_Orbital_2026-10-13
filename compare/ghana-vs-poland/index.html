Poland has 4% more people than Ghana: 36,435,861 vs 35,064,272, a gap of 1.4 million. Ghana is growing faster (+1.83% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 642,712 people a year against −123,164. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ghana would overtake Poland in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Poland is the older society: 20.8% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Ghana, while Ghana has more children (35.4% under 15 vs 14.5%). People in Poland live 12.7 years longer on average (78.4 vs 65.7). GDP per capita is 8.7× higher in Poland ($28,420 vs $3,257).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ghana) and 2025 (Poland); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
