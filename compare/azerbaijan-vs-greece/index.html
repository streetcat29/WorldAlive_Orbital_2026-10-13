Greece has about the same population as Azerbaijan: 10,413,962 vs 10,246,996, a gap of 166,966. Azerbaijan is growing faster (+0.43% vs +0.08% a year), adding about 44,261 people a year against +8,832. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Azerbaijan would overtake Greece in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Greece is the older society: 24.4% aged 65+ against 9.2% in Azerbaijan, while Azerbaijan has more children (21.2% under 15 vs 12.9%). Azerbaijan is 1.5× as densely populated (122.9 vs 80.7 people per km²). People in Greece live 7.3 years longer on average (81.8 vs 74.6). GDP per capita is 3.6× higher in Greece ($26,948 vs $7,411).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Azerbaijan) and 2025 (Greece); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
