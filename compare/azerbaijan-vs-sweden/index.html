Sweden has 3% more people than Azerbaijan: 10,596,620 vs 10,246,996, a gap of 349,624. Azerbaijan is growing faster (+0.43% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 44,261 people a year against +26,945. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Azerbaijan would overtake Sweden in about 19 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2044) — a projection, not a forecast. Sweden is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 9.2% in Azerbaijan, while Azerbaijan has more children (21.2% under 15 vs 16.7%). Azerbaijan is 4.7× as densely populated (122.9 vs 25.9 people per km²). People in Sweden live 9.5 years longer on average (84.1 vs 74.6). GDP per capita is 8.5× higher in Sweden ($63,133 vs $7,411).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Azerbaijan) and 2025 (Sweden); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
