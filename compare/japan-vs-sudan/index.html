Japan has 2.4× the population of Sudan: 123,366,734 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 71.7 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Japan in about 30 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2055) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 12.7× as densely populated (341.5 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Japan live 17.5 years longer on average (84.0 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 30.9× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
