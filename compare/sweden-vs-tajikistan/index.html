Tajikistan has about the same population as Sweden: 10,786,734 vs 10,596,620, a gap of 190,114. Tajikistan is growing faster (+1.83% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 197,606 people a year against +26,945. Sweden is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Tajikistan, while Tajikistan has more children (35.9% under 15 vs 16.7%). Tajikistan is 2.9× as densely populated (74.9 vs 25.9 people per km²). People in Sweden live 12.1 years longer on average (84.1 vs 71.9). GDP per capita is 38.6× higher in Sweden ($63,133 vs $1,637).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sweden) and 2025 (Tajikistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
