Egypt has 1.8× the population of South Africa: 118,365,995 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 53.6 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +1.15% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +744,395. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Africa is the older society: 6.9% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 25.7%). Egypt is 2.2× as densely populated (115.1 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in Egypt live 5.5 years longer on average (71.8 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
