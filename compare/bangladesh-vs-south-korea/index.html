Bangladesh has 3.4× the population of South Korea: 175,686,899 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 124.0 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 10.2%). Bangladesh is 2.5× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 529.8 people per km²). People in South Korea live 8.7 years longer on average (83.6 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 13.9× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
