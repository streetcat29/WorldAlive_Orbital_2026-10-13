India has 2763.7× the population of Maldives: 1,463,865,525 vs 529,676, a gap of 1.46 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.35% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +1,880. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Maldives. Maldives is 3.6× as densely populated (1,765.1 vs 483.7 people per km²). People in Maldives live 9.0 years longer on average (81.3 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in Maldives ($14,615 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Maldives); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
