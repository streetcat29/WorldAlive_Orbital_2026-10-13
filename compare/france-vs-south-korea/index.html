France has 1.3× the population of South Korea: 68,720,337 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 17.0 million. France is growing faster (+0.25% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 168,891 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 20.3% in South Korea. South Korea is 4.2× as densely populated (529.8 vs 126.9 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in France ($48,986 vs $36,227).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (France) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
