Japan has 1.7× the population of Thailand: 123,366,734 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 51.7 million. Thailand is shrinking more slowly (−0.07% vs −0.49% a year), adding about -48,132 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Thailand would overtake Japan in about 128 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2153) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 16.0% in Thailand, while Thailand has more children (14.4% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 2.4× as densely populated (341.5 vs 140.3 people per km²). People in Japan live 7.5 years longer on average (84.0 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 4.5× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
