Hungary has 5% more people than Belarus: 9,514,251 vs 9,085,991, a gap of 428,260. Both are growing at almost the same pace (−0.51% vs −0.50% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Hungary is the older society: 21.2% aged 65+ against 18.3% in Belarus, while Belarus has more children (15.9% under 15 vs 14.4%). Hungary is 2.3× as densely populated (105.1 vs 45.2 people per km²). People in Hungary live 2.4 years longer on average (76.7 vs 74.4). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Hungary ($25,907 vs $10,279).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Belarus) and 2025 (Hungary); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
