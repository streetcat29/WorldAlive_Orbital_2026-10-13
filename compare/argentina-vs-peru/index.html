Argentina has 1.3× the population of Peru: 45,851,378 vs 34,576,665, a gap of 11.3 million. Peru is growing faster (+1.04% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 360,692 people a year against +155,482. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Peru would overtake Argentina in about 40 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2065) — a projection, not a forecast. Argentina is the older society: 12.6% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Peru, while Peru has more children (23.6% under 15 vs 21.0%). Peru is 1.6× as densely populated (26.4 vs 16.6 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Argentina ($14,898 vs $9,684).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Argentina) and 2025 (Peru); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
