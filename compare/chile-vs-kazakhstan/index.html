Kazakhstan has 5% more people than Chile: 20,843,754 vs 19,859,921, a gap of 983,833. Kazakhstan is growing faster (+1.21% vs +0.48% a year), adding about 252,709 people a year against +95,379. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Chile is the older society: 14.6% aged 65+ against 9.0% in Kazakhstan, while Kazakhstan has more children (29.1% under 15 vs 16.5%). Chile is 3.5× as densely populated (26.5 vs 7.5 people per km²). People in Chile live 6.8 years longer on average (81.4 vs 74.5).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chile) and 2025 (Kazakhstan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
