Eswatini has 1.3× the population of Fiji: 1,256,174 vs 933,154, a gap of 323,020. Eswatini is growing faster (+1.07% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 13,423 people a year against +4,380. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Fiji is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 4.3% in Eswatini, while Eswatini has more children (33.0% under 15 vs 26.8%). Eswatini is 1.4× as densely populated (71.5 vs 50.6 people per km²). People in Fiji live 3.2 years longer on average (67.5 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Fiji ($6,642 vs $4,108).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Eswatini) and 2025 (Fiji); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
