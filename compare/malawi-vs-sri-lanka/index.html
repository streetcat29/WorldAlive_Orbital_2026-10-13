Malawi has about the same population as Sri Lanka: 22,216,120 vs 21,756,000, a gap of 460,120. Malawi is growing faster (+2.56% vs −0.73% a year), adding about 568,034 people a year against −159,415. Sri Lanka is the older society: 12.5% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Malawi, while Malawi has more children (40.1% under 15 vs 21.7%). Sri Lanka is 1.6× as densely populated (356.2 vs 223.8 people per km²). People in Sri Lanka live 10.1 years longer on average (77.7 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 7.4× higher in Sri Lanka ($5,002 vs $672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Malawi) and 2025 (Sri Lanka); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
