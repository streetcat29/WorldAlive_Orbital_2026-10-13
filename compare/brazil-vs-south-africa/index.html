Brazil vs South Africa

Brazil has 3.3× the population of South Africa: 212,812,405 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 148.1 million. South Africa is growing faster (+1.15% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 744,395 people a year against +815,392. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, South Africa would overtake Brazil in about 155 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2180) — a projection, not a forecast. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 6.9% in South Africa, while South Africa has more children (25.7% under 15 vs 19.4%). South Africa is 2.1× as densely populated (52.1 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Brazil live 9.7 years longer on average (76.0 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $6,598).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
