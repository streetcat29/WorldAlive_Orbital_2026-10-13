Kiribati has 1.2× the population of Micronesia: 136,488 vs 113,683, a gap of 22,805. Kiribati is growing faster (+1.45% vs +0.46% a year), adding about 1,984 people a year against +524. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Micronesia is the older society: 6.2% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Kiribati, while Kiribati has more children (34.4% under 15 vs 31.5%). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Micronesia ($4,414 vs $2,559).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kiribati) and 2025 (Micronesia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
