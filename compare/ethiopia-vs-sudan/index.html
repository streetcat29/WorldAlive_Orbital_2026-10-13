Ethiopia has 2.6× the population of Sudan: 135,472,051 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 83.8 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +2.38% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +1,227,656. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Ethiopia is 4.3× as densely populated (114.0 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Ethiopia live 1.1 years longer on average (67.6 vs 66.5).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
