Bangladesh has 1.7× the population of Vietnam: 175,686,899 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 74.1 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +612,685. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 22.9%). Bangladesh is 4.1× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 320.2 people per km²). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
