Bahrain has 1.2× the population of Cyprus: 1,600,366 vs 1,370,754, a gap of 229,612. Cyprus is growing faster (+0.91% vs +0.73% a year), adding about 12,529 people a year against +11,739. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cyprus would overtake Bahrain in about 86 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2111) — a projection, not a forecast. Cyprus is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 4.0% in Bahrain, while Bahrain has more children (18.4% under 15 vs 16.1%). Bahrain is 13.5× as densely populated (1,971.3 vs 145.6 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Cyprus ($41,783 vs $30,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bahrain) and 2025 (Cyprus); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
