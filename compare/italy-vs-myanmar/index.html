Italy has 7% more people than Myanmar: 58,915,656 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 4.1 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against −37,036. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Myanmar would overtake Italy in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 2.4× as densely populated (199.5 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Italy live 16.9 years longer on average (84.0 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 29.1× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
