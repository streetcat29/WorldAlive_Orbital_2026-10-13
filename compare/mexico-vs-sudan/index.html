Mexico has 2.6× the population of Sudan: 131,946,900 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 80.3 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +1,090,386. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Mexico in about 60 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2085) — a projection, not a forecast. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 24.1%). Mexico is 2.5× as densely populated (66.7 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Mexico live 8.7 years longer on average (75.3 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 11.9× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
