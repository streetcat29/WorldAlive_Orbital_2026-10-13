Norway has about the same population as Central African Republic: 5,610,870 vs 5,513,282, a gap of 97,588. Central African Republic is growing faster (+3.37% vs +0.69% a year), adding about 185,684 people a year against +38,724. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Central African Republic would overtake Norway in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Norway is 1.8× as densely populated (15.2 vs 8.3 people per km²). People in Norway live 25.5 years longer on average (83.2 vs 57.7). GDP per capita is 170.1× higher in Norway ($94,594 vs $556).
