Djibouti has 1.3× the population of Comoros: 1,184,076 vs 882,847, a gap of 301,229. Comoros is growing faster (+1.85% vs +1.31% a year), adding about 16,370 people a year against +15,454. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Comoros would overtake Djibouti in about 53 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2078) — a projection, not a forecast. Comoros is 9.2× as densely populated (457.0 vs 49.7 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Djibouti ($3,906 vs $2,056).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Comoros) and 2025 (Djibouti); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
