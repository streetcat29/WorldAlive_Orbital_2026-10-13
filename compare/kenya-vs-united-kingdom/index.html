United Kingdom has 1.2× the population of Kenya: 69,487,000 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 12.0 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +206,306. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake United Kingdom in about 12 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2037) — a projection, not a forecast. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 3.0× as densely populated (283.2 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 17.6 years longer on average (81.4 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 24.4× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
