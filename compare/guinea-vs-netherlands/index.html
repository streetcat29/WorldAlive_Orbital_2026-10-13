Netherlands has 1.2× the population of Guinea: 18,087,633 vs 15,099,727, a gap of 3.0 million. Guinea is growing faster (+2.31% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 348,943 people a year against +94,394. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guinea would overtake Netherlands in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Netherlands is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Guinea, while Guinea has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 14.9%). Netherlands is 9.1× as densely populated (531.0 vs 58.6 people per km²). People in Netherlands live 21.1 years longer on average (82.0 vs 60.9). GDP per capita is 39.3× higher in Netherlands ($73,684 vs $1,877).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Guinea) and 2025 (Netherlands); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
