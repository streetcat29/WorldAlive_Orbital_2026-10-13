Kazakhstan has 10% more people than Romania: 20,843,754 vs 19,020,271, a gap of 1.8 million. Kazakhstan is growing faster (+1.21% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 252,709 people a year against −31,507. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Romania is the older society: 20.1% aged 65+ against 9.0% in Kazakhstan, while Kazakhstan has more children (29.1% under 15 vs 15.6%). Romania is 11.0× as densely populated (82.8 vs 7.5 people per km²). People in Romania live 1.9 years longer on average (76.5 vs 74.5). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Romania ($22,538 vs $14,692).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kazakhstan) and 2025 (Romania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
