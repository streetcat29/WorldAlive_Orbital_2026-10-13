Zimbabwe has 12% more people than Guinea: 16,950,795 vs 15,099,727, a gap of 1.9 million. Guinea is growing faster (+2.31% vs +1.88% a year), adding about 348,943 people a year against +319,413. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guinea would overtake Zimbabwe in about 27 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2052) — a projection, not a forecast. Guinea is 1.4× as densely populated (58.6 vs 42.2 people per km²). People in Zimbabwe live 2.2 years longer on average (63.1 vs 60.9). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Zimbabwe ($3,021 vs $1,877).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Guinea) and 2025 (Zimbabwe); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
