Vietnam has 1.2× the population of Türkiye: 101,598,527 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 15.7 million. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 21.0%). Vietnam is 2.9× as densely populated (320.2 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 2.7 years longer on average (77.4 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 3.7× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $5,066).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Türkiye) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
