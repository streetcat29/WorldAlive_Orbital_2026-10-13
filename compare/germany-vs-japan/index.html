Japan has 1.5× the population of Germany: 123,366,734 vs 83,491,249, a gap of 39.9 million. Germany is shrinking more slowly (−0.03% vs −0.49% a year), adding about -25,340 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Germany would overtake Japan in about 85 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2110) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 23.7% in Germany, while Germany has more children (13.9% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 1.4× as densely populated (341.5 vs 238.4 people per km²). People in Japan live 3.2 years longer on average (84.0 vs 80.8). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $35,951).
