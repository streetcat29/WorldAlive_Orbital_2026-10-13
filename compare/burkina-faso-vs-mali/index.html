Mali has 5% more people than Burkina Faso: 25,198,821 vs 24,074,580, a gap of 1.1 million. Mali is growing faster (+2.90% vs +2.21% a year), adding about 730,719 people a year against +531,626. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Burkina Faso is 4.3× as densely populated (84.2 vs 19.5 people per km²).
