Austria has 1.7× the population of Slovakia: 9,208,163 vs 5,413,813, a gap of 3.8 million. Austria is growing faster (+0.33% vs −0.15% a year), adding about 30,231 people a year against −8,250. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Austria is the older society: 21.1% aged 65+ against 19.0% in Slovakia, while Slovakia has more children (15.6% under 15 vs 14.1%). People in Austria live 3.6 years longer on average (82.0 vs 78.4). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Austria ($62,930 vs $28,544).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Austria) and 2025 (Slovakia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
