Ethiopia has 2.5× the population of Myanmar: 135,472,051 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 80.6 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Myanmar is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 24.1%). Ethiopia is 1.4× as densely populated (114.0 vs 82.9 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Myanmar ($1,489 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
