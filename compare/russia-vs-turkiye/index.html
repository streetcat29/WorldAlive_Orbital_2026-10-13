Russia vs Türkiye

Russia has 1.7× the population of Türkiye: 143,513,328 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 57.6 million. Türkiye is growing faster (+0.42% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 360,651 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Türkiye would overtake Russia in about 97 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2122) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 17.0%). Türkiye is 12.6× as densely populated (110.9 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 4.0 years longer on average (77.4 vs 73.4).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Russia) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
