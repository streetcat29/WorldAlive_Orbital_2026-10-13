Serbia has about the same population as Bulgaria: 6,549,143 vs 6,433,302, a gap of 115,841. Bulgaria is shrinking more slowly (−0.13% vs −0.57% a year), adding about -8,114 people a year against −37,227. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Bulgaria would overtake Serbia in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Serbia is 1.3× as densely populated (78.8 vs 59.4 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Bulgaria ($20,328 vs $15,262).
