Nigeria has 12% more people than Brazil: 237,527,782 vs 212,812,405, a gap of 24.7 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +815,392. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 19.4%). Nigeria is 9.9× as densely populated (250.2 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Brazil live 21.4 years longer on average (76.0 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 8.8× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
