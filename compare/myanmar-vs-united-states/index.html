United States has 6.2× the population of Myanmar: 341,784,857 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 286.9 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against +1,785,717. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 17.1%). Myanmar is 2.3× as densely populated (82.9 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 11.8 years longer on average (78.9 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 60.5× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
