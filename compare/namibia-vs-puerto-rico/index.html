Puerto Rico has about the same population as Namibia: 3,184,835 vs 3,092,816, a gap of 92,019. Namibia is growing faster (+2.05% vs −0.55% a year), adding about 63,329 people a year against −17,637. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Namibia would overtake Puerto Rico in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Puerto Rico is the older society: 25.3% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Namibia, while Namibia has more children (36.7% under 15 vs 11.2%). Puerto Rico is 100.4× as densely populated (361.2 vs 3.6 people per km²). People in Puerto Rico live 14.4 years longer on average (81.9 vs 67.5). GDP per capita is 8.3× higher in Puerto Rico ($40,620 vs $4,876).
