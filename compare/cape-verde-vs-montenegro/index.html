Montenegro has 1.2× the population of Cape Verde: 623,129 vs 527,326, a gap of 95,803. Cape Verde is growing faster (+0.47% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 2,455 people a year against −396. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cape Verde would overtake Montenegro in about 32 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2057) — a projection, not a forecast. Montenegro is the older society: 18.3% aged 65+ against 7.2% in Cape Verde, while Cape Verde has more children (24.7% under 15 vs 17.9%). Cape Verde is 2.8× as densely populated (129.6 vs 46.4 people per km²). People in Montenegro live 1.7 years longer on average (77.9 vs 76.2). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Montenegro ($14,817 vs $5,796).
