Kenya has 11% more people than Sudan: 57,532,493 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 5.9 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +1.93% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +1,110,192. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Kenya in about 24 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2049) — a projection, not a forecast. Kenya is 3.6× as densely populated (95.3 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Sudan live 2.7 years longer on average (66.5 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Kenya ($2,363 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
