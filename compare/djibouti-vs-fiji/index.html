Djibouti has 1.3× the population of Fiji: 1,184,076 vs 933,154, a gap of 250,922. Djibouti is growing faster (+1.31% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 15,454 people a year against +4,380. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Fiji is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Djibouti, while Djibouti has more children (28.8% under 15 vs 26.8%). People in Fiji live 1.3 years longer on average (67.5 vs 66.2). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Fiji ($6,642 vs $3,906).
