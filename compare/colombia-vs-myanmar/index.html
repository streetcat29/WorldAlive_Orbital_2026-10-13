Myanmar has about the same population as Colombia: 54,850,648 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 1.4 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +351,682. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake Myanmar in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 20.0%). Myanmar is 1.8× as densely populated (82.9 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 10.8 years longer on average (77.9 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 5.8× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
