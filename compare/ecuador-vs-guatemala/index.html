Guatemala has about the same population as Ecuador: 18,687,881 vs 18,289,896, a gap of 397,985. Guatemala is growing faster (+1.52% vs +0.85% a year), adding about 283,664 people a year against +155,074. Ecuador is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Guatemala, while Guatemala has more children (31.0% under 15 vs 23.9%). Guatemala is 2.3× as densely populated (169.1 vs 72.4 people per km²). People in Ecuador live 4.8 years longer on average (77.6 vs 72.7).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ecuador) and 2025 (Guatemala); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
