Czechia has about the same population as Papua New Guinea: 10,886,878 vs 10,762,817, a gap of 124,061. Papua New Guinea is growing faster (+1.75% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 187,947 people a year against −18,135. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Papua New Guinea would overtake Czechia in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Czechia is the older society: 21.2% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Papua New Guinea, while Papua New Guinea has more children (33.1% under 15 vs 15.1%). Czechia is 6.1× as densely populated (140.8 vs 22.9 people per km²). People in Czechia live 13.7 years longer on average (80.0 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 11.9× higher in Czechia ($35,917 vs $3,020).
