Seychelles has 8% more people than Micronesia: 122,730 vs 113,683, a gap of 9,047. Seychelles is growing faster (+1.13% vs +0.46% a year), adding about 1,384 people a year against +524. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Seychelles is the older society: 8.7% aged 65+ against 6.2% in Micronesia, while Micronesia has more children (31.5% under 15 vs 19.8%). Seychelles is 1.6× as densely populated (260.4 vs 160.9 people per km²). People in Seychelles live 8.9 years longer on average (76.3 vs 67.4). GDP per capita is 4.4× higher in Seychelles ($19,449 vs $4,414).
