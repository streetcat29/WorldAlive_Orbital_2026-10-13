Niger has 5% more people than North Korea: 27,917,831 vs 26,571,036, a gap of 1.3 million. Niger is growing faster (+3.22% vs +0.27% a year), adding about 899,764 people a year against +72,311. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. North Korea is the older society: 12.7% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Niger, while Niger has more children (46.2% under 15 vs 18.9%). North Korea is 10.6× as densely populated (219.4 vs 20.7 people per km²). People in North Korea live 12.3 years longer on average (73.7 vs 61.4).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Niger) and 2025 (North Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
