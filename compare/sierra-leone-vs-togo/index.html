Sierra Leone has about the same population as Togo: 8,819,794 vs 8,591,626, a gap of 228,168. Togo is growing faster (+2.18% vs +2.04% a year), adding about 187,090 people a year against +179,588. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Togo would overtake Sierra Leone in about 19 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2044) — a projection, not a forecast. GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Togo ($1,384 vs $846).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sierra Leone) and 2025 (Togo); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
