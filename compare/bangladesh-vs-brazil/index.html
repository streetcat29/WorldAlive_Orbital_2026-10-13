Brazil has 1.2× the population of Bangladesh: 212,812,405 vs 175,686,899, a gap of 37.1 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +815,392. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Bangladesh would overtake Brazil in about 23 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2048) — a projection, not a forecast. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 19.4%). Bangladesh is 52.2× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Brazil live 1.1 years longer on average (76.0 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 4.1× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Brazil); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
