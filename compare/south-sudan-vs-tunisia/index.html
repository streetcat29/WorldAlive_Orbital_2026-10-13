Tunisia has about the same population as South Sudan: 12,348,573 vs 12,188,788, a gap of 159,785. South Sudan is growing faster (+2.03% vs +0.58% a year), adding about 247,884 people a year against +71,672. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, South Sudan would overtake Tunisia in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Tunisia is the older society: 9.9% aged 65+ against 3.1% in South Sudan, while South Sudan has more children (38.0% under 15 vs 23.6%). Tunisia is 4.3× as densely populated (78.5 vs 18.2 people per km²). People in Tunisia live 19.0 years longer on average (76.7 vs 57.7). GDP per capita is 4.3× higher in Tunisia ($4,657 vs $1,080).
