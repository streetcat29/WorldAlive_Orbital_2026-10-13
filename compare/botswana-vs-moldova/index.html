Botswana has 9% more people than Moldova: 2,562,122 vs 2,360,527, a gap of 201,595. Botswana is growing faster (+1.61% vs −1.75% a year), adding about 41,314 people a year against −41,414. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Moldova is the older society: 16.9% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Botswana, while Botswana has more children (32.0% under 15 vs 19.5%). Moldova is 19.5× as densely populated (85.4 vs 4.4 people per km²). People in Moldova live 2.0 years longer on average (71.3 vs 69.3).
