China has 19.6× the population of Thailand: 1,406,585,000 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 1.33 billion. Thailand is shrinking more slowly (−0.07% vs −0.17% a year), adding about -48,132 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 14.9% in China, while China has more children (15.4% under 15 vs 14.4%). People in China live 1.5 years longer on average (78.0 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in China ($13,862 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
