Belgium has 4% more people than Jordan: 11,941,781 vs 11,520,684, a gap of 421,097. Belgium is growing faster (+0.70% vs −0.28% a year), adding about 83,462 people a year against −32,147. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Belgium is the older society: 21.0% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Jordan, while Jordan has more children (30.2% under 15 vs 15.7%). Belgium is 3.0× as densely populated (386.3 vs 128.8 people per km²). People in Belgium live 4.3 years longer on average (82.3 vs 78.0). GDP per capita is 11.4× higher in Belgium ($60,750 vs $5,348).
