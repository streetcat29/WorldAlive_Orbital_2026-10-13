Curaçao has 1.3× the population of Seychelles: 156,263 vs 122,730, a gap of 33,533. Seychelles is growing faster (+1.13% vs +0.19% a year), adding about 1,384 people a year against +296. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Seychelles would overtake Curaçao in about 26 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2051) — a projection, not a forecast. Curaçao is the older society: 17.3% aged 65+ against 8.7% in Seychelles, while Seychelles has more children (19.8% under 15 vs 14.6%). Curaçao is 1.3× as densely populated (348.3 vs 260.4 people per km²).
