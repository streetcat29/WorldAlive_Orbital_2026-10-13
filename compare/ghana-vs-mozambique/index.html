Mozambique has about the same population as Ghana: 35,631,653 vs 35,064,272, a gap of 567,381. Mozambique is growing faster (+2.85% vs +1.83% a year), adding about 1,014,184 people a year against +642,712. Ghana is the older society: 3.8% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Mozambique, while Mozambique has more children (44.2% under 15 vs 35.4%). Ghana is 3.5× as densely populated (148.5 vs 42.8 people per km²). People in Ghana live 1.9 years longer on average (65.7 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 5.2× higher in Ghana ($3,257 vs $627).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ghana) and 2025 (Mozambique); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
