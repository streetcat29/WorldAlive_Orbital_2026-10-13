Saudi Arabia has 4% more people than Mozambique: 36,973,555 vs 35,631,653, a gap of 1.3 million. Saudi Arabia is growing faster (+4.63% vs +2.85% a year), adding about 1,712,320 people a year against +1,014,184. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Mozambique is 2.7× as densely populated (42.8 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Saudi Arabia live 15.2 years longer on average (79.0 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 55.1× higher in Saudi Arabia ($34,537 vs $627).
