Egypt has 1.3× the population of Iran: 118,365,995 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 25.9 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +853,875. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 22.0%). Egypt is 2.1× as densely populated (115.1 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 6.0 years longer on average (77.9 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
