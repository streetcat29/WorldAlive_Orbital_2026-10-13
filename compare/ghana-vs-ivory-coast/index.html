Ghana has 7% more people than Ivory Coast: 35,064,272 vs 32,711,547, a gap of 2.4 million. Ivory Coast is growing faster (+2.40% vs +1.83% a year), adding about 786,702 people a year against +642,712. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ivory Coast would overtake Ghana in about 12 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2037) — a projection, not a forecast. Ghana is the older society: 3.8% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Ivory Coast, while Ivory Coast has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 35.4%). Ghana is 1.5× as densely populated (148.5 vs 98.0 people per km²). People in Ghana live 3.6 years longer on average (65.7 vs 62.1).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ghana) and 2025 (Ivory Coast); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
