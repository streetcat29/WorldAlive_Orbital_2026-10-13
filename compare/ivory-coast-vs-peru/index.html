Peru has 6% more people than Ivory Coast: 34,576,665 vs 32,711,547, a gap of 1.9 million. Ivory Coast is growing faster (+2.40% vs +1.04% a year), adding about 786,702 people a year against +360,692. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ivory Coast would overtake Peru in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Peru is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Ivory Coast, while Ivory Coast has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 23.6%). Ivory Coast is 3.7× as densely populated (98.0 vs 26.4 people per km²). People in Peru live 15.8 years longer on average (77.9 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Peru ($9,684 vs $3,050).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ivory Coast) and 2025 (Peru); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
