Panama has 1.2× the population of Georgia: 4,571,189 vs 3,935,766, a gap of 635,423. Georgia is growing faster (+3.18% vs +1.22% a year), adding about 125,219 people a year against +55,953. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Georgia would overtake Panama in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Georgia is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 9.7% in Panama, while Panama has more children (24.5% under 15 vs 20.5%). People in Panama live 5.1 years longer on average (79.8 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Panama ($19,790 vs $9,692).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Georgia) and 2025 (Panama); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
