Morocco has 5% more people than Poland: 38,430,770 vs 36,435,861, a gap of 2.0 million. Morocco is growing faster (+0.91% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 351,197 people a year against −123,164. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Poland is the older society: 20.8% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Morocco, while Morocco has more children (25.2% under 15 vs 14.5%). Poland is 1.4× as densely populated (119.8 vs 84.5 people per km²). People in Poland live 2.9 years longer on average (78.4 vs 75.5). GDP per capita is 6.1× higher in Poland ($28,420 vs $4,672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Morocco) and 2025 (Poland); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
