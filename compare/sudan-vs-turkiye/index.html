Türkiye has 1.7× the population of Sudan: 85,878,556 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 34.2 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +360,651. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Türkiye in about 26 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2051) — a projection, not a forecast. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 21.0%). Türkiye is 4.1× as densely populated (110.9 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 10.9 years longer on average (77.4 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 16.0× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sudan) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
