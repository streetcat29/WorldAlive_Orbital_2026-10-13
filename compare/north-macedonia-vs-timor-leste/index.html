North Macedonia has 1.3× the population of Timor-Leste: 1,820,909 vs 1,418,517, a gap of 402,392. Timor-Leste is growing faster (+1.27% vs −0.19% a year), adding about 17,993 people a year against −3,447. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Timor-Leste would overtake North Macedonia in about 17 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2042) — a projection, not a forecast. North Macedonia is the older society: 18.5% aged 65+ against 5.2% in Timor-Leste, while Timor-Leste has more children (33.1% under 15 vs 16.5%). People in North Macedonia live 8.7 years longer on average (76.6 vs 67.9). GDP per capita is 7.8× higher in North Macedonia ($10,490 vs $1,341).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (North Macedonia) and 2025 (Timor-Leste); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
