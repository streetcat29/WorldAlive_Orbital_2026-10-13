Uruguay has 8% more people than Bosnia and Herzegovina: 3,384,688 vs 3,140,095, a gap of 244,593. Uruguay is shrinking more slowly (−0.06% vs −0.77% a year), adding about -1,899 people a year against −24,066. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bosnia and Herzegovina is the older society: 22.9% aged 65+ against 16.3% in Uruguay, while Uruguay has more children (17.9% under 15 vs 12.8%). Bosnia and Herzegovina is 3.2× as densely populated (62.2 vs 19.4 people per km²). GDP per capita is 2.4× higher in Uruguay ($25,216 vs $10,382).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bosnia and Herzegovina) and 2025 (Uruguay); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
