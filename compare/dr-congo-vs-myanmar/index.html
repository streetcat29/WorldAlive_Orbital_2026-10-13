DR Congo has 2.1× the population of Myanmar: 112,832,473 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 58.0 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Myanmar is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 24.1%). Myanmar is 1.8× as densely populated (82.9 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Myanmar live 5.0 years longer on average (67.1 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in Myanmar ($1,489 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
