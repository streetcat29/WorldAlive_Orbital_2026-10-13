Vietnam has 1.8× the population of Kenya: 101,598,527 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 44.1 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +612,685. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Vietnam in about 43 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2068) — a projection, not a forecast. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 22.9%). Vietnam is 3.4× as densely populated (320.2 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in Vietnam live 10.9 years longer on average (74.7 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
