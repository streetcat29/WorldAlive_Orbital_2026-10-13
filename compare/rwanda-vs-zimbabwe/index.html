Zimbabwe has 1.2× the population of Rwanda: 16,950,795 vs 14,569,341, a gap of 2.4 million. Rwanda is growing faster (+2.17% vs +1.88% a year), adding about 316,180 people a year against +319,413. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Rwanda would overtake Zimbabwe in about 53 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2078) — a projection, not a forecast. Rwanda is 13.4× as densely populated (565.6 vs 42.2 people per km²). People in Rwanda live 5.0 years longer on average (68.0 vs 63.1). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in Zimbabwe ($3,021 vs $1,124).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Rwanda) and 2025 (Zimbabwe); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
