Pakistan has 7% more people than Nigeria: 255,219,554 vs 237,527,782, a gap of 17.7 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +3,981,282. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Nigeria would overtake Pakistan in about 14 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2039) — a projection, not a forecast. Pakistan is the older society: 4.4% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 36.2%). People in Pakistan live 13.2 years longer on average (67.8 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Pakistan ($1,596 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nigeria) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
