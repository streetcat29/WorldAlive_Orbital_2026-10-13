Algeria has 8% more people than Afghanistan: 47,435,312 vs 43,844,111, a gap of 3.6 million. Afghanistan is growing faster (+2.77% vs +1.32% a year), adding about 1,213,252 people a year against +625,105. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Afghanistan would overtake Algeria in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Algeria is the older society: 6.8% aged 65+ against 2.4% in Afghanistan, while Afghanistan has more children (42.6% under 15 vs 29.9%). Afghanistan is 3.3× as densely populated (63.6 vs 19.4 people per km²). People in Algeria live 10.2 years longer on average (76.5 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 14.5× higher in Algeria ($6,051 vs $417).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Afghanistan) and 2025 (Algeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
