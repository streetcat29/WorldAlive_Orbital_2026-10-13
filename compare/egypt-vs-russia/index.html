Russia has 1.2× the population of Egypt: 143,513,328 vs 118,365,995, a gap of 25.1 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Egypt would overtake Russia in about 12 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2037) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 17.0%). Egypt is 13.1× as densely populated (115.1 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 1.6 years longer on average (73.4 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 5.7× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
