Austria has about the same population as Switzerland: 9,208,163 vs 9,092,436, a gap of 115,727. Switzerland is growing faster (+0.96% vs +0.33% a year), adding about 87,271 people a year against +30,231. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Switzerland would overtake Austria in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Switzerland is 2.0× as densely populated (225.0 vs 110.7 people per km²). People in Switzerland live 2.4 years longer on average (84.4 vs 82.0). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in Switzerland ($114,769 vs $62,930).
