Switzerland has 3% more people than Sierra Leone: 9,092,436 vs 8,819,794, a gap of 272,642. Sierra Leone is growing faster (+2.04% vs +0.96% a year), adding about 179,588 people a year against +87,271. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sierra Leone would overtake Switzerland in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Switzerland is the older society: 20.4% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Sierra Leone, while Sierra Leone has more children (37.7% under 15 vs 14.9%). Switzerland is 1.9× as densely populated (225.0 vs 117.2 people per km²). People in Switzerland live 22.4 years longer on average (84.4 vs 62.0). GDP per capita is 135.6× higher in Switzerland ($114,769 vs $846).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sierra Leone) and 2025 (Switzerland); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
