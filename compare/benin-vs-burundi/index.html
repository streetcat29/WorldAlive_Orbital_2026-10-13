Benin has about the same population as Burundi: 14,814,460 vs 14,390,003, a gap of 424,457. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+2.40% vs +2.41% a year). Burundi is 4.3× as densely populated (533.1 vs 125.1 people per km²). People in Burundi live 2.9 years longer on average (63.8 vs 61.0). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in Benin ($1,658 vs $234).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Benin) and 2025 (Burundi); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
