Austria has about the same population as Belarus: 9,208,163 vs 9,085,991, a gap of 122,172. Austria is growing faster (+0.33% vs −0.51% a year), adding about 30,231 people a year against −46,519. Austria is the older society: 21.1% aged 65+ against 18.3% in Belarus, while Belarus has more children (15.9% under 15 vs 14.1%). Austria is 2.4× as densely populated (110.7 vs 45.2 people per km²). People in Austria live 7.6 years longer on average (82.0 vs 74.4). GDP per capita is 6.1× higher in Austria ($62,930 vs $10,279).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Austria) and 2025 (Belarus); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
