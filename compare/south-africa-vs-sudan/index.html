South Africa has 1.3× the population of Sudan: 64,747,319 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 13.1 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +1.15% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +744,395. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake South Africa in about 18 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2043) — a projection, not a forecast. South Africa is the older society: 6.9% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 25.7%). South Africa is 1.9× as densely populated (52.1 vs 26.8 people per km²). GDP per capita is 5.7× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Africa) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
