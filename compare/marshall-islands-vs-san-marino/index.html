Marshall Islands has 6% more people than San Marino: 36,282 vs 34,109, a gap of 2,173. San Marino is growing faster (+0.39% vs −3.43% a year), adding about 132 people a year against −1,244. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, San Marino would overtake Marshall Islands in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. San Marino is the older society: 23.1% aged 65+ against 4.9% in Marshall Islands, while Marshall Islands has more children (33.9% under 15 vs 11.9%). San Marino is 2.6× as densely populated (564.3 vs 215.7 people per km²). People in San Marino live 18.7 years longer on average (85.8 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in San Marino ($59,871 vs $8,489).
