India has 10.8× the population of Ethiopia: 1,463,865,525 vs 135,472,051, a gap of 1.33 billion. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +12,987,174. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ethiopia would overtake India in about 143 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2168) — a projection, not a forecast. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 24.2%). India is 4.2× as densely populated (483.7 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in India live 4.6 years longer on average (72.2 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 2.9× higher in India ($2,702 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (India); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
