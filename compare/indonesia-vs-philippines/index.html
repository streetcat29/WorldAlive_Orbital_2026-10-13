Indonesia vs Philippines

Indonesia has 2.4× the population of Philippines: 285,721,236 vs 116,786,962, a gap of 168.9 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.78% vs +0.81% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Indonesia is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 24.2%). Philippines is 2.6× as densely populated (385.3 vs 148.6 people per km²). People in Indonesia live 1.3 years longer on average (71.3 vs 69.9).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Philippines); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
