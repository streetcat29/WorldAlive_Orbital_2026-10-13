Guyana has 1.2× the population of Luxembourg: 835,986 vs 686,970, a gap of 149,016. Luxembourg is growing faster (+1.46% vs +0.59% a year), adding about 10,031 people a year against +4,913. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Luxembourg would overtake Guyana in about 23 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2048) — a projection, not a forecast. Luxembourg is the older society: 15.8% aged 65+ against 7.0% in Guyana, while Guyana has more children (28.9% under 15 vs 15.7%). Luxembourg is 66.1× as densely populated (258.9 vs 3.9 people per km²). People in Luxembourg live 12.9 years longer on average (83.2 vs 70.3). GDP per capita is 4.5× higher in Luxembourg ($147,252 vs $32,414).
