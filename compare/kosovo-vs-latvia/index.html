Latvia has 1.2× the population of Kosovo: 1,847,785 vs 1,576,876, a gap of 270,909. Latvia is shrinking more slowly (−0.99% vs −1.10% a year), adding about -18,249 people a year against −17,381. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Latvia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 10.5% in Kosovo, while Kosovo has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 15.2%). People in Kosovo live 1.8 years longer on average (78.2 vs 76.4). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Latvia ($26,312 vs $7,899).
