Togo has 15% more people than Hong Kong: 8,591,626 vs 7,498,900, a gap of 1.1 million. Togo is growing faster (+2.18% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 187,090 people a year against −25,158. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Hong Kong is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Togo, while Togo has more children (40.4% under 15 vs 10.3%). Hong Kong is 47.5× as densely populated (7,177.2 vs 151.2 people per km²). People in Hong Kong live 22.5 years longer on average (85.4 vs 62.9). GDP per capita is 41.2× higher in Hong Kong ($56,983 vs $1,384).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Hong Kong) and 2025 (Togo); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
