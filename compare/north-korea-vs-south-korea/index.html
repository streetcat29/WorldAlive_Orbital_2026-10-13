South Korea has 1.9× the population of North Korea: 51,684,564 vs 26,571,036, a gap of 25.1 million. North Korea is growing faster (+0.27% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 72,311 people a year against −66,458. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, North Korea would overtake South Korea in about 166 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2191) — a projection, not a forecast. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 12.7% in North Korea, while North Korea has more children (18.9% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 2.4× as densely populated (529.8 vs 219.4 people per km²). People in South Korea live 9.9 years longer on average (83.6 vs 73.7).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (North Korea) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
