China has 24.4× the population of Kenya: 1,406,585,000 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 1.35 billion. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake China in about 152 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2177) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 1.6× as densely populated (150.3 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in China live 14.2 years longer on average (78.0 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 5.9× higher in China ($13,862 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
