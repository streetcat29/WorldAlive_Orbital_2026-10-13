Germany has 1.4× the population of Italy: 83,491,249 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 24.6 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (−0.03% vs −0.06% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 23.7% in Germany, while Germany has more children (13.9% under 15 vs 11.7%). People in Italy live 3.2 years longer on average (84.0 vs 80.8). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $43,309).
