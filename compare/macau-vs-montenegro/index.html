Macau has 10% more people than Montenegro: 685,900 vs 623,129, a gap of 62,771. Montenegro is shrinking more slowly (−0.06% vs −0.16% a year), adding about -396 people a year against −1,099. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Montenegro would overtake Macau in about 99 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2124) — a projection, not a forecast. People in Macau live 5.4 years longer on average (83.3 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 5.1× higher in Macau ($75,902 vs $14,817).
