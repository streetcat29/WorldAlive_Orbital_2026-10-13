Cyprus has about the same population as Estonia: 1,370,754 vs 1,366,475, a gap of 4,279. Cyprus is growing faster (+0.91% vs −0.43% a year), adding about 12,529 people a year against −5,853. Estonia is the older society: 21.8% aged 65+ against 14.9% in Cyprus, while Cyprus has more children (16.1% under 15 vs 15.3%). Cyprus is 4.5× as densely populated (145.6 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Cyprus live 2.5 years longer on average (81.8 vs 79.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cyprus) and 2025 (Estonia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
