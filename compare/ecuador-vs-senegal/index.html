Senegal has 4% more people than Ecuador: 18,931,966 vs 18,289,896, a gap of 642,070. Senegal is growing faster (+2.30% vs +0.85% a year), adding about 434,940 people a year against +155,074. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Ecuador is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Senegal, while Senegal has more children (37.7% under 15 vs 23.9%). People in Ecuador live 8.7 years longer on average (77.6 vs 68.9). GDP per capita is 3.6× higher in Ecuador ($7,125 vs $1,955).
