Malta has 10% more people than Cape Verde: 579,704 vs 527,326, a gap of 52,378. Malta is growing faster (+1.89% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 10,960 people a year against +2,455. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Malta is the older society: 20.5% aged 65+ against 7.2% in Cape Verde, while Cape Verde has more children (24.7% under 15 vs 12.9%). Malta is 13.3× as densely populated (1,727.3 vs 129.6 people per km²). People in Malta live 6.7 years longer on average (83.0 vs 76.2). GDP per capita is 8.3× higher in Malta ($47,907 vs $5,796).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cape Verde) and 2025 (Malta); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
