Colombia has 1.2× the population of Argentina: 53,425,635 vs 45,851,378, a gap of 7.6 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +155,482. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Argentina ($14,898 vs $8,562).
