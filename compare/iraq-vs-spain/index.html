Spain has 5% more people than Iraq: 49,355,143 vs 47,020,774, a gap of 2.3 million. Iraq is growing faster (+2.10% vs +1.03% a year), adding about 989,089 people a year against +508,918. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iraq would overtake Spain in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Spain is the older society: 21.6% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Iraq, while Iraq has more children (36.0% under 15 vs 12.6%). People in Spain live 11.5 years longer on average (83.9 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in Spain ($38,627 vs $5,410).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iraq) and 2025 (Spain); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
