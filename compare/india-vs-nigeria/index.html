India has 6.2× the population of Nigeria: 1,463,865,525 vs 237,527,782, a gap of 1.23 billion. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +12,987,174. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Nigeria would overtake India in about 155 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2180) — a projection, not a forecast. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 24.2%). India is 1.9× as densely populated (483.7 vs 250.2 people per km²). People in India live 17.6 years longer on average (72.2 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in India ($2,702 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
