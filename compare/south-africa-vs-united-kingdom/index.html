United Kingdom has 7% more people than South Africa: 69,487,000 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 4.7 million. South Africa is growing faster (+1.15% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 744,395 people a year against +206,306. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, South Africa would overtake United Kingdom in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 6.9% in South Africa, while South Africa has more children (25.7% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 5.4× as densely populated (283.2 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 15.1 years longer on average (81.4 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 8.7× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $6,598).
