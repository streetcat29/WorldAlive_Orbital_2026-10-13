Switzerland has about the same population as Belarus: 9,092,436 vs 9,085,991, a gap of 6,445. Switzerland is growing faster (+0.96% vs −0.51% a year), adding about 87,271 people a year against −46,519. Switzerland is the older society: 20.4% aged 65+ against 18.3% in Belarus, while Belarus has more children (15.9% under 15 vs 14.9%). Switzerland is 5.0× as densely populated (225.0 vs 45.2 people per km²). People in Switzerland live 10.0 years longer on average (84.4 vs 74.4). GDP per capita is 11.2× higher in Switzerland ($114,769 vs $10,279).
