Indonesia vs Nigeria

Indonesia has 1.2× the population of Nigeria: 285,721,236 vs 237,527,782, a gap of 48.2 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +2,242,079. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Nigeria would overtake Indonesia in about 14 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2039) — a projection, not a forecast. Indonesia is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 24.2%). Nigeria is 1.7× as densely populated (250.2 vs 148.6 people per km²). People in Indonesia live 16.7 years longer on average (71.3 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 4.1× higher in Indonesia ($5,060 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
