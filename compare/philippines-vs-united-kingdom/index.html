Philippines has 1.7× the population of United Kingdom: 116,786,962 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 47.3 million. Philippines is growing faster (+0.81% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 947,122 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 17.0%). Philippines is 1.4× as densely populated (385.3 vs 283.2 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 11.4 years longer on average (81.4 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 13.8× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $4,171).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Philippines) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
