China has 20.2× the population of United Kingdom: 1,406,585,000 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 1.34 billion. United Kingdom is growing faster (+0.30% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 206,306 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 14.9% in China. United Kingdom is 1.9× as densely populated (283.2 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 3.4 years longer on average (81.4 vs 78.0). GDP per capita is 4.2× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $13,862).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
