Nepal has 4% more people than Venezuela: 29,618,118 vs 28,516,896, a gap of 1.1 million. Venezuela is growing faster (+0.39% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 111,571 people a year against −32,918. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Venezuela would overtake Nepal in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Venezuela is the older society: 10.0% aged 65+ against 6.6% in Nepal, while Nepal has more children (28.1% under 15 vs 24.9%). Nepal is 6.5× as densely populated (207.1 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Venezuela live 2.0 years longer on average (72.7 vs 70.6). GDP per capita is 2.3× higher in Venezuela ($3,495 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nepal) and 2025 (Venezuela); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
