Uganda has 4% more people than Spain: 51,384,894 vs 49,355,143, a gap of 2.0 million. Uganda is growing faster (+2.70% vs +1.03% a year), adding about 1,388,391 people a year against +508,918. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Spain is the older society: 21.6% aged 65+ against 2.2% in Uganda, while Uganda has more children (43.1% under 15 vs 12.6%). Uganda is 2.5× as densely populated (242.7 vs 96.8 people per km²). People in Spain live 15.4 years longer on average (83.9 vs 68.5). GDP per capita is 32.0× higher in Spain ($38,627 vs $1,206).
