India has 8.3× the population of Bangladesh: 1,463,865,525 vs 175,686,899, a gap of 1.29 billion. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +12,987,174. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Bangladesh is 2.7× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 483.7 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 2.7 years longer on average (74.9 vs 72.2).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (India); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
