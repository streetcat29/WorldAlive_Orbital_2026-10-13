Peru has 1.2× the population of Cameroon: 34,576,665 vs 29,879,337, a gap of 4.7 million. Cameroon is growing faster (+2.56% vs +1.04% a year), adding about 765,311 people a year against +360,692. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cameroon would overtake Peru in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Peru is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 2.8% in Cameroon, while Cameroon has more children (41.1% under 15 vs 23.6%). Cameroon is 2.3× as densely populated (60.0 vs 26.4 people per km²). People in Peru live 14.0 years longer on average (77.9 vs 64.0). GDP per capita is 4.9× higher in Peru ($9,684 vs $1,972).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cameroon) and 2025 (Peru); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
