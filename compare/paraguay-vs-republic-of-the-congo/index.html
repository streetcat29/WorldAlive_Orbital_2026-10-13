Paraguay has 8% more people than Republic of the Congo: 7,013,078 vs 6,484,437, a gap of 528,641. Republic of the Congo is growing faster (+2.36% vs +1.20% a year), adding about 153,273 people a year against +84,431. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Republic of the Congo would overtake Paraguay in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Paraguay is the older society: 6.7% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Republic of the Congo, while Republic of the Congo has more children (39.9% under 15 vs 28.4%). People in Paraguay live 8.0 years longer on average (74.0 vs 66.0). GDP per capita is 2.8× higher in Paraguay ($7,027 vs $2,515).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Paraguay) and 2025 (Republic of the Congo); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
