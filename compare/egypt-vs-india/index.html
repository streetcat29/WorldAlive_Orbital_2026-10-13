India has 12.4× the population of Egypt: 1,463,865,525 vs 118,365,995, a gap of 1.35 billion. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +12,987,174. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 24.2%). India is 4.2× as densely populated (483.7 vs 115.1 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (India); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
