Ethiopia has 2.5× the population of Colombia: 135,472,051 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 82.0 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +1.01% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +542,012. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 20.0%). Ethiopia is 2.4× as densely populated (114.0 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 10.3 years longer on average (77.9 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 9.2× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Ethiopia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
