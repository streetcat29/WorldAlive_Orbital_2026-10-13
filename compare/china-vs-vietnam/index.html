China has 13.8× the population of Vietnam: 1,406,585,000 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 1.30 billion. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 15.4%). Vietnam is 2.1× as densely populated (320.2 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in China live 3.3 years longer on average (78.0 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in China ($13,862 vs $5,066).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
