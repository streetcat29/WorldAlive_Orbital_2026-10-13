Russia has 1.6× the population of Iran: 143,513,328 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 51.1 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iran would overtake Russia in about 43 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2068) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 17.0%). Iran is 6.4× as densely populated (55.8 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 4.4 years longer on average (77.9 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 4.5× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
