Colombia has 1.9× the population of Venezuela: 53,425,635 vs 28,516,896, a gap of 24.9 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.39% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +111,571. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Colombia is 1.5× as densely populated (47.2 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Colombia live 5.2 years longer on average (77.9 vs 72.7). GDP per capita is 2.4× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $3,495).
