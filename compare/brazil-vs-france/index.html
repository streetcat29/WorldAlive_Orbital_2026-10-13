Brazil has 3.1× the population of France: 212,812,405 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 144.1 million. Brazil is growing faster (+0.38% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 815,392 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 11.5% in Brazil, while Brazil has more children (19.4% under 15 vs 16.2%). France is 5.0× as densely populated (126.9 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in France live 7.0 years longer on average (83.0 vs 76.0). GDP per capita is 4.6× higher in France ($48,986 vs $10,713).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (France); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
