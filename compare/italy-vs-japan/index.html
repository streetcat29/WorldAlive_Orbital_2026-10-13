Japan has 2.1× the population of Italy: 123,366,734 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 64.5 million. Italy is shrinking more slowly (−0.06% vs −0.49% a year), adding about -37,036 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Italy would overtake Japan in about 172 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2197) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 25.1% in Italy, while Italy has more children (11.7% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 1.7× as densely populated (341.5 vs 199.5 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
