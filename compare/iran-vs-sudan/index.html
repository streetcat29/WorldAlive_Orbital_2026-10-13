Iran has 1.8× the population of Sudan: 92,417,681 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 40.8 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +853,875. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Iran in about 40 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2065) — a projection, not a forecast. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 22.0%). Iran is 2.1× as densely populated (55.8 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 11.3 years longer on average (77.9 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 3.4× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
