Jamaica has 11% more people than Botswana: 2,837,077 vs 2,562,122, a gap of 274,955. Botswana is growing faster (+1.61% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 41,314 people a year against −2,097. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Botswana would overtake Jamaica in about 6 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2031) — a projection, not a forecast. Jamaica is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Botswana, while Botswana has more children (32.0% under 15 vs 18.3%). Jamaica is 59.9× as densely populated (262.2 vs 4.4 people per km²). People in Jamaica live 2.3 years longer on average (71.6 vs 69.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Botswana) and 2025 (Jamaica); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
