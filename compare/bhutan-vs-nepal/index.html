Nepal has 37.2× the population of Bhutan: 29,618,118 vs 796,682, a gap of 28.8 million. Bhutan is growing faster (+0.65% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 5,175 people a year against −32,918. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Nepal is 10.0× as densely populated (207.1 vs 20.6 people per km²). People in Bhutan live 2.6 years longer on average (73.3 vs 70.6). GDP per capita is 2.9× higher in Bhutan ($4,493 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bhutan) and 2025 (Nepal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
