Bosnia and Herzegovina has about the same population as Namibia: 3,140,095 vs 3,092,816, a gap of 47,279. Namibia is growing faster (+2.05% vs −0.77% a year), adding about 63,329 people a year against −24,066. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Namibia would overtake Bosnia and Herzegovina in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Bosnia and Herzegovina is the older society: 22.9% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Namibia, while Namibia has more children (36.7% under 15 vs 12.8%). Bosnia and Herzegovina is 17.3× as densely populated (62.2 vs 3.6 people per km²). People in Bosnia and Herzegovina live 10.5 years longer on average (78.0 vs 67.5). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Bosnia and Herzegovina ($10,382 vs $4,876).
