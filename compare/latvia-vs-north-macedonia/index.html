Latvia has about the same population as North Macedonia: 1,847,785 vs 1,820,909, a gap of 26,876. North Macedonia is shrinking more slowly (−0.19% vs −0.99% a year), adding about -3,447 people a year against −18,249. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, North Macedonia would overtake Latvia in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Latvia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 18.5% in North Macedonia, while North Macedonia has more children (16.5% under 15 vs 15.2%). North Macedonia is 2.4× as densely populated (72.5 vs 30.3 people per km²). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Latvia ($26,312 vs $10,490).
