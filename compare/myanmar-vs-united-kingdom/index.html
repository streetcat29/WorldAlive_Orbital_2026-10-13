United Kingdom has 1.3× the population of Myanmar: 69,487,000 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 14.6 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against +206,306. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Myanmar would overtake United Kingdom in about 69 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2094) — a projection, not a forecast. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 3.4× as densely populated (283.2 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 14.3 years longer on average (81.4 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 38.7× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
