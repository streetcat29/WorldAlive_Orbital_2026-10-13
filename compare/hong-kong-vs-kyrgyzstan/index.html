Hong Kong has about the same population as Kyrgyzstan: 7,498,900 vs 7,343,064, a gap of 155,836. Kyrgyzstan is growing faster (+1.66% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 122,207 people a year against −25,158. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kyrgyzstan would overtake Hong Kong in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Hong Kong is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 6.0% in Kyrgyzstan, while Kyrgyzstan has more children (31.9% under 15 vs 10.3%). Hong Kong is 193.9× as densely populated (7,177.2 vs 37.0 people per km²). People in Hong Kong live 13.0 years longer on average (85.4 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 18.5× higher in Hong Kong ($56,983 vs $3,081).
