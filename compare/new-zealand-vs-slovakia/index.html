Slovakia has about the same population as New Zealand: 5,413,813 vs 5,324,700, a gap of 89,113. New Zealand is growing faster (+0.65% vs −0.15% a year), adding about 34,814 people a year against −8,250. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, New Zealand would overtake Slovakia in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Slovakia is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 17.6% in New Zealand, while New Zealand has more children (17.9% under 15 vs 15.6%). Slovakia is 5.7× as densely populated (112.9 vs 19.7 people per km²). People in New Zealand live 3.6 years longer on average (82.0 vs 78.4). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in New Zealand ($49,591 vs $28,544).
