United States has 4.0× the population of Türkiye: 341,784,857 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 255.9 million. United States is growing faster (+0.52% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 1,785,717 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 17.1%). Türkiye is 3.0× as densely populated (110.9 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 1.5 years longer on average (78.9 vs 77.4). GDP per capita is 4.8× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $18,599).
