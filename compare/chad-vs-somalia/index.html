Chad has 7% more people than Somalia: 21,003,705 vs 19,654,739, a gap of 1.3 million. Chad is growing faster (+3.41% vs +3.34% a year), adding about 716,671 people a year against +656,429. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Somalia is 1.9× as densely populated (29.3 vs 15.3 people per km²). People in Somalia live 3.7 years longer on average (59.0 vs 55.2). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Chad ($1,022 vs $661).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chad) and 2025 (Somalia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
