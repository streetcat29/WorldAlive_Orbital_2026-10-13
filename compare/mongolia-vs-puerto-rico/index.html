Mongolia has 12% more people than Puerto Rico: 3,568,978 vs 3,184,835, a gap of 384,143. Mongolia is growing faster (+1.25% vs −0.55% a year), adding about 44,466 people a year against −17,637. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Puerto Rico is the older society: 25.3% aged 65+ against 5.4% in Mongolia, while Mongolia has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 11.2%). Puerto Rico is 161.7× as densely populated (361.2 vs 2.2 people per km²). People in Puerto Rico live 9.5 years longer on average (81.9 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 5.7× higher in Puerto Rico ($40,620 vs $7,108).
