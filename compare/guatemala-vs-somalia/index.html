Somalia has 5% more people than Guatemala: 19,654,739 vs 18,687,881, a gap of 966,858. Somalia is growing faster (+3.34% vs +1.52% a year), adding about 656,429 people a year against +283,664. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Guatemala is the older society: 5.0% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Somalia, while Somalia has more children (46.6% under 15 vs 31.0%). Guatemala is 5.8× as densely populated (169.1 vs 29.3 people per km²). People in Guatemala live 13.8 years longer on average (72.7 vs 59.0). GDP per capita is 10.0× higher in Guatemala ($6,598 vs $661).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Guatemala) and 2025 (Somalia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
