Moldova has 5% more people than Guinea-Bissau: 2,360,527 vs 2,249,515, a gap of 111,012. Guinea-Bissau is growing faster (+2.16% vs −1.75% a year), adding about 48,686 people a year against −41,414. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guinea-Bissau would overtake Moldova in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Moldova is the older society: 16.9% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Guinea-Bissau, while Guinea-Bissau has more children (38.3% under 15 vs 19.5%). People in Moldova live 7.1 years longer on average (71.3 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 7.7× higher in Moldova ($8,622 vs $1,124).
