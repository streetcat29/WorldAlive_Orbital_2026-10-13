South Korea has 5% more people than Spain: 51,684,564 vs 49,355,143, a gap of 2.3 million. Spain is growing faster (+1.03% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 508,918 people a year against −66,458. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Spain would overtake South Korea in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Spain is the older society: 21.6% aged 65+ against 20.3% in South Korea. South Korea is 5.5× as densely populated (529.8 vs 96.8 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Korea) and 2025 (Spain); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
