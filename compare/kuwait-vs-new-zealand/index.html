New Zealand has 9% more people than Kuwait: 5,324,700 vs 4,865,298, a gap of 459,402. New Zealand is growing faster (+0.65% vs −0.65% a year), adding about 34,814 people a year against −31,860. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. New Zealand is the older society: 17.6% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Kuwait, while Kuwait has more children (18.0% under 15 vs 17.9%). Kuwait is 13.8× as densely populated (272.4 vs 19.7 people per km²). People in Kuwait live 2.6 years longer on average (84.6 vs 82.0). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in New Zealand ($49,591 vs $32,312).
