Bahamas has 1.2× the population of Vanuatu: 403,033 vs 335,169, a gap of 67,864. Vanuatu is growing faster (+2.23% vs +0.44% a year), adding about 7,475 people a year against +1,754. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Vanuatu would overtake Bahamas in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Bahamas is the older society: 12.1% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Vanuatu, while Vanuatu has more children (38.0% under 15 vs 17.5%). Bahamas is 1.5× as densely populated (39.9 vs 26.3 people per km²). People in Bahamas live 3.1 years longer on average (74.7 vs 71.7). GDP per capita is 9.8× higher in Bahamas ($39,455 vs $4,039).
