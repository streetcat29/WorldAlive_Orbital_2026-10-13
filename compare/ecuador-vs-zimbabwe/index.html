Ecuador has 8% more people than Zimbabwe: 18,289,896 vs 16,950,795, a gap of 1.3 million. Zimbabwe is growing faster (+1.88% vs +0.85% a year), adding about 319,413 people a year against +155,074. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Zimbabwe would overtake Ecuador in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Ecuador is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Zimbabwe, while Zimbabwe has more children (40.3% under 15 vs 23.9%). Ecuador is 1.7× as densely populated (72.4 vs 42.2 people per km²). People in Ecuador live 14.5 years longer on average (77.6 vs 63.1). GDP per capita is 2.4× higher in Ecuador ($7,125 vs $3,021).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ecuador) and 2025 (Zimbabwe); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
