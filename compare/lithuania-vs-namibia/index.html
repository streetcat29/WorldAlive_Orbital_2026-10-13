Namibia has 7% more people than Lithuania: 3,092,816 vs 2,888,774, a gap of 204,042. Namibia is growing faster (+2.05% vs +0.02% a year), adding about 63,329 people a year against +496. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Lithuania is the older society: 20.7% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Namibia, while Namibia has more children (36.7% under 15 vs 14.5%). Lithuania is 12.7× as densely populated (45.9 vs 3.6 people per km²). People in Lithuania live 9.7 years longer on average (77.2 vs 67.5). GDP per capita is 6.8× higher in Lithuania ($32,959 vs $4,876).
