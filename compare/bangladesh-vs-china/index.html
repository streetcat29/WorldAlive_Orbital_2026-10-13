China has 8.0× the population of Bangladesh: 1,406,585,000 vs 175,686,899, a gap of 1.23 billion. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Bangladesh would overtake China in about 150 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2175) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 15.4%). Bangladesh is 8.8× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in China live 3.1 years longer on average (78.0 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 5.3× higher in China ($13,862 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (China); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
