Togo has 13% more people than Turkmenistan: 8,591,626 vs 7,618,847, a gap of 972,779. Togo is growing faster (+2.18% vs +1.65% a year), adding about 187,090 people a year against +125,375. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Turkmenistan is the older society: 4.8% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Togo, while Togo has more children (40.4% under 15 vs 31.2%). Togo is 9.6× as densely populated (151.2 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Turkmenistan live 7.3 years longer on average (70.2 vs 62.9). GDP per capita is 4.7× higher in Turkmenistan ($6,540 vs $1,384).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Togo) and 2025 (Turkmenistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
