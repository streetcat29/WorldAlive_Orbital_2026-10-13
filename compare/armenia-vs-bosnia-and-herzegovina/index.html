Bosnia and Herzegovina has about the same population as Armenia: 3,140,095 vs 3,086,700, a gap of 53,395. Armenia is growing faster (+1.74% vs −0.77% a year), adding about 53,664 people a year against −24,066. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Armenia would overtake Bosnia and Herzegovina in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Bosnia and Herzegovina is the older society: 22.9% aged 65+ against 14.4% in Armenia, while Armenia has more children (19.0% under 15 vs 12.8%). Armenia is 1.7× as densely populated (105.1 vs 62.2 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Armenia) and 2025 (Bosnia and Herzegovina); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
