Brazil has 2.3× the population of Iran: 212,812,405 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 120.4 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against +815,392. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iran would overtake Brazil in about 154 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2179) — a projection, not a forecast. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 19.4%). Iran is 2.2× as densely populated (55.8 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Iran live 1.8 years longer on average (77.9 vs 76.0). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
