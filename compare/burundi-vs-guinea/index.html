Guinea has 5% more people than Burundi: 15,099,727 vs 14,390,003, a gap of 709,724. Burundi is growing faster (+2.41% vs +2.31% a year), adding about 346,352 people a year against +348,943. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Burundi would overtake Guinea in about 50 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2075) — a projection, not a forecast. Burundi is 9.1× as densely populated (533.1 vs 58.6 people per km²). People in Burundi live 2.9 years longer on average (63.8 vs 60.9). GDP per capita is 8.0× higher in Guinea ($1,877 vs $234).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Burundi) and 2025 (Guinea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
