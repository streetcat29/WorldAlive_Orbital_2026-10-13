United States has 6.4× the population of Colombia: 341,784,857 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 288.4 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +1,785,717. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 17.1%). GDP per capita is 10.5× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $8,562).
