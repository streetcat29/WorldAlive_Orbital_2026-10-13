Philippines has 2.2× the population of Colombia: 116,786,962 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 63.4 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.81% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +947,122. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 20.0%). Philippines is 8.2× as densely populated (385.3 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 8.0 years longer on average (77.9 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $4,171).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Philippines); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
