Egypt has 2.7× the population of Afghanistan: 118,365,995 vs 43,844,111, a gap of 74.5 million. Afghanistan is growing faster (+2.77% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 1,213,252 people a year against +1,841,995. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Afghanistan would overtake Egypt in about 82 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2107) — a projection, not a forecast. Egypt is the older society: 5.3% aged 65+ against 2.4% in Afghanistan, while Afghanistan has more children (42.6% under 15 vs 31.6%). Egypt is 1.8× as densely populated (115.1 vs 63.6 people per km²). People in Egypt live 5.5 years longer on average (71.8 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 7.4× higher in Egypt ($3,086 vs $417).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Afghanistan) and 2025 (Egypt); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
