Myanmar has 6% more people than South Korea: 54,850,648 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 3.2 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 6.4× as densely populated (529.8 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in South Korea live 16.5 years longer on average (83.6 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 24.3× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
