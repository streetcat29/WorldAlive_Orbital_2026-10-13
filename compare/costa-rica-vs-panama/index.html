Costa Rica has 13% more people than Panama: 5,152,950 vs 4,571,189, a gap of 581,761. Panama is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.45% a year), adding about 55,953 people a year against +23,092. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Panama would overtake Costa Rica in about 15 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2040) — a projection, not a forecast. Costa Rica is the older society: 12.8% aged 65+ against 9.7% in Panama, while Panama has more children (24.5% under 15 vs 18.3%). Costa Rica is 1.7× as densely populated (100.0 vs 60.1 people per km²). People in Costa Rica live 1.2 years longer on average (81.0 vs 79.8).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Costa Rica) and 2025 (Panama); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
