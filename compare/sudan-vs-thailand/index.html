Thailand has 1.4× the population of Sudan: 71,619,863 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 20.0 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against −48,132. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Thailand in about 13 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2038) — a projection, not a forecast. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 5.2× as densely populated (140.3 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Thailand live 10.0 years longer on average (76.6 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 6.9× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sudan) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
