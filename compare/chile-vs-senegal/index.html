Chile has 5% more people than Senegal: 19,859,921 vs 18,931,966, a gap of 927,955. Senegal is growing faster (+2.30% vs +0.48% a year), adding about 434,940 people a year against +95,379. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Senegal would overtake Chile in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Chile is the older society: 14.6% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Senegal, while Senegal has more children (37.7% under 15 vs 16.5%). Senegal is 3.5× as densely populated (93.9 vs 26.5 people per km²). People in Chile live 12.4 years longer on average (81.4 vs 68.9). GDP per capita is 9.2× higher in Chile ($17,995 vs $1,955).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chile) and 2025 (Senegal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
