Burundi has 1.2× the population of Tunisia: 14,390,003 vs 12,348,573, a gap of 2.0 million. Burundi is growing faster (+2.41% vs +0.58% a year), adding about 346,352 people a year against +71,672. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Tunisia is the older society: 9.9% aged 65+ against 2.5% in Burundi, while Burundi has more children (44.0% under 15 vs 23.6%). Burundi is 6.8× as densely populated (533.1 vs 78.5 people per km²). People in Tunisia live 12.9 years longer on average (76.7 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 19.9× higher in Tunisia ($4,657 vs $234).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Burundi) and 2025 (Tunisia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
