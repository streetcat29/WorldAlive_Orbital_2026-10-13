Ireland has about the same population as Slovakia: 5,484,367 vs 5,413,813, a gap of 70,554. Ireland is growing faster (+1.63% vs −0.15% a year), adding about 89,300 people a year against −8,250. Slovakia is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 16.2% in Ireland, while Ireland has more children (18.1% under 15 vs 15.6%). Slovakia is 1.5× as densely populated (112.9 vs 77.1 people per km²). People in Ireland live 4.6 years longer on average (83.0 vs 78.4). GDP per capita is 4.6× higher in Ireland ($131,592 vs $28,544).
