Mongolia has 5% more people than Uruguay: 3,568,978 vs 3,384,688, a gap of 184,290. Mongolia is growing faster (+1.25% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 44,466 people a year against −1,899. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Uruguay is the older society: 16.3% aged 65+ against 5.4% in Mongolia, while Mongolia has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 17.9%). Uruguay is 8.7× as densely populated (19.4 vs 2.2 people per km²). People in Uruguay live 5.9 years longer on average (78.3 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in Uruguay ($25,216 vs $7,108).
