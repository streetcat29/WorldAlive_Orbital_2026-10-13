Germany has 1.5× the population of Myanmar: 83,491,249 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 28.6 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against −25,340. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Myanmar would overtake Germany in about 63 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2088) — a projection, not a forecast. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 13.9%). Germany is 2.9× as densely populated (238.4 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Germany live 13.7 years longer on average (80.8 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 40.6× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Germany) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
