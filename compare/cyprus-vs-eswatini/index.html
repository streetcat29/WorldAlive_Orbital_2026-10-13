Cyprus has 9% more people than Eswatini: 1,370,754 vs 1,256,174, a gap of 114,580. Eswatini is growing faster (+1.07% vs +0.91% a year), adding about 13,423 people a year against +12,529. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Eswatini would overtake Cyprus in about 56 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2081) — a projection, not a forecast. Cyprus is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 4.3% in Eswatini, while Eswatini has more children (33.0% under 15 vs 16.1%). Cyprus is 2.0× as densely populated (145.6 vs 71.5 people per km²). People in Cyprus live 17.6 years longer on average (81.8 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 10.2× higher in Cyprus ($41,783 vs $4,108).
