Peru has 1.2× the population of Venezuela: 34,576,665 vs 28,516,896, a gap of 6.1 million. Peru is growing faster (+1.04% vs +0.39% a year), adding about 360,692 people a year against +111,571. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. People in Peru live 5.3 years longer on average (77.9 vs 72.7). GDP per capita is 2.8× higher in Peru ($9,684 vs $3,495).
