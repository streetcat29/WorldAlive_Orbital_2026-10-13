Estonia has 9% more people than Eswatini: 1,366,475 vs 1,256,174, a gap of 110,301. Eswatini is growing faster (+1.07% vs −0.43% a year), adding about 13,423 people a year against −5,853. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Eswatini would overtake Estonia in about 6 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2031) — a projection, not a forecast. Estonia is the older society: 21.8% aged 65+ against 4.3% in Eswatini, while Eswatini has more children (33.0% under 15 vs 15.3%). Eswatini is 2.2× as densely populated (71.5 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Estonia live 15.0 years longer on average (79.3 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 8.4× higher in Estonia ($34,418 vs $4,108).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Estonia) and 2025 (Eswatini); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
