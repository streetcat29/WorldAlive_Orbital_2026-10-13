Honduras has about the same population as Portugal: 11,005,850 vs 10,804,871, a gap of 200,979. Honduras is growing faster (+1.65% vs +1.03% a year), adding about 181,638 people a year against +110,756. Portugal is the older society: 24.9% aged 65+ against 4.5% in Honduras, while Honduras has more children (30.3% under 15 vs 12.7%). People in Portugal live 9.3 years longer on average (82.4 vs 73.0). GDP per capita is 8.9× higher in Portugal ($32,082 vs $3,598).
