Pakistan has 3.7× the population of United Kingdom: 255,219,554 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 185.7 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 17.0%). People in United Kingdom live 13.6 years longer on average (81.4 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 36.1× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $1,596).
