Italy has 14% more people than South Korea: 58,915,656 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 7.2 million. Italy is shrinking more slowly (−0.06% vs −0.13% a year), adding about -37,036 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 20.3% in South Korea. South Korea is 2.7× as densely populated (529.8 vs 199.5 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
