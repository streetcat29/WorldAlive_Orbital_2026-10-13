Nigeria has 4.1× the population of Kenya: 237,527,782 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 180.0 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +1.93% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +1,110,192. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Nigeria is 2.6× as densely populated (250.2 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in Kenya live 9.2 years longer on average (63.8 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Kenya ($2,363 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
