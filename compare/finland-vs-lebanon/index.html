Lebanon has 4% more people than Finland: 5,849,421 vs 5,646,436, a gap of 202,985. Lebanon is growing faster (+0.75% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 43,621 people a year against +26,587. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Finland is the older society: 24.2% aged 65+ against 10.4% in Lebanon, while Lebanon has more children (25.6% under 15 vs 14.3%). Lebanon is 30.7× as densely populated (564.4 vs 18.4 people per km²). People in Finland live 4.4 years longer on average (82.3 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 12.6× higher in Finland ($56,149 vs $4,473).
