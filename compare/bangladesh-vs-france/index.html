Bangladesh has 2.6× the population of France: 175,686,899 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 107.0 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 16.2%). Bangladesh is 10.4× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 126.9 people per km²). People in France live 8.1 years longer on average (83.0 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 18.9× higher in France ($48,986 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (France); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
