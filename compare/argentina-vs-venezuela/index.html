Argentina has 1.6× the population of Venezuela: 45,851,378 vs 28,516,896, a gap of 17.3 million. Venezuela is growing faster (+0.39% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 111,571 people a year against +155,482. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Argentina is the older society: 12.6% aged 65+ against 10.0% in Venezuela, while Venezuela has more children (24.9% under 15 vs 21.0%). Venezuela is 1.9× as densely populated (32.1 vs 16.6 people per km²). People in Argentina live 4.9 years longer on average (77.5 vs 72.7). GDP per capita is 4.3× higher in Argentina ($14,898 vs $3,495).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Argentina) and 2025 (Venezuela); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
