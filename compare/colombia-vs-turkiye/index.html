Türkiye has 1.6× the population of Colombia: 85,878,556 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 32.5 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +360,651. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake Türkiye in about 80 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2105) — a projection, not a forecast. Türkiye is 2.4× as densely populated (110.9 vs 47.2 people per km²). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $8,562).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
