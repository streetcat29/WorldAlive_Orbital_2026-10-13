Lebanon has about the same population as Liberia: 5,849,421 vs 5,731,206, a gap of 118,215. Liberia is growing faster (+2.09% vs +0.75% a year), adding about 119,629 people a year against +43,621. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Liberia would overtake Lebanon in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Lebanon is the older society: 10.4% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Liberia, while Liberia has more children (39.1% under 15 vs 25.6%). Lebanon is 9.9× as densely populated (564.4 vs 57.0 people per km²). People in Lebanon live 15.6 years longer on average (77.9 vs 62.3). GDP per capita is 4.9× higher in Lebanon ($4,473 vs $915).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Lebanon) and 2025 (Liberia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
