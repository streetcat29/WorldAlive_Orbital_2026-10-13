DR Congo has 1.9× the population of Italy: 112,832,473 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 53.9 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 4.3× as densely populated (199.5 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Italy live 21.9 years longer on average (84.0 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 53.7× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Italy); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
