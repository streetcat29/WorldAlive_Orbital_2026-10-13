Germany has 1.6× the population of South Korea: 83,491,249 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 31.8 million. Germany is shrinking more slowly (−0.03% vs −0.13% a year), adding about -25,340 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 20.3% in South Korea. South Korea is 2.2× as densely populated (529.8 vs 238.4 people per km²). People in South Korea live 2.8 years longer on average (83.6 vs 80.8). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $36,227).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Germany) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
