Iceland has 1.4× the population of Barbados: 392,404 vs 282,623, a gap of 109,781. Iceland is growing faster (+1.51% vs +0.06% a year), adding about 5,943 people a year against +156. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Barbados is the older society: 17.1% aged 65+ against 16.0% in Iceland, while Iceland has more children (17.5% under 15 vs 17.1%). Barbados is 171.7× as densely populated (656.6 vs 3.8 people per km²). People in Iceland live 6.5 years longer on average (82.8 vs 76.3). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in Iceland ($98,323 vs $28,365).
