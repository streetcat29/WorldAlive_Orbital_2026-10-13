Tanzania has 1.4× the population of Sudan: 70,545,865 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 18.9 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +2.38% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +1,227,656. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Tanzania is 2.8× as densely populated (75.2 vs 26.8 people per km²).
