United States has 2.8× the population of Japan: 341,784,857 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 218.4 million. United States is growing faster (+0.52% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 1,785,717 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 18.4% in United States, while United States has more children (17.1% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 9.3× as densely populated (341.5 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in Japan live 5.1 years longer on average (84.0 vs 78.9). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $35,951).
