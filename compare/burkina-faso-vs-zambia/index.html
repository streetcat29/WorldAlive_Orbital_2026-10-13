Burkina Faso has 10% more people than Zambia: 24,074,580 vs 21,913,874, a gap of 2.2 million. Zambia is growing faster (+2.77% vs +2.21% a year), adding about 607,255 people a year against +531,626. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Zambia would overtake Burkina Faso in about 17 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2042) — a projection, not a forecast. Burkina Faso is 3.0× as densely populated (84.2 vs 27.9 people per km²). People in Zambia live 5.2 years longer on average (66.5 vs 61.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Burkina Faso) and 2025 (Zambia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
