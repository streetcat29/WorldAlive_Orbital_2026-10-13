Trinidad and Tobago has 10% more people than Mauritius: 1,367,764 vs 1,243,741, a gap of 124,023. Trinidad and Tobago is shrinking more slowly (−0.04% vs −0.16% a year), adding about -569 people a year against −2,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Mauritius is the older society: 14.1% aged 65+ against 12.9% in Trinidad and Tobago, while Trinidad and Tobago has more children (17.5% under 15 vs 14.5%). Mauritius is 2.3× as densely populated (625.3 vs 266.6 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Trinidad and Tobago ($18,967 vs $12,991).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mauritius) and 2025 (Trinidad and Tobago); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
