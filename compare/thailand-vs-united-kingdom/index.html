Thailand has 3% more people than United Kingdom: 71,619,863 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 2.1 million. United Kingdom is growing faster (+0.30% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 206,306 people a year against −48,132. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, United Kingdom would overtake Thailand in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 16.0% in Thailand. United Kingdom is 2.0× as densely populated (283.2 vs 140.3 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 4.8 years longer on average (81.4 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Thailand) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
