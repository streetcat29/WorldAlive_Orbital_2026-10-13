Sweden has 1.9× the population of Norway: 10,596,620 vs 5,610,870, a gap of 5.0 million. Norway is growing faster (+0.69% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 38,724 people a year against +26,945. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Norway would overtake Sweden in about 146 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2171) — a projection, not a forecast. Sweden is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 19.1% in Norway. Sweden is 1.7× as densely populated (25.9 vs 15.2 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Norway ($94,594 vs $63,133).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Norway) and 2025 (Sweden); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
