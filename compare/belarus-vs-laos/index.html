Belarus has 1.2× the population of Laos: 9,085,991 vs 7,873,046, a gap of 1.2 million. Laos is growing faster (+1.32% vs −0.51% a year), adding about 103,910 people a year against −46,519. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Laos would overtake Belarus in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Belarus is the older society: 18.3% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Laos, while Laos has more children (29.8% under 15 vs 15.9%). Belarus is 1.4× as densely populated (45.2 vs 33.2 people per km²). People in Belarus live 5.1 years longer on average (74.4 vs 69.2). GDP per capita is 4.4× higher in Belarus ($10,279 vs $2,325).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Belarus) and 2025 (Laos); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
