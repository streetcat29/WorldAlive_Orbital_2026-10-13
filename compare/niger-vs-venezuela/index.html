Venezuela has about the same population as Niger: 28,516,896 vs 27,917,831, a gap of 599,065. Niger is growing faster (+3.22% vs +0.39% a year), adding about 899,764 people a year against +111,571. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Niger would overtake Venezuela in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Venezuela is the older society: 10.0% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Niger, while Niger has more children (46.2% under 15 vs 24.9%). Venezuela is 1.6× as densely populated (32.1 vs 20.7 people per km²). People in Venezuela live 11.2 years longer on average (72.7 vs 61.4). GDP per capita is 4.5× higher in Venezuela ($3,495 vs $775).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Niger) and 2025 (Venezuela); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
