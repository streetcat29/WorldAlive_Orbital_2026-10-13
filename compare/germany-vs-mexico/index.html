Mexico has 1.6× the population of Germany: 131,946,900 vs 83,491,249, a gap of 48.5 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against −25,340. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 13.9%). Germany is 3.6× as densely populated (238.4 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Germany live 5.5 years longer on average (80.8 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 4.4× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $13,889).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Germany) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
