India has 20.4× the population of Thailand: 1,463,865,525 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 1.39 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 14.4%). India is 3.4× as densely populated (483.7 vs 140.3 people per km²). People in Thailand live 4.3 years longer on average (76.6 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 3.0× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
