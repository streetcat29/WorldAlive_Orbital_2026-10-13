Bahrain has 13% more people than Timor-Leste: 1,600,366 vs 1,418,517, a gap of 181,849. Timor-Leste is growing faster (+1.27% vs +0.73% a year), adding about 17,993 people a year against +11,739. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Timor-Leste would overtake Bahrain in about 23 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2048) — a projection, not a forecast. Timor-Leste is the older society: 5.2% aged 65+ against 4.0% in Bahrain. Bahrain is 21.2× as densely populated (1,971.3 vs 93.1 people per km²). People in Bahrain live 13.5 years longer on average (81.4 vs 67.9). GDP per capita is 22.8× higher in Bahrain ($30,597 vs $1,341).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bahrain) and 2025 (Timor-Leste); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
