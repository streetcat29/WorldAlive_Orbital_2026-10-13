Iran has 1.7× the population of Colombia: 92,417,681 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 39.0 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +853,875. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 20.0%). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $3,924).
