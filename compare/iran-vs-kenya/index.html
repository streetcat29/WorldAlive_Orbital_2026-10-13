Iran has 1.6× the population of Kenya: 92,417,681 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 34.9 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +853,875. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Iran in about 47 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2072) — a projection, not a forecast. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 22.0%). Kenya is 1.7× as densely populated (95.3 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 14.0 years longer on average (77.9 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
