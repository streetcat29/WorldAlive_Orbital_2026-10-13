Libya has 6% more people than Nicaragua: 7,458,555 vs 7,007,502, a gap of 451,053. Nicaragua is growing faster (+1.31% vs +1.04% a year), adding about 91,963 people a year against +77,938. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Nicaragua would overtake Libya in about 23 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2048) — a projection, not a forecast. Nicaragua is 13.7× as densely populated (56.7 vs 4.2 people per km²). People in Nicaragua live 4.0 years longer on average (75.1 vs 71.1). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Libya ($6,449 vs $3,173).
