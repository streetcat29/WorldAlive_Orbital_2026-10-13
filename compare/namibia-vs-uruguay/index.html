Uruguay has 9% more people than Namibia: 3,384,688 vs 3,092,816, a gap of 291,872. Namibia is growing faster (+2.05% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 63,329 people a year against −1,899. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Namibia would overtake Uruguay in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Uruguay is the older society: 16.3% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Namibia, while Namibia has more children (36.7% under 15 vs 17.9%). Uruguay is 5.4× as densely populated (19.4 vs 3.6 people per km²). People in Uruguay live 10.8 years longer on average (78.3 vs 67.5). GDP per capita is 5.2× higher in Uruguay ($25,216 vs $4,876).
