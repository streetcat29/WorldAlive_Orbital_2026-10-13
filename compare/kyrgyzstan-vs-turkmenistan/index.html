Turkmenistan has 4% more people than Kyrgyzstan: 7,618,847 vs 7,343,064, a gap of 275,783. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+1.66% vs +1.65% a year). Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kyrgyzstan would overtake Turkmenistan in about 198 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2223) — a projection, not a forecast. Kyrgyzstan is the older society: 6.0% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Turkmenistan. Kyrgyzstan is 2.4× as densely populated (37.0 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Kyrgyzstan live 2.2 years longer on average (72.4 vs 70.2). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Turkmenistan ($6,540 vs $3,081).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kyrgyzstan) and 2025 (Turkmenistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
