Thailand has 11% more people than South Africa: 71,619,863 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 6.9 million. South Africa is growing faster (+1.15% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 744,395 people a year against −48,132. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, South Africa would overtake Thailand in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 6.9% in South Africa, while South Africa has more children (25.7% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 2.7× as densely populated (140.3 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in Thailand live 10.3 years longer on average (76.6 vs 66.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Africa) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
