Nigeria has 2.6× the population of Iran: 237,527,782 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 145.1 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +853,875. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 22.0%). Nigeria is 4.5× as densely populated (250.2 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 23.2 years longer on average (77.9 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
