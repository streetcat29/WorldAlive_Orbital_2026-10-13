Tajikistan has 4% more people than Greece: 10,786,734 vs 10,413,962, a gap of 372,772. Tajikistan is growing faster (+1.83% vs +0.08% a year), adding about 197,606 people a year against +8,832. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Greece is the older society: 24.4% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Tajikistan, while Tajikistan has more children (35.9% under 15 vs 12.9%). People in Greece live 9.9 years longer on average (81.8 vs 71.9). GDP per capita is 16.5× higher in Greece ($26,948 vs $1,637).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Greece) and 2025 (Tajikistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
