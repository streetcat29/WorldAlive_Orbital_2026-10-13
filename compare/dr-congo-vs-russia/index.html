Russia has 1.3× the population of DR Congo: 143,513,328 vs 112,832,473, a gap of 30.7 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, DR Congo would overtake Russia in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 17.0%). DR Congo is 5.3× as densely populated (46.7 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 11.4 years longer on average (73.4 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 21.7× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
