France has 1.3× the population of Myanmar: 68,720,337 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 13.9 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against +168,891. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Myanmar would overtake France in about 57 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2082) — a projection, not a forecast. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 16.2%). France is 1.5× as densely populated (126.9 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in France live 15.9 years longer on average (83.0 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 32.9× higher in France ($48,986 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (France) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
