Argentina has 5% more people than Afghanistan: 45,851,378 vs 43,844,111, a gap of 2.0 million. Afghanistan is growing faster (+2.77% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 1,213,252 people a year against +155,482. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Afghanistan would overtake Argentina in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Argentina is the older society: 12.6% aged 65+ against 2.4% in Afghanistan, while Afghanistan has more children (42.6% under 15 vs 21.0%). Afghanistan is 3.8× as densely populated (63.6 vs 16.6 people per km²). People in Argentina live 11.3 years longer on average (77.5 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 35.7× higher in Argentina ($14,898 vs $417).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Afghanistan) and 2025 (Argentina); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
