Palestine has about the same population as New Zealand: 5,413,596 vs 5,324,700, a gap of 88,896. Palestine is growing faster (+2.33% vs +0.65% a year), adding about 125,897 people a year against +34,814. GDP per capita is 15.6× higher in New Zealand ($49,591 vs $3,171).
