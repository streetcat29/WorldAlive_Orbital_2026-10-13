Denmark has 6% more people than Finland: 6,009,169 vs 5,646,436, a gap of 362,733. Denmark is growing faster (+0.54% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 32,263 people a year against +26,587. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Finland is the older society: 24.2% aged 65+ against 21.1% in Denmark, while Denmark has more children (15.6% under 15 vs 14.3%). Denmark is 8.1× as densely populated (148.7 vs 18.4 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Denmark ($76,970 vs $56,149).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Denmark) and 2025 (Finland); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
