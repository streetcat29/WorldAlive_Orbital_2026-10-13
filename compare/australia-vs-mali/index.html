Australia has 10% more people than Mali: 27,614,411 vs 25,198,821, a gap of 2.4 million. Mali is growing faster (+2.90% vs +1.53% a year), adding about 730,719 people a year against +423,354. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Mali would overtake Australia in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Australia is the older society: 18.1% aged 65+ against 2.4% in Mali, while Mali has more children (45.8% under 15 vs 17.7%). Mali is 5.6× as densely populated (19.5 vs 3.5 people per km²). People in Australia live 22.4 years longer on average (83.1 vs 60.7). GDP per capita is 54.6× higher in Australia ($65,130 vs $1,193).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Australia) and 2025 (Mali); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
