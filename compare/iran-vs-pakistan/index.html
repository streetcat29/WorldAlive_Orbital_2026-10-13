Pakistan has 2.8× the population of Iran: 255,219,554 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 162.8 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +853,875. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 22.0%). Pakistan is 5.7× as densely populated (321.1 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 10.1 years longer on average (77.9 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
