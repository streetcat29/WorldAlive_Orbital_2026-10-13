China vs DR Congo

China has 12.5× the population of DR Congo: 1,406,585,000 vs 112,832,473, a gap of 1.29 billion. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, DR Congo would overtake China in about 75 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2100) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 3.2× as densely populated (150.3 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in China live 15.9 years longer on average (78.0 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 17.2× higher in China ($13,862 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (DR Congo); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
