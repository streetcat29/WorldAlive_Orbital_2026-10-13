Russia has 1.2× the population of Japan: 143,513,328 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 20.1 million. Russia is shrinking more slowly (−0.11% vs −0.49% a year), adding about -156,235 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 17.8% in Russia, while Russia has more children (17.0% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 38.9× as densely populated (341.5 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Japan live 10.6 years longer on average (84.0 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $17,547).
