DR Congo has 1.6× the population of Tanzania: 112,832,473 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 42.3 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +2.86% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +2,014,190. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Tanzania is 1.6× as densely populated (75.2 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Tanzania live 5.1 years longer on average (67.2 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Tanzania ($1,319 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
