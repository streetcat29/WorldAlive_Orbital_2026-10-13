Ethiopia has 10% more people than Japan: 135,472,051 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 12.1 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 3.0× as densely populated (341.5 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in Japan live 16.4 years longer on average (84.0 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 38.5× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
