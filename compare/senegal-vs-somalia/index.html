Somalia has 4% more people than Senegal: 19,654,739 vs 18,931,966, a gap of 722,773. Somalia is growing faster (+3.34% vs +2.30% a year), adding about 656,429 people a year against +434,940. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Senegal is the older society: 3.6% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Somalia, while Somalia has more children (46.6% under 15 vs 37.7%). Senegal is 3.2× as densely populated (93.9 vs 29.3 people per km²). People in Senegal live 10.0 years longer on average (68.9 vs 59.0). GDP per capita is 3.0× higher in Senegal ($1,955 vs $661).
