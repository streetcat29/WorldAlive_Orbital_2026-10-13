Slovenia has 1.2× the population of North Macedonia: 2,130,986 vs 1,820,909, a gap of 310,077. Slovenia is growing faster (+0.17% vs −0.19% a year), adding about 3,589 people a year against −3,447. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Slovenia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 18.5% in North Macedonia, while North Macedonia has more children (16.5% under 15 vs 14.5%). Slovenia is 1.5× as densely populated (105.3 vs 72.5 people per km²). People in Slovenia live 5.7 years longer on average (82.3 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 3.6× higher in Slovenia ($37,376 vs $10,490).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (North Macedonia) and 2025 (Slovenia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
