Nepal has 1.4× the population of Sri Lanka: 29,618,118 vs 21,756,000, a gap of 7.9 million. Nepal is shrinking more slowly (−0.11% vs −0.73% a year), adding about -32,918 people a year against −159,415. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Sri Lanka is the older society: 12.5% aged 65+ against 6.6% in Nepal, while Nepal has more children (28.1% under 15 vs 21.7%). Sri Lanka is 1.7× as densely populated (356.2 vs 207.1 people per km²). People in Sri Lanka live 7.0 years longer on average (77.7 vs 70.6). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Sri Lanka ($5,002 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nepal) and 2025 (Sri Lanka); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
