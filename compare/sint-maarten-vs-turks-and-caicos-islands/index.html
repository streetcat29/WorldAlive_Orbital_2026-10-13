Turks and Caicos Islands has 7% more people than Sint Maarten: 46,855 vs 43,923, a gap of 2,932. Sint Maarten is growing faster (+1.31% vs +0.69% a year), adding about 577 people a year against +321. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sint Maarten would overtake Turks and Caicos Islands in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Sint Maarten is the older society: 14.5% aged 65+ against 11.7% in Turks and Caicos Islands, while Turks and Caicos Islands has more children (16.0% under 15 vs 14.8%). Sint Maarten is 25.9× as densely populated (1,257.3 vs 48.6 people per km²). People in Turks and Caicos Islands live 1.7 years longer on average (78.2 vs 76.5).
