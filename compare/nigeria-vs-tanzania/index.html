Nigeria has 3.4× the population of Tanzania: 237,527,782 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 167.0 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +2.06% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +4,898,468. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Nigeria in about 153 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2178) — a projection, not a forecast. Nigeria is 3.3× as densely populated (250.2 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in Tanzania live 12.6 years longer on average (67.2 vs 54.6).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nigeria) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
