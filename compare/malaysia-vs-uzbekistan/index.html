Uzbekistan has about the same population as Malaysia: 37,053,428 vs 35,977,838, a gap of 1.1 million. Uzbekistan is growing faster (+1.88% vs +1.17% a year), adding about 698,104 people a year against +422,638. Malaysia is the older society: 8.0% aged 65+ against 6.1% in Uzbekistan, while Uzbekistan has more children (31.3% under 15 vs 21.3%). Malaysia is 1.3× as densely populated (106.9 vs 80.9 people per km²). People in Malaysia live 4.3 years longer on average (76.8 vs 72.5). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Malaysia ($13,125 vs $3,968).
