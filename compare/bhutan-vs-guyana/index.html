Guyana has 5% more people than Bhutan: 835,986 vs 796,682, a gap of 39,304. Bhutan is growing faster (+0.65% vs +0.59% a year), adding about 5,175 people a year against +4,913. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Bhutan would overtake Guyana in about 78 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2103) — a projection, not a forecast.
