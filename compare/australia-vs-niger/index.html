Niger has about the same population as Australia: 27,917,831 vs 27,614,411, a gap of 303,420. Niger is growing faster (+3.22% vs +1.53% a year), adding about 899,764 people a year against +423,354. Australia is the older society: 18.1% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Niger, while Niger has more children (46.2% under 15 vs 17.7%). Niger is 6.0× as densely populated (20.7 vs 3.5 people per km²). People in Australia live 21.6 years longer on average (83.1 vs 61.4). GDP per capita is 84.0× higher in Australia ($65,130 vs $775).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Australia) and 2025 (Niger); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
