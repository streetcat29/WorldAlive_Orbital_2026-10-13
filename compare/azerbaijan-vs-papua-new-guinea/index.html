Papua New Guinea has 5% more people than Azerbaijan: 10,762,817 vs 10,246,996, a gap of 515,821. Papua New Guinea is growing faster (+1.75% vs +0.43% a year), adding about 187,947 people a year against +44,261. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Azerbaijan is the older society: 9.2% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Papua New Guinea, while Papua New Guinea has more children (33.1% under 15 vs 21.2%). Azerbaijan is 5.4× as densely populated (122.9 vs 22.9 people per km²). People in Azerbaijan live 8.3 years longer on average (74.6 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Azerbaijan ($7,411 vs $3,020).
