Estonia has 1.2× the population of Djibouti: 1,366,475 vs 1,184,076, a gap of 182,399. Djibouti is growing faster (+1.31% vs −0.43% a year), adding about 15,454 people a year against −5,853. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Djibouti would overtake Estonia in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Estonia is the older society: 21.8% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Djibouti, while Djibouti has more children (28.8% under 15 vs 15.3%). Djibouti is 1.6× as densely populated (49.7 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Estonia live 13.1 years longer on average (79.3 vs 66.2). GDP per capita is 8.8× higher in Estonia ($34,418 vs $3,906).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Djibouti) and 2025 (Estonia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
