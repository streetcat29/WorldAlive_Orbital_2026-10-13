Kyrgyzstan has 12% more people than Serbia: 7,343,064 vs 6,549,143, a gap of 793,921. Kyrgyzstan is growing faster (+1.66% vs −0.57% a year), adding about 122,207 people a year against −37,227. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Serbia is the older society: 23.1% aged 65+ against 6.0% in Kyrgyzstan, while Kyrgyzstan has more children (31.9% under 15 vs 14.3%). Serbia is 2.1× as densely populated (78.8 vs 37.0 people per km²). People in Serbia live 3.6 years longer on average (76.0 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 5.0× higher in Serbia ($15,262 vs $3,081).
