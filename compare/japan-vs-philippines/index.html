Japan has 6% more people than Philippines: 123,366,734 vs 116,786,962, a gap of 6.6 million. Philippines is growing faster (+0.81% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 947,122 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Philippines would overtake Japan in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 11.2%). People in Japan live 14.1 years longer on average (84.0 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 8.6× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $4,171).
