Gabon has 10% more people than Moldova: 2,593,130 vs 2,360,527, a gap of 232,603. Gabon is growing faster (+2.11% vs −1.75% a year), adding about 54,752 people a year against −41,414. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Moldova is the older society: 16.9% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Gabon, while Gabon has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 19.5%). Moldova is 8.9× as densely populated (85.4 vs 9.6 people per km²). People in Moldova live 2.8 years longer on average (71.3 vs 68.5).
