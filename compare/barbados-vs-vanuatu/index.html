Vanuatu has 1.2× the population of Barbados: 335,169 vs 282,623, a gap of 52,546. Vanuatu is growing faster (+2.23% vs +0.06% a year), adding about 7,475 people a year against +156. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Barbados is the older society: 17.1% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Vanuatu, while Vanuatu has more children (38.0% under 15 vs 17.1%). Barbados is 25.0× as densely populated (656.6 vs 26.3 people per km²). People in Barbados live 4.7 years longer on average (76.3 vs 71.7). GDP per capita is 7.0× higher in Barbados ($28,365 vs $4,039).
