Germany has 1.6× the population of Colombia: 83,491,249 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 30.1 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against −25,340. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake Germany in about 43 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2068) — a projection, not a forecast. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 13.9%). Germany is 5.1× as densely populated (238.4 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Germany live 2.9 years longer on average (80.8 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $8,562).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Germany); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
