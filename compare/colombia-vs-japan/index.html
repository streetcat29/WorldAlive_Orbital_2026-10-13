Japan has 2.3× the population of Colombia: 123,366,734 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 69.9 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake Japan in about 56 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2081) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 7.2× as densely populated (341.5 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Japan live 6.1 years longer on average (84.0 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 4.2× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $8,562).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
