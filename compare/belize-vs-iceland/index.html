Belize has 8% more people than Iceland: 422,924 vs 392,404, a gap of 30,520. Iceland is growing faster (+1.51% vs +1.39% a year), adding about 5,943 people a year against +5,893. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iceland would overtake Belize in about 62 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2087) — a projection, not a forecast. Iceland is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 5.2% in Belize, while Belize has more children (26.3% under 15 vs 17.5%). Belize is 4.7× as densely populated (18.0 vs 3.8 people per km²). People in Iceland live 9.1 years longer on average (82.8 vs 73.7). GDP per capita is 12.5× higher in Iceland ($98,323 vs $7,865).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Belize) and 2025 (Iceland); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
