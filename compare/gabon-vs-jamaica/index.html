Jamaica has 9% more people than Gabon: 2,837,077 vs 2,593,130, a gap of 243,947. Gabon is growing faster (+2.11% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 54,752 people a year against −2,097. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Gabon would overtake Jamaica in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Jamaica is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Gabon, while Gabon has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 18.3%). Jamaica is 27.2× as densely populated (262.2 vs 9.6 people per km²). People in Jamaica live 3.1 years longer on average (71.6 vs 68.5).
