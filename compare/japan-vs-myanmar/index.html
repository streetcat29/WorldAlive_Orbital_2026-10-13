Japan has 2.2× the population of Myanmar: 123,366,734 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 68.5 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Myanmar would overtake Japan in about 72 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2097) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 4.1× as densely populated (341.5 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Japan live 16.9 years longer on average (84.0 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 24.1× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
