Tanzania has 1.4× the population of South Korea: 70,545,865 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 18.9 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 7.0× as densely populated (529.8 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in South Korea live 16.4 years longer on average (83.6 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 27.5× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Korea) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
