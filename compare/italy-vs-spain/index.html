Italy has 1.2× the population of Spain: 58,915,656 vs 49,355,143, a gap of 9.6 million. Spain is growing faster (+1.03% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 508,918 people a year against −37,036. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Spain would overtake Italy in about 16 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2041) — a projection, not a forecast. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 21.6% in Spain, while Spain has more children (12.6% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 2.1× as densely populated (199.5 vs 96.8 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Spain); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
