Mexico has 1.8× the population of Thailand: 131,946,900 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 60.3 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 2.1× as densely populated (140.3 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Thailand live 1.3 years longer on average (76.6 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
