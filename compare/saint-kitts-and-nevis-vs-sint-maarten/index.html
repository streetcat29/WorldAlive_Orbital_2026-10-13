Saint Kitts and Nevis has 7% more people than Sint Maarten: 46,922 vs 43,923, a gap of 2,999. Sint Maarten is growing faster (+1.31% vs +0.17% a year), adding about 577 people a year against +79. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sint Maarten would overtake Saint Kitts and Nevis in about 6 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2031) — a projection, not a forecast. Sint Maarten is the older society: 14.5% aged 65+ against 11.7% in Saint Kitts and Nevis, while Saint Kitts and Nevis has more children (18.3% under 15 vs 14.8%). Sint Maarten is 7.0× as densely populated (1,257.3 vs 179.8 people per km²). People in Sint Maarten live 4.2 years longer on average (76.5 vs 72.3). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Sint Maarten ($42,978 vs $25,223).
