Australia has 4% more people than North Korea: 27,614,411 vs 26,571,036, a gap of 1.0 million. Australia is growing faster (+1.53% vs +0.27% a year), adding about 423,354 people a year against +72,311. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Australia is the older society: 18.1% aged 65+ against 12.7% in North Korea, while North Korea has more children (18.9% under 15 vs 17.7%). North Korea is 63.3× as densely populated (219.4 vs 3.5 people per km²). People in Australia live 9.3 years longer on average (83.1 vs 73.7).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Australia) and 2025 (North Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
