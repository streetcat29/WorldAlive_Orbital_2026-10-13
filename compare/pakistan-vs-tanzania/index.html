Pakistan has 3.6× the population of Tanzania: 255,219,554 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 184.7 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +3,981,282. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Pakistan in about 99 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2124) — a projection, not a forecast. Pakistan is the older society: 4.4% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 36.2%). Pakistan is 4.3× as densely populated (321.1 vs 75.2 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Pakistan) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
