Mexico has 1.2× the population of DR Congo: 131,946,900 vs 112,832,473, a gap of 19.1 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +1,090,386. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, DR Congo would overtake Mexico in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 24.1%). Mexico is 1.4× as densely populated (66.7 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Mexico live 13.2 years longer on average (75.3 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 17.2× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
