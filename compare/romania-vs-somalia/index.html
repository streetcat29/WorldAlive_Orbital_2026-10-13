Somalia has 3% more people than Romania: 19,654,739 vs 19,020,271, a gap of 634,468. Somalia is growing faster (+3.34% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 656,429 people a year against −31,507. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Romania is the older society: 20.1% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Somalia, while Somalia has more children (46.6% under 15 vs 15.6%). Romania is 2.8× as densely populated (82.8 vs 29.3 people per km²). People in Romania live 17.5 years longer on average (76.5 vs 59.0). GDP per capita is 34.1× higher in Romania ($22,538 vs $661).
