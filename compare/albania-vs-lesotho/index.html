Lesotho has about the same population as Albania: 2,363,325 vs 2,349,580, a gap of 13,745. Lesotho is growing faster (+1.10% vs −1.17% a year), adding about 26,045 people a year against −27,388. Albania is the older society: 17.6% aged 65+ against 3.9% in Lesotho, while Lesotho has more children (34.1% under 15 vs 16.6%). People in Albania live 22.0 years longer on average (79.8 vs 57.8). GDP per capita is 11.9× higher in Albania ($12,998 vs $1,089).
