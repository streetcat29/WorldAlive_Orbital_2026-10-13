Laos has 5% more people than Hong Kong: 7,873,046 vs 7,498,900, a gap of 374,146. Laos is growing faster (+1.32% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 103,910 people a year against −25,158. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Hong Kong is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Laos, while Laos has more children (29.8% under 15 vs 10.3%). Hong Kong is 216.1× as densely populated (7,177.2 vs 33.2 people per km²). People in Hong Kong live 16.2 years longer on average (85.4 vs 69.2). GDP per capita is 24.5× higher in Hong Kong ($56,983 vs $2,325).
