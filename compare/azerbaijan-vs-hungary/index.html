Azerbaijan has 8% more people than Hungary: 10,246,996 vs 9,514,251, a gap of 732,745. Azerbaijan is growing faster (+0.43% vs −0.50% a year), adding about 44,261 people a year against −47,694. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Hungary is the older society: 21.2% aged 65+ against 9.2% in Azerbaijan, while Azerbaijan has more children (21.2% under 15 vs 14.4%). People in Hungary live 2.1 years longer on average (76.7 vs 74.6). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in Hungary ($25,907 vs $7,411).
