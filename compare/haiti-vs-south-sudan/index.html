South Sudan has about the same population as Haiti: 12,188,788 vs 11,906,095, a gap of 282,693. South Sudan is growing faster (+2.03% vs +1.13% a year), adding about 247,884 people a year against +134,293. Haiti is the older society: 4.8% aged 65+ against 3.1% in South Sudan, while South Sudan has more children (38.0% under 15 vs 30.8%). Haiti is 23.2× as densely populated (422.3 vs 18.2 people per km²). People in Haiti live 7.4 years longer on average (65.1 vs 57.7). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Haiti ($2,694 vs $1,080).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Haiti) and 2025 (South Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
