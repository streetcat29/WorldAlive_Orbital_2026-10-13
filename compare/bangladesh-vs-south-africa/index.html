Bangladesh has 2.7× the population of South Africa: 175,686,899 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 110.9 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +1.15% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +744,395. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bangladesh is 25.3× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 8.6 years longer on average (74.9 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
