Vanuatu has 13% more people than New Caledonia: 335,169 vs 295,333, a gap of 39,836. Vanuatu is growing faster (+2.23% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 7,475 people a year against +2,706. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. New Caledonia is the older society: 11.8% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Vanuatu, while Vanuatu has more children (38.0% under 15 vs 21.1%). Vanuatu is 1.7× as densely populated (26.3 vs 15.9 people per km²). People in New Caledonia live 7.3 years longer on average (78.9 vs 71.7). GDP per capita is 7.2× higher in New Caledonia ($29,213 vs $4,039).
