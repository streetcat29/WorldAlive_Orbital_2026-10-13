Vietnam has 2.0× the population of Sudan: 101,598,527 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 49.9 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +612,685. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Vietnam in about 38 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2063) — a projection, not a forecast. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 22.9%). Vietnam is 12.0× as densely populated (320.2 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Vietnam live 8.2 years longer on average (74.7 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 4.4× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sudan) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
