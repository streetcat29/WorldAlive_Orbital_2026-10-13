Mali has 13% more people than Malawi: 25,198,821 vs 22,216,120, a gap of 3.0 million. Mali is growing faster (+2.90% vs +2.56% a year), adding about 730,719 people a year against +568,034. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Malawi is 11.5× as densely populated (223.8 vs 19.5 people per km²). People in Malawi live 6.9 years longer on average (67.6 vs 60.7). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in Mali ($1,193 vs $672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Malawi) and 2025 (Mali); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
