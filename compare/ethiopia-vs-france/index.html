Ethiopia has 2.0× the population of France: 135,472,051 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 66.8 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 16.2%). People in France live 15.4 years longer on average (83.0 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 52.5× higher in France ($48,986 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (France); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
