Nepal has 6% more people than Niger: 29,618,118 vs 27,917,831, a gap of 1.7 million. Niger is growing faster (+3.22% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 899,764 people a year against −32,918. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Niger would overtake Nepal in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Nepal is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Niger, while Niger has more children (46.2% under 15 vs 28.1%). Nepal is 10.0× as densely populated (207.1 vs 20.7 people per km²). People in Nepal live 9.2 years longer on average (70.6 vs 61.4). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Nepal ($1,536 vs $775).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nepal) and 2025 (Niger); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
