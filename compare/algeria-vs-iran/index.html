Iran has 1.9× the population of Algeria: 92,417,681 vs 47,435,312, a gap of 45.0 million. Algeria is growing faster (+1.32% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 625,105 people a year against +853,875. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Algeria would overtake Iran in about 169 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2194) — a projection, not a forecast. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 6.8% in Algeria, while Algeria has more children (29.9% under 15 vs 22.0%). Iran is 2.9× as densely populated (55.8 vs 19.4 people per km²). People in Iran live 1.4 years longer on average (77.9 vs 76.5). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Algeria ($6,051 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Algeria) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
