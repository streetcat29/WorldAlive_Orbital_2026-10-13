Philippines has 1.7× the population of France: 116,786,962 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 48.1 million. Philippines is growing faster (+0.81% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 947,122 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 16.2%). Philippines is 3.0× as densely populated (385.3 vs 126.9 people per km²). People in France live 13.0 years longer on average (83.0 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 11.7× higher in France ($48,986 vs $4,171).
