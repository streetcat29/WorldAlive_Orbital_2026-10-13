Ethiopia has 1.9× the population of Tanzania: 135,472,051 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 64.9 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +2.55% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +3,455,994. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Ethiopia is 1.5× as densely populated (114.0 vs 75.2 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Tanzania ($1,319 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
