Oman has about the same population as Slovakia: 5,494,691 vs 5,413,813, a gap of 80,878. Oman is growing faster (+3.96% vs −0.15% a year), adding about 217,398 people a year against −8,250. Slovakia is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Oman, while Oman has more children (24.3% under 15 vs 15.6%). Slovakia is 6.9× as densely populated (112.9 vs 16.3 people per km²). People in Oman live 1.9 years longer on average (80.2 vs 78.4). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Slovakia ($28,544 vs $19,947).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Oman) and 2025 (Slovakia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
