Syria has about the same population as Mali: 25,620,427 vs 25,198,821, a gap of 421,606. Syria is growing faster (+3.77% vs +2.90% a year), adding about 965,638 people a year against +730,719. Syria is the older society: 4.8% aged 65+ against 2.4% in Mali, while Mali has more children (45.8% under 15 vs 28.4%). Syria is 6.6× as densely populated (128.5 vs 19.5 people per km²). People in Syria live 11.9 years longer on average (72.6 vs 60.7).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mali) and 2025 (Syria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
