Syria has 1.2× the population of Malawi: 25,620,427 vs 22,216,120, a gap of 3.4 million. Syria is growing faster (+3.77% vs +2.56% a year), adding about 965,638 people a year against +568,034. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Syria is the older society: 4.8% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Malawi, while Malawi has more children (40.1% under 15 vs 28.4%). Malawi is 1.7× as densely populated (223.8 vs 128.5 people per km²). People in Syria live 5.0 years longer on average (72.6 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Syria ($1,057 vs $672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Malawi) and 2025 (Syria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
