Namibia has about the same population as Armenia: 3,092,816 vs 3,086,700, a gap of 6,116. Namibia is growing faster (+2.05% vs +1.74% a year), adding about 63,329 people a year against +53,664. Armenia is the older society: 14.4% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Namibia, while Namibia has more children (36.7% under 15 vs 19.0%). Armenia is 29.2× as densely populated (105.1 vs 3.6 people per km²). People in Armenia live 10.8 years longer on average (78.3 vs 67.5). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Armenia ($9,474 vs $4,876).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Armenia) and 2025 (Namibia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
