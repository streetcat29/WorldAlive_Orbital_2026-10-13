Argentina has 10% more people than Canada: 45,851,378 vs 41,651,653, a gap of 4.2 million. Canada is growing faster (+0.94% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 391,155 people a year against +155,482. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Canada would overtake Argentina in about 16 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2041) — a projection, not a forecast. Canada is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 12.6% in Argentina, while Argentina has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 15.0%). Argentina is 3.7× as densely populated (16.6 vs 4.6 people per km²). People in Canada live 4.6 years longer on average (82.1 vs 77.5). GDP per capita is 3.7× higher in Canada ($55,698 vs $14,898).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Argentina) and 2025 (Canada); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
