Sint Maarten has 7% more people than Liechtenstein: 43,923 vs 41,024, a gap of 2,899. Liechtenstein is growing faster (+1.41% vs +1.31% a year), adding about 578 people a year against +577. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Liechtenstein would overtake Sint Maarten in about 71 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2096) — a projection, not a forecast. Liechtenstein is the older society: 21.4% aged 65+ against 14.5% in Sint Maarten, while Sint Maarten has more children (14.8% under 15 vs 14.2%). Sint Maarten is 5.0× as densely populated (1,257.3 vs 249.0 people per km²). People in Liechtenstein live 7.7 years longer on average (84.2 vs 76.5). GDP per capita is 5.1× higher in Liechtenstein ($220,167 vs $42,978).
