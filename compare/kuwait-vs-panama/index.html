Kuwait has 6% more people than Panama: 4,865,298 vs 4,571,189, a gap of 294,109. Panama is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.65% a year), adding about 55,953 people a year against −31,860. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Panama would overtake Kuwait in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Panama is the older society: 9.7% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Kuwait. Kuwait is 4.5× as densely populated (272.4 vs 60.1 people per km²). People in Kuwait live 4.8 years longer on average (84.6 vs 79.8). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Kuwait ($32,312 vs $19,790).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kuwait) and 2025 (Panama); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
