Egypt has 2.1× the population of Kenya: 118,365,995 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 60.8 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +1,841,995. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Egypt in about 193 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2218) — a projection, not a forecast. Egypt is the older society: 5.3% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 31.6%). People in Egypt live 8.0 years longer on average (71.8 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Egypt ($3,086 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
