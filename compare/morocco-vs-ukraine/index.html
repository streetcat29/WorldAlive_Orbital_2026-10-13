Ukraine has about the same population as Morocco: 38,980,376 vs 38,430,770, a gap of 549,606. Ukraine is growing faster (+2.92% vs +0.91% a year), adding about 1,136,565 people a year against +351,197. Ukraine is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Morocco, while Morocco has more children (25.2% under 15 vs 13.7%). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Ukraine ($5,866 vs $4,672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Morocco) and 2025 (Ukraine); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
