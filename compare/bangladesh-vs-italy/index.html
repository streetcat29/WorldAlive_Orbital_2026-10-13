Bangladesh has 3.0× the population of Italy: 175,686,899 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 116.8 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 11.7%). Bangladesh is 6.6× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 199.5 people per km²). People in Italy live 9.0 years longer on average (84.0 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 16.7× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Italy); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
