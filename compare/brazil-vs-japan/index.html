Brazil has 1.7× the population of Japan: 212,812,405 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 89.4 million. Brazil is growing faster (+0.38% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 815,392 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 11.5% in Brazil, while Brazil has more children (19.4% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 13.5× as densely populated (341.5 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Japan live 8.0 years longer on average (84.0 vs 76.0). GDP per capita is 3.4× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $10,713).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
