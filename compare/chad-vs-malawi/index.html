Malawi has 6% more people than Chad: 22,216,120 vs 21,003,705, a gap of 1.2 million. Chad is growing faster (+3.41% vs +2.56% a year), adding about 716,671 people a year against +568,034. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Chad would overtake Malawi in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Malawi is 14.6× as densely populated (223.8 vs 15.3 people per km²). People in Malawi live 12.3 years longer on average (67.6 vs 55.2). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Chad ($1,022 vs $672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chad) and 2025 (Malawi); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
