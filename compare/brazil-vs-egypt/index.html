Brazil has 1.8× the population of Egypt: 212,812,405 vs 118,365,995, a gap of 94.4 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +815,392. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Egypt would overtake Brazil in about 50 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2075) — a projection, not a forecast. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 19.4%). Egypt is 4.6× as densely populated (115.1 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Brazil live 4.2 years longer on average (76.0 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Egypt); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
