Tanzania has 1.2× the population of Italy: 70,545,865 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 11.6 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 2.7× as densely populated (199.5 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in Italy live 16.7 years longer on average (84.0 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 32.8× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
