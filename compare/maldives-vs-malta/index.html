Malta has 9% more people than Maldives: 579,704 vs 529,676, a gap of 50,028. Malta is growing faster (+1.89% vs +0.35% a year), adding about 10,960 people a year against +1,880. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Malta is the older society: 20.5% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Maldives, while Maldives has more children (19.0% under 15 vs 12.9%). People in Malta live 1.7 years longer on average (83.0 vs 81.3). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Malta ($47,907 vs $14,615).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Maldives) and 2025 (Malta); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
