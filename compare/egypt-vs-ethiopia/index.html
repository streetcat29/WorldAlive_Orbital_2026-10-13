Ethiopia has 14% more people than Egypt: 135,472,051 vs 118,365,995, a gap of 17.1 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +1,841,995. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Egypt is the older society: 5.3% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 31.6%). People in Egypt live 4.2 years longer on average (71.8 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Egypt ($3,086 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Ethiopia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
