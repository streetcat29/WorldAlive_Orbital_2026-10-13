Lesotho has 5% more people than Guinea-Bissau: 2,363,325 vs 2,249,515, a gap of 113,810. Guinea-Bissau is growing faster (+2.16% vs +1.10% a year), adding about 48,686 people a year against +26,045. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guinea-Bissau would overtake Lesotho in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. People in Guinea-Bissau live 6.5 years longer on average (64.3 vs 57.8).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Guinea-Bissau) and 2025 (Lesotho); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
