Antigua and Barbuda has 1.2× the population of Cayman Islands: 94,209 vs 75,844, a gap of 18,365. Cayman Islands is growing faster (+1.85% vs +0.46% a year), adding about 1,400 people a year against +438. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cayman Islands would overtake Antigua and Barbuda in about 16 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2041) — a projection, not a forecast. People in Cayman Islands live 2.8 years longer on average (80.5 vs 77.8). GDP per capita is 4.2× higher in Cayman Islands ($104,293 vs $24,819).
