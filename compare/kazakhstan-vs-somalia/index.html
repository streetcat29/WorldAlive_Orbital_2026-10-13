Kazakhstan has 6% more people than Somalia: 20,843,754 vs 19,654,739, a gap of 1.2 million. Somalia is growing faster (+3.34% vs +1.21% a year), adding about 656,429 people a year against +252,709. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Somalia would overtake Kazakhstan in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Kazakhstan is the older society: 9.0% aged 65+ against 2.6% in Somalia, while Somalia has more children (46.6% under 15 vs 29.1%). Somalia is 3.9× as densely populated (29.3 vs 7.5 people per km²). People in Kazakhstan live 15.6 years longer on average (74.5 vs 59.0). GDP per capita is 22.2× higher in Kazakhstan ($14,692 vs $661).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kazakhstan) and 2025 (Somalia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
