Russia has 6% more people than Ethiopia: 143,513,328 vs 135,472,051, a gap of 8.0 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ethiopia would overtake Russia in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 17.0%). Ethiopia is 13.0× as densely populated (114.0 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 5.8 years longer on average (73.4 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 18.8× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
