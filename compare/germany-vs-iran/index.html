Iran has 11% more people than Germany: 92,417,681 vs 83,491,249, a gap of 8.9 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against −25,340. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 13.9%). Germany is 4.3× as densely populated (238.4 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Germany live 2.9 years longer on average (80.8 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 15.4× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $3,924).
