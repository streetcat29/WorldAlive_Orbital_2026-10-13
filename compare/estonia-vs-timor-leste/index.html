Timor-Leste has 4% more people than Estonia: 1,418,517 vs 1,366,475, a gap of 52,042. Timor-Leste is growing faster (+1.27% vs −0.43% a year), adding about 17,993 people a year against −5,853. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Estonia is the older society: 21.8% aged 65+ against 5.2% in Timor-Leste, while Timor-Leste has more children (33.1% under 15 vs 15.3%). Timor-Leste is 2.9× as densely populated (93.1 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Estonia live 11.4 years longer on average (79.3 vs 67.9). GDP per capita is 25.7× higher in Estonia ($34,418 vs $1,341).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Estonia) and 2025 (Timor-Leste); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
