Finland has about the same population as Norway: 5,646,436 vs 5,610,870, a gap of 35,566. Norway is growing faster (+0.69% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 38,724 people a year against +26,587. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Norway would overtake Finland in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Finland is the older society: 24.2% aged 65+ against 19.1% in Norway, while Norway has more children (15.9% under 15 vs 14.3%). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Norway ($94,594 vs $56,149).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Finland) and 2025 (Norway); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
