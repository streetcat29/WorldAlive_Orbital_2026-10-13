Channel Islands has 8% more people than Curaçao: 168,466 vs 156,263, a gap of 12,203. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.20% vs +0.19% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Channel Islands is 2.4× as densely populated (842.1 vs 348.3 people per km²). People in Channel Islands live 4.2 years longer on average (81.2 vs 77.0). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Channel Islands ($74,586 vs $22,833).
