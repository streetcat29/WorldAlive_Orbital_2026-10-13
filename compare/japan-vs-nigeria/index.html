Nigeria has 1.9× the population of Japan: 237,527,782 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 114.2 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 1.4× as densely populated (341.5 vs 250.2 people per km²). People in Japan live 29.4 years longer on average (84.0 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 29.4× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
