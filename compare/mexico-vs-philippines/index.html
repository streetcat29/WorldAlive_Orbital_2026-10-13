Mexico has 13% more people than Philippines: 131,946,900 vs 116,786,962, a gap of 15.2 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.83% vs +0.81% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 24.1%). Philippines is 5.8× as densely populated (385.3 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Mexico live 5.3 years longer on average (75.3 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $4,171).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Philippines); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
