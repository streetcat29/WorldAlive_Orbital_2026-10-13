Sri Lanka has 10% more people than Chile: 21,756,000 vs 19,859,921, a gap of 1.9 million. Chile is growing faster (+0.48% vs −0.73% a year), adding about 95,379 people a year against −159,415. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Chile would overtake Sri Lanka in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Chile is the older society: 14.6% aged 65+ against 12.5% in Sri Lanka, while Sri Lanka has more children (21.7% under 15 vs 16.5%). Sri Lanka is 13.5× as densely populated (356.2 vs 26.5 people per km²). People in Chile live 3.7 years longer on average (81.4 vs 77.7). GDP per capita is 3.6× higher in Chile ($17,995 vs $5,002).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chile) and 2025 (Sri Lanka); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
