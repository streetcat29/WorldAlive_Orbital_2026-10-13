Kyrgyzstan has 5% more people than Paraguay: 7,343,064 vs 7,013,078, a gap of 329,986. Kyrgyzstan is growing faster (+1.66% vs +1.20% a year), adding about 122,207 people a year against +84,431. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Kyrgyzstan is 2.1× as densely populated (37.0 vs 17.3 people per km²). People in Paraguay live 1.6 years longer on average (74.0 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 2.3× higher in Paraguay ($7,027 vs $3,081).
