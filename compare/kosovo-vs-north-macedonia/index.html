North Macedonia has 1.2× the population of Kosovo: 1,820,909 vs 1,576,876, a gap of 244,033. North Macedonia is shrinking more slowly (−0.19% vs −1.10% a year), adding about -3,447 people a year against −17,381. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. North Macedonia is the older society: 18.5% aged 65+ against 10.5% in Kosovo, while Kosovo has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 16.5%). People in Kosovo live 1.6 years longer on average (78.2 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in North Macedonia ($10,490 vs $7,899).
