Kosovo has 1.2× the population of Trinidad and Tobago: 1,576,876 vs 1,367,764, a gap of 209,112. Trinidad and Tobago is shrinking more slowly (−0.04% vs −1.10% a year), adding about -569 people a year against −17,381. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Trinidad and Tobago would overtake Kosovo in about 13 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2038) — a projection, not a forecast. Trinidad and Tobago is the older society: 12.9% aged 65+ against 10.5% in Kosovo, while Kosovo has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 17.5%). People in Kosovo live 4.6 years longer on average (78.2 vs 73.6). GDP per capita is 2.4× higher in Trinidad and Tobago ($18,967 vs $7,899).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kosovo) and 2025 (Trinidad and Tobago); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
