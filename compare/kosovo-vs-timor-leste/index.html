Kosovo vs Timor-Leste

Kosovo has 11% more people than Timor-Leste: 1,576,876 vs 1,418,517, a gap of 158,359. Timor-Leste is growing faster (+1.27% vs −1.10% a year), adding about 17,993 people a year against −17,381. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Timor-Leste would overtake Kosovo in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Kosovo is the older society: 10.5% aged 65+ against 5.2% in Timor-Leste, while Timor-Leste has more children (33.1% under 15 vs 20.0%). People in Kosovo live 10.3 years longer on average (78.2 vs 67.9). GDP per capita is 5.9× higher in Kosovo ($7,899 vs $1,341).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kosovo) and 2025 (Timor-Leste); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
