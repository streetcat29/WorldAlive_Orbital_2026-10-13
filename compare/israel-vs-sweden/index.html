Sweden has 5% more people than Israel: 10,596,620 vs 10,122,800, a gap of 473,820. Israel is growing faster (+1.20% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 121,324 people a year against +26,945. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Israel would overtake Sweden in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Sweden is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 12.7% in Israel, while Israel has more children (27.2% under 15 vs 16.7%). Israel is 17.6× as densely populated (455.1 vs 25.9 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Israel) and 2025 (Sweden); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
