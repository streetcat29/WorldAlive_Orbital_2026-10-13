Montenegro has 7% more people than Malta: 623,129 vs 579,704, a gap of 43,425. Malta is growing faster (+1.89% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 10,960 people a year against −396. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Malta would overtake Montenegro in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Malta is the older society: 20.5% aged 65+ against 18.3% in Montenegro, while Montenegro has more children (17.9% under 15 vs 12.9%). Malta is 37.3× as densely populated (1,727.3 vs 46.4 people per km²). People in Malta live 5.1 years longer on average (83.0 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Malta ($47,907 vs $14,817).
