Australia has 8% more people than Syria: 27,614,411 vs 25,620,427, a gap of 2.0 million. Syria is growing faster (+3.77% vs +1.53% a year), adding about 965,638 people a year against +423,354. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Syria would overtake Australia in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Australia is the older society: 18.1% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Syria, while Syria has more children (28.4% under 15 vs 17.7%). Syria is 37.1× as densely populated (128.5 vs 3.5 people per km²). People in Australia live 10.5 years longer on average (83.1 vs 72.6). GDP per capita is 61.6× higher in Australia ($65,130 vs $1,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Australia) and 2025 (Syria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
