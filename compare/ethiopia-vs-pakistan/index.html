Pakistan has 1.9× the population of Ethiopia: 255,219,554 vs 135,472,051, a gap of 119.7 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +3,981,282. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ethiopia would overtake Pakistan in about 64 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2089) — a projection, not a forecast. Pakistan is the older society: 4.4% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 36.2%). Pakistan is 2.8× as densely populated (321.1 vs 114.0 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Pakistan ($1,596 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
