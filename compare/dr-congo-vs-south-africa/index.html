DR Congo has 1.7× the population of South Africa: 112,832,473 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 48.1 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +1.15% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +744,395. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Africa is the older society: 6.9% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 25.7%). People in South Africa live 4.2 years longer on average (66.3 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 8.2× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
