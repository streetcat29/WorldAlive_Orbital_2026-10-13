Thailand has 1.3× the population of Colombia: 71,619,863 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 18.2 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against −48,132. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake Thailand in about 27 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2052) — a projection, not a forecast. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 3.0× as densely populated (140.3 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 1.3 years longer on average (77.9 vs 76.6).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
