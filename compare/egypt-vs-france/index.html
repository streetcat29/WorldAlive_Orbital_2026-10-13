Egypt has 1.7× the population of France: 118,365,995 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 49.6 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 16.2%). People in France live 11.2 years longer on average (83.0 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 15.9× higher in France ($48,986 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (France); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
