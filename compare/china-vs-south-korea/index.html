China has 27.2× the population of South Korea: 1,406,585,000 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 1.35 billion. Both are growing at almost the same pace (−0.17% vs −0.13% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 14.9% in China, while China has more children (15.4% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 3.5× as densely populated (529.8 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in South Korea live 5.6 years longer on average (83.6 vs 78.0). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $13,862).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
