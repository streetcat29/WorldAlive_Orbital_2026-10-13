Japan has 1.8× the population of France: 123,366,734 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 54.6 million. France is growing faster (+0.25% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 168,891 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, France would overtake Japan in about 79 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2104) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 22.5% in France, while France has more children (16.2% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 2.7× as densely populated (341.5 vs 126.9 people per km²). People in Japan live 1.1 years longer on average (84.0 vs 83.0). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in France ($48,986 vs $35,951).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (France) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
