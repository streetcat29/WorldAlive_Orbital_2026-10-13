Northern Mariana Islands has 9% more people than Gibraltar: 43,541 vs 40,126, a gap of 3,415. Gibraltar is growing faster (+2.01% vs −1.68% a year), adding about 805 people a year against −731. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Gibraltar would overtake Northern Mariana Islands in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast.
