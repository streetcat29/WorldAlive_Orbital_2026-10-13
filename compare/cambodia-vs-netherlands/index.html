Netherlands has about the same population as Cambodia: 18,087,633 vs 17,847,982, a gap of 239,651. Cambodia is growing faster (+1.18% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 210,416 people a year against +94,394. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cambodia would overtake Netherlands in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Netherlands is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 6.4% in Cambodia, while Cambodia has more children (29.5% under 15 vs 14.9%). Netherlands is 5.4× as densely populated (531.0 vs 98.7 people per km²). People in Netherlands live 11.1 years longer on average (82.0 vs 70.8). GDP per capita is 25.7× higher in Netherlands ($73,684 vs $2,872).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cambodia) and 2025 (Netherlands); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
