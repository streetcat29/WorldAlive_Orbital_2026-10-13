Cambodia has 1.2× the population of Benin: 17,847,982 vs 14,814,460, a gap of 3.0 million. Benin is growing faster (+2.40% vs +1.18% a year), adding about 355,979 people a year against +210,416. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Benin would overtake Cambodia in about 15 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2040) — a projection, not a forecast. Cambodia is the older society: 6.4% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Benin, while Benin has more children (41.3% under 15 vs 29.5%). People in Cambodia live 9.9 years longer on average (70.8 vs 61.0). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Cambodia ($2,872 vs $1,658).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Benin) and 2025 (Cambodia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
