Algeria has 3% more people than Argentina: 47,435,312 vs 45,851,378, a gap of 1.6 million. Algeria is growing faster (+1.32% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 625,105 people a year against +155,482. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Argentina is the older society: 12.6% aged 65+ against 6.8% in Algeria, while Algeria has more children (29.9% under 15 vs 21.0%). People in Argentina live 1.1 years longer on average (77.5 vs 76.5). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Argentina ($14,898 vs $6,051).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Algeria) and 2025 (Argentina); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
