Myanmar vs Nigeria

Nigeria has 4.3× the population of Myanmar: 237,527,782 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 182.7 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Myanmar is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 24.1%). Nigeria is 3.0× as densely populated (250.2 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Myanmar live 12.5 years longer on average (67.1 vs 54.6).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
