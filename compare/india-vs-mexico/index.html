India has 11.1× the population of Mexico: 1,463,865,525 vs 131,946,900, a gap of 1.33 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +1,090,386. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 24.1%). India is 7.2× as densely populated (483.7 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Mexico live 3.0 years longer on average (75.3 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 5.1× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
