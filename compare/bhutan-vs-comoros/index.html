Comoros has 11% more people than Bhutan: 882,847 vs 796,682, a gap of 86,165. Comoros is growing faster (+1.85% vs +0.65% a year), adding about 16,370 people a year against +5,175. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bhutan is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 4.5% in Comoros, while Comoros has more children (36.9% under 15 vs 20.4%). Comoros is 22.2× as densely populated (457.0 vs 20.6 people per km²). People in Bhutan live 6.2 years longer on average (73.3 vs 67.0). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Bhutan ($4,493 vs $2,056).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bhutan) and 2025 (Comoros); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
