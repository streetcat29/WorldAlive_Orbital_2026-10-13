United Kingdom has 1.3× the population of Colombia: 69,487,000 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 16.1 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +206,306. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake United Kingdom in about 37 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2062) — a projection, not a forecast. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 6.0× as densely populated (283.2 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 3.5 years longer on average (81.4 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 6.7× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $8,562).
