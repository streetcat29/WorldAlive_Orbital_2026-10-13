Bangladesh has 331.7× the population of Maldives: 175,686,899 vs 529,676, a gap of 175.2 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.35% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +1,880. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bangladesh is the older society: 6.7% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Maldives. Maldives is 1.3× as densely populated (1,765.1 vs 1,319.2 people per km²). People in Maldives live 6.4 years longer on average (81.3 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 5.6× higher in Maldives ($14,615 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Maldives); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
