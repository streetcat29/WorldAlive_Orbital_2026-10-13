India has 15.8× the population of Iran: 1,463,865,525 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 1.37 billion. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.89% vs +0.92% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 22.0%). India is 8.7× as densely populated (483.7 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 5.6 years longer on average (77.9 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
