Bangladesh has 220.5× the population of Bhutan: 175,686,899 vs 796,682, a gap of 174.9 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.65% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +5,175. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bangladesh is 64.0× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 20.6 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 1.7 years longer on average (74.9 vs 73.3). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Bhutan ($4,493 vs $2,597).
