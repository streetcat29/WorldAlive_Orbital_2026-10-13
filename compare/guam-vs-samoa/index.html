Samoa has 1.3× the population of Guam: 219,306 vs 168,999, a gap of 50,307. Guam is growing faster (+0.73% vs +0.59% a year), adding about 1,226 people a year against +1,291. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guam would overtake Samoa in about 190 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2215) — a projection, not a forecast. Guam is the older society: 13.1% aged 65+ against 6.1% in Samoa, while Samoa has more children (38.1% under 15 vs 25.9%). Guam is 4.0× as densely populated (308.3 vs 77.9 people per km²). People in Guam live 5.6 years longer on average (77.4 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in Guam ($41,833 vs $5,873).
