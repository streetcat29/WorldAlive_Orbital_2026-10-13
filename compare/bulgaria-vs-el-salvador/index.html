Bulgaria has about the same population as El Salvador: 6,433,302 vs 6,365,503, a gap of 67,799. El Salvador is growing faster (+0.43% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 27,369 people a year against −8,114. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, El Salvador would overtake Bulgaria in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Bulgaria is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 8.3% in El Salvador, while El Salvador has more children (24.4% under 15 vs 14.4%). El Salvador is 5.1× as densely populated (304.5 vs 59.4 people per km²). People in Bulgaria live 3.5 years longer on average (75.8 vs 72.3). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in Bulgaria ($20,328 vs $5,767).
