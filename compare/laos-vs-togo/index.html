Togo has 9% more people than Laos: 8,591,626 vs 7,873,046, a gap of 718,580. Togo is growing faster (+2.18% vs +1.32% a year), adding about 187,090 people a year against +103,910. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Laos is the older society: 4.8% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Togo, while Togo has more children (40.4% under 15 vs 29.8%). Togo is 4.6× as densely populated (151.2 vs 33.2 people per km²). People in Laos live 6.3 years longer on average (69.2 vs 62.9). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Laos ($2,325 vs $1,384).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Laos) and 2025 (Togo); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
