Bangladesh has 1.9× the population of Iran: 175,686,899 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 83.3 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +853,875. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 22.0%). Bangladesh is 23.6× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 2.9 years longer on average (77.9 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
