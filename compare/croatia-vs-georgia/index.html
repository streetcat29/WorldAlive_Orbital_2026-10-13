Georgia has about the same population as Croatia: 3,935,766 vs 3,876,200, a gap of 59,566. Georgia is growing faster (+3.18% vs +0.26% a year), adding about 125,219 people a year against +10,013. GDP per capita is 2.8× higher in Croatia ($27,104 vs $9,692).
