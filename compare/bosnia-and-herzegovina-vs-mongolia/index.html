Mongolia has 14% more people than Bosnia and Herzegovina: 3,568,978 vs 3,140,095, a gap of 428,883. Mongolia is growing faster (+1.25% vs −0.77% a year), adding about 44,466 people a year against −24,066. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bosnia and Herzegovina is the older society: 22.9% aged 65+ against 5.4% in Mongolia, while Mongolia has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 12.8%). Bosnia and Herzegovina is 27.9× as densely populated (62.2 vs 2.2 people per km²). People in Bosnia and Herzegovina live 5.6 years longer on average (78.0 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Bosnia and Herzegovina ($10,382 vs $7,108).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bosnia and Herzegovina) and 2025 (Mongolia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
