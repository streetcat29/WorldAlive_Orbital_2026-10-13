Israel has 6% more people than Hungary: 10,122,800 vs 9,514,251, a gap of 608,549. Israel is growing faster (+1.20% vs −0.50% a year), adding about 121,324 people a year against −47,694. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Hungary is the older society: 21.2% aged 65+ against 12.7% in Israel, while Israel has more children (27.2% under 15 vs 14.4%). Israel is 4.3× as densely populated (455.1 vs 105.1 people per km²). People in Israel live 6.5 years longer on average (83.2 vs 76.7). GDP per capita is 2.3× higher in Israel ($60,337 vs $25,907).
