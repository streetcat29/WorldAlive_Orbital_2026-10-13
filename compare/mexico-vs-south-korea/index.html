Mexico has 2.6× the population of South Korea: 131,946,900 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 80.3 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 7.9× as densely populated (529.8 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in South Korea live 8.4 years longer on average (83.6 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $13,889).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
