Türkiye has 1.2× the population of United Kingdom: 85,878,556 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 16.4 million. Türkiye is growing faster (+0.42% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 360,651 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 2.6× as densely populated (283.2 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 4.0 years longer on average (81.4 vs 77.4). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $18,599).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Türkiye) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
