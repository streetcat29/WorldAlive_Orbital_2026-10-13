Georgia has 10% more people than Mongolia: 3,935,766 vs 3,568,978, a gap of 366,788. Georgia is growing faster (+3.18% vs +1.25% a year), adding about 125,219 people a year against +44,466. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Georgia is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 5.4% in Mongolia, while Mongolia has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 20.5%). Georgia is 29.1× as densely populated (65.0 vs 2.2 people per km²). People in Georgia live 2.3 years longer on average (74.7 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Georgia ($9,692 vs $7,108).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Georgia) and 2025 (Mongolia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
