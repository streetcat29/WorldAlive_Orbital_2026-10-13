Brazil has 3.9× the population of Myanmar: 212,812,405 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 158.0 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against +815,392. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 19.4%). Myanmar is 3.3× as densely populated (82.9 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Brazil live 8.9 years longer on average (76.0 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 7.2× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
