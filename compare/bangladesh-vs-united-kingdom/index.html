Bangladesh has 2.5× the population of United Kingdom: 175,686,899 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 106.2 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 17.0%). Bangladesh is 4.7× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 283.2 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 6.5 years longer on average (81.4 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 22.2× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
