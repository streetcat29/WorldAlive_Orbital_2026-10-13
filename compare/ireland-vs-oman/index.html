Oman has about the same population as Ireland: 5,494,691 vs 5,484,367, a gap of 10,324. Oman is growing faster (+3.96% vs +1.63% a year), adding about 217,398 people a year against +89,300. Ireland is the older society: 16.2% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Oman, while Oman has more children (24.3% under 15 vs 18.1%). Ireland is 4.7× as densely populated (77.1 vs 16.3 people per km²). People in Ireland live 2.8 years longer on average (83.0 vs 80.2). GDP per capita is 6.6× higher in Ireland ($131,592 vs $19,947).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ireland) and 2025 (Oman); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
