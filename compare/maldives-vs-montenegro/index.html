Montenegro has 1.2× the population of Maldives: 623,129 vs 529,676, a gap of 93,453. Maldives is growing faster (+0.35% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 1,880 people a year against −396. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Maldives would overtake Montenegro in about 39 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2064) — a projection, not a forecast. Montenegro is the older society: 18.3% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Maldives, while Maldives has more children (19.0% under 15 vs 17.9%). Maldives is 38.1× as densely populated (1,765.1 vs 46.4 people per km²). People in Maldives live 3.4 years longer on average (81.3 vs 77.9).
