Dominican Republic has 5% more people than Honduras: 11,520,487 vs 11,005,850, a gap of 514,637. Honduras is growing faster (+1.65% vs +0.81% a year), adding about 181,638 people a year against +93,307. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Honduras would overtake Dominican Republic in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Dominican Republic is the older society: 8.2% aged 65+ against 4.5% in Honduras, while Honduras has more children (30.3% under 15 vs 26.2%). Dominican Republic is 2.5× as densely populated (235.1 vs 95.1 people per km²). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in Dominican Republic ($11,059 vs $3,598).
