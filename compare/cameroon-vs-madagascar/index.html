Madagascar has 10% more people than Cameroon: 32,740,678 vs 29,879,337, a gap of 2.9 million. Cameroon is growing faster (+2.56% vs +2.40% a year), adding about 765,311 people a year against +785,059. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cameroon would overtake Madagascar in about 56 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2081) — a projection, not a forecast. GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Cameroon ($1,972 vs $599).
