Puerto Rico has 3% more people than Armenia: 3,184,835 vs 3,086,700, a gap of 98,135. Armenia is growing faster (+1.74% vs −0.55% a year), adding about 53,664 people a year against −17,637. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Armenia would overtake Puerto Rico in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Puerto Rico is 3.4× as densely populated (361.2 vs 105.1 people per km²). People in Puerto Rico live 3.6 years longer on average (81.9 vs 78.3). GDP per capita is 4.3× higher in Puerto Rico ($40,620 vs $9,474).
