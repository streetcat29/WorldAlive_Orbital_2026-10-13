Paraguay has 7% more people than Serbia: 7,013,078 vs 6,549,143, a gap of 463,935. Paraguay is growing faster (+1.20% vs −0.57% a year), adding about 84,431 people a year against −37,227. People in Serbia live 2.0 years longer on average (76.0 vs 74.0). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Serbia ($15,262 vs $7,027).
