Bhutan has 1.2× the population of Luxembourg: 796,682 vs 686,970, a gap of 109,712. Luxembourg is growing faster (+1.46% vs +0.65% a year), adding about 10,031 people a year against +5,175. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Luxembourg would overtake Bhutan in about 18 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2043) — a projection, not a forecast. Luxembourg is the older society: 15.8% aged 65+ against 6.6% in Bhutan, while Bhutan has more children (20.4% under 15 vs 15.7%). Luxembourg is 12.6× as densely populated (258.9 vs 20.6 people per km²). People in Luxembourg live 9.9 years longer on average (83.2 vs 73.3). GDP per capita is 32.8× higher in Luxembourg ($147,252 vs $4,493).
