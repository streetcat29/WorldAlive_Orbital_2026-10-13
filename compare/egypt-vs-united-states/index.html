United States has 2.9× the population of Egypt: 341,784,857 vs 118,365,995, a gap of 223.4 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +1,785,717. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Egypt would overtake United States in about 103 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2128) — a projection, not a forecast. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 17.1%). Egypt is 3.1× as densely populated (115.1 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 7.1 years longer on average (78.9 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 29.2× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
