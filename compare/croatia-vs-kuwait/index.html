Kuwait has 1.3× the population of Croatia: 4,865,298 vs 3,876,200, a gap of 989,098. Croatia is growing faster (+0.26% vs −0.65% a year), adding about 10,013 people a year against −31,860. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Croatia would overtake Kuwait in about 25 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2050) — a projection, not a forecast. Croatia is the older society: 23.6% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Kuwait, while Kuwait has more children (18.0% under 15 vs 13.7%). Kuwait is 3.9× as densely populated (272.4 vs 69.0 people per km²). People in Kuwait live 5.7 years longer on average (84.6 vs 78.9).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Croatia) and 2025 (Kuwait); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
