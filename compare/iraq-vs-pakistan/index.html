Pakistan has 5.4× the population of Iraq: 255,219,554 vs 47,020,774, a gap of 208.2 million. Iraq is growing faster (+2.10% vs +1.56% a year), adding about 989,089 people a year against +3,981,282. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Pakistan is 3.1× as densely populated (321.1 vs 103.8 people per km²). People in Iraq live 4.6 years longer on average (72.4 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 3.4× higher in Iraq ($5,410 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iraq) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
