Belarus has 6% more people than Togo: 9,085,991 vs 8,591,626, a gap of 494,365. Togo is growing faster (+2.18% vs −0.51% a year), adding about 187,090 people a year against −46,519. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Togo would overtake Belarus in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Belarus is the older society: 18.3% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Togo, while Togo has more children (40.4% under 15 vs 15.9%). Togo is 3.3× as densely populated (151.2 vs 45.2 people per km²). People in Belarus live 11.4 years longer on average (74.4 vs 62.9). GDP per capita is 7.4× higher in Belarus ($10,279 vs $1,384).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Belarus) and 2025 (Togo); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
