Botswana has 9% more people than Albania: 2,562,122 vs 2,349,580, a gap of 212,542. Botswana is growing faster (+1.61% vs −1.17% a year), adding about 41,314 people a year against −27,388. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Albania is the older society: 17.6% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Botswana, while Botswana has more children (32.0% under 15 vs 16.6%). Albania is 20.1× as densely populated (88.0 vs 4.4 people per km²). People in Albania live 10.5 years longer on average (79.8 vs 69.3). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Albania ($12,998 vs $7,778).
