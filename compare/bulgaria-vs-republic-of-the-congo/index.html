Republic of the Congo has about the same population as Bulgaria: 6,484,437 vs 6,433,302, a gap of 51,135. Republic of the Congo is growing faster (+2.36% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 153,273 people a year against −8,114. Bulgaria is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Republic of the Congo, while Republic of the Congo has more children (39.9% under 15 vs 14.4%). Bulgaria is 3.3× as densely populated (59.4 vs 18.1 people per km²). People in Bulgaria live 9.8 years longer on average (75.8 vs 66.0). GDP per capita is 8.1× higher in Bulgaria ($20,328 vs $2,515).
