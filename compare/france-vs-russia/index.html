Russia has 2.1× the population of France: 143,513,328 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 74.8 million. France is growing faster (+0.25% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 168,891 people a year against −156,235. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 17.8% in Russia, while Russia has more children (17.0% under 15 vs 16.2%). France is 14.4× as densely populated (126.9 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in France live 9.5 years longer on average (83.0 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 2.8× higher in France ($48,986 vs $17,547).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (France) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
