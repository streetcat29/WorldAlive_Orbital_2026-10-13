Cape Verde has 1.3× the population of Bahamas: 527,326 vs 403,033, a gap of 124,293. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.44% vs +0.47% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bahamas is the older society: 12.1% aged 65+ against 7.2% in Cape Verde, while Cape Verde has more children (24.7% under 15 vs 17.5%). Cape Verde is 3.2× as densely populated (129.6 vs 39.9 people per km²). People in Cape Verde live 1.5 years longer on average (76.2 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 6.8× higher in Bahamas ($39,455 vs $5,796).
