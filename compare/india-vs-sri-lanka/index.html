India has 67.3× the population of Sri Lanka: 1,463,865,525 vs 21,756,000, a gap of 1.44 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.73% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −159,415. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Sri Lanka is the older society: 12.5% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 21.7%). India is 1.4× as densely populated (483.7 vs 356.2 people per km²). People in Sri Lanka live 5.4 years longer on average (77.7 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Sri Lanka ($5,002 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Sri Lanka); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
