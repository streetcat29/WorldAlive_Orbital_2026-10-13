Marshall Islands has 2.1× the population of Palau: 36,282 vs 17,663, a gap of 18,619. Palau is shrinking more slowly (−0.18% vs −3.43% a year), adding about -32 people a year against −1,244. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Palau would overtake Marshall Islands in about 22 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2047) — a projection, not a forecast. Palau is the older society: 11.8% aged 65+ against 4.9% in Marshall Islands, while Marshall Islands has more children (33.9% under 15 vs 17.9%). Marshall Islands is 5.6× as densely populated (215.7 vs 38.5 people per km²). People in Palau live 2.3 years longer on average (69.4 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 2.3× higher in Palau ($19,532 vs $8,489).
