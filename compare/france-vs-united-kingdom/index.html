United Kingdom has about the same population as France: 69,487,000 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 766,663. United Kingdom is growing faster (+0.30% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 206,306 people a year against +168,891. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 19.7% in United Kingdom, while United Kingdom has more children (17.0% under 15 vs 16.2%). United Kingdom is 2.2× as densely populated (283.2 vs 126.9 people per km²). People in France live 1.6 years longer on average (83.0 vs 81.4).
