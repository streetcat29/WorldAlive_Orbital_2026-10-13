Guinea has 4% more people than Rwanda: 15,099,727 vs 14,569,341, a gap of 530,386. Guinea is growing faster (+2.31% vs +2.17% a year), adding about 348,943 people a year against +316,180. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Rwanda is 9.6× as densely populated (565.6 vs 58.6 people per km²). People in Rwanda live 7.1 years longer on average (68.0 vs 60.9). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Guinea ($1,877 vs $1,124).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Guinea) and 2025 (Rwanda); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
