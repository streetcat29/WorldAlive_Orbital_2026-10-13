Pakistan has 1.8× the population of Russia: 255,219,554 vs 143,513,328, a gap of 111.7 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against −156,235. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 17.0%). Pakistan is 36.6× as densely populated (321.1 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 5.6 years longer on average (73.4 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 11.0× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Pakistan) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
