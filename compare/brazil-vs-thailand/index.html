Brazil has 3.0× the population of Thailand: 212,812,405 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 141.2 million. Brazil is growing faster (+0.38% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 815,392 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 11.5% in Brazil, while Brazil has more children (19.4% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 5.6× as densely populated (140.3 vs 25.3 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
