Thailand has 4% more people than France: 71,619,863 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 2.9 million. France is growing faster (+0.25% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 168,891 people a year against −48,132. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, France would overtake Thailand in about 13 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2038) — a projection, not a forecast. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 16.0% in Thailand. People in France live 6.4 years longer on average (83.0 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 6.1× higher in France ($48,986 vs $8,057).
