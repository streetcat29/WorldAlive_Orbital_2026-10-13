Antigua and Barbuda has 12% more people than Isle of Man: 94,209 vs 84,118, a gap of 10,091. Antigua and Barbuda is growing faster (+0.46% vs −0.05% a year), adding about 438 people a year against −42. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Isle of Man is the older society: 23.6% aged 65+ against 12.3% in Antigua and Barbuda, while Antigua and Barbuda has more children (17.5% under 15 vs 13.9%). Antigua and Barbuda is 1.4× as densely populated (212.1 vs 147.7 people per km²). People in Isle of Man live 3.3 years longer on average (81.1 vs 77.8). GDP per capita is 3.6× higher in Isle of Man ($90,015 vs $24,819).
