Cuba has about the same population as Czechia: 10,937,203 vs 10,886,878, a gap of 50,325. Czechia is shrinking more slowly (−0.17% vs −0.39% a year), adding about -18,135 people a year against −42,497. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Czechia would overtake Cuba in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Czechia is the older society: 21.2% aged 65+ against 17.0% in Cuba. Czechia is 1.3× as densely populated (140.8 vs 106.2 people per km²). People in Czechia live 1.7 years longer on average (80.0 vs 78.3). GDP per capita is 3.7× higher in Czechia ($35,917 vs $9,605).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cuba) and 2025 (Czechia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
