Lithuania has 11% more people than Gabon: 2,888,774 vs 2,593,130, a gap of 295,644. Gabon is growing faster (+2.11% vs +0.02% a year), adding about 54,752 people a year against +496. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Gabon would overtake Lithuania in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Lithuania is the older society: 20.7% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Gabon, while Gabon has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 14.5%). Lithuania is 4.8× as densely populated (45.9 vs 9.6 people per km²). People in Lithuania live 8.7 years longer on average (77.2 vs 68.5). GDP per capita is 4.0× higher in Lithuania ($32,959 vs $8,263).
