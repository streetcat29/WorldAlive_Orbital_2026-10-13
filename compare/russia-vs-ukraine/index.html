Russia has 3.7× the population of Ukraine: 143,513,328 vs 38,980,376, a gap of 104.5 million. Ukraine is growing faster (+2.92% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 1,136,565 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ukraine would overtake Russia in about 43 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2068) — a projection, not a forecast. Ukraine is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 17.8% in Russia, while Russia has more children (17.0% under 15 vs 13.7%). Ukraine is 7.4× as densely populated (65.1 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Ukraine live 1.3 years longer on average (74.7 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 3.0× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $5,866).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Russia) and 2025 (Ukraine); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
