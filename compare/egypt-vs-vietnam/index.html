Egypt has 1.2× the population of Vietnam: 118,365,995 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 16.8 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +612,685. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 22.9%). Vietnam is 2.8× as densely populated (320.2 vs 115.1 people per km²). People in Vietnam live 2.9 years longer on average (74.7 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
