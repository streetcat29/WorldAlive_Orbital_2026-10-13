Ethiopia has about the same population as Mexico: 135,472,051 vs 131,946,900, a gap of 3.5 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +1,090,386. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 24.1%). Ethiopia is 1.7× as densely populated (114.0 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Mexico live 7.7 years longer on average (75.3 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 14.9× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
