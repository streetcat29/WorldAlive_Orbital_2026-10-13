Afghanistan has 12% more people than Angola: 43,844,111 vs 39,040,039, a gap of 4.8 million. Angola is growing faster (+3.00% vs +2.77% a year), adding about 1,171,595 people a year against +1,213,252. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Angola would overtake Afghanistan in about 50 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2075) — a projection, not a forecast. Afghanistan is 2.2× as densely populated (63.6 vs 29.5 people per km²). People in Afghanistan live 1.5 years longer on average (66.3 vs 64.8). GDP per capita is 7.5× higher in Angola ($3,129 vs $417).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Afghanistan) and 2025 (Angola); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
