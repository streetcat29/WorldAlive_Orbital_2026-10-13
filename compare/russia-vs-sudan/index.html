Russia has 2.8× the population of Sudan: 143,513,328 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 91.9 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against −156,235. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Russia in about 41 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2066) — a projection, not a forecast. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 17.0%). Sudan is 3.1× as densely populated (26.8 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 6.9 years longer on average (73.4 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 15.1× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Russia) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
