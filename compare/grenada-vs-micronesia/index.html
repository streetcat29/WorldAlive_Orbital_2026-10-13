Grenada has 3% more people than Micronesia: 117,303 vs 113,683, a gap of 3,620. Micronesia is growing faster (+0.46% vs +0.08% a year), adding about 524 people a year against +96. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Micronesia would overtake Grenada in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Grenada is the older society: 12.6% aged 65+ against 6.2% in Micronesia, while Micronesia has more children (31.5% under 15 vs 19.0%). Grenada is 2.1× as densely populated (344.4 vs 160.9 people per km²). People in Grenada live 8.0 years longer on average (75.4 vs 67.4). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in Grenada ($12,107 vs $4,414).
