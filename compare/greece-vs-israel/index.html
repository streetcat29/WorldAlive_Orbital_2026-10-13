Greece has about the same population as Israel: 10,413,962 vs 10,122,800, a gap of 291,162. Israel is growing faster (+1.20% vs +0.08% a year), adding about 121,324 people a year against +8,832. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Israel would overtake Greece in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Greece is the older society: 24.4% aged 65+ against 12.7% in Israel, while Israel has more children (27.2% under 15 vs 12.9%). Israel is 5.6× as densely populated (455.1 vs 80.7 people per km²). People in Israel live 1.4 years longer on average (83.2 vs 81.8). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Israel ($60,337 vs $26,948).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Greece) and 2025 (Israel); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
