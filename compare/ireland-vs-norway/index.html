Norway has about the same population as Ireland: 5,610,870 vs 5,484,367, a gap of 126,503. Ireland is growing faster (+1.63% vs +0.69% a year), adding about 89,300 people a year against +38,724. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ireland would overtake Norway in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Norway is the older society: 19.1% aged 65+ against 16.2% in Ireland, while Ireland has more children (18.1% under 15 vs 15.9%). Ireland is 5.1× as densely populated (77.1 vs 15.2 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Ireland ($131,592 vs $94,594).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ireland) and 2025 (Norway); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
