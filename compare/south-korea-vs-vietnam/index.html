Vietnam has 2.0× the population of South Korea: 101,598,527 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 49.9 million. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 1.7× as densely populated (529.8 vs 320.2 people per km²). People in South Korea live 8.9 years longer on average (83.6 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 7.2× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $5,066).
