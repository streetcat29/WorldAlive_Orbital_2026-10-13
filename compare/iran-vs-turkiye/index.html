Iran has 8% more people than Türkiye: 92,417,681 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 6.5 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 21.0%). Türkiye is 2.0× as densely populated (110.9 vs 55.8 people per km²). GDP per capita is 4.7× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
