Zambia has 5% more people than Kazakhstan: 21,913,874 vs 20,843,754, a gap of 1.1 million. Zambia is growing faster (+2.77% vs +1.21% a year), adding about 607,255 people a year against +252,709. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Kazakhstan is the older society: 9.0% aged 65+ against 2.0% in Zambia, while Zambia has more children (41.0% under 15 vs 29.1%). Zambia is 3.7× as densely populated (27.9 vs 7.5 people per km²). People in Kazakhstan live 8.0 years longer on average (74.5 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 11.1× higher in Kazakhstan ($14,692 vs $1,318).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kazakhstan) and 2025 (Zambia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
