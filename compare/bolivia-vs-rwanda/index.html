Rwanda has 1.2× the population of Bolivia: 14,569,341 vs 12,581,843, a gap of 2.0 million. Rwanda is growing faster (+2.17% vs +1.35% a year), adding about 316,180 people a year against +169,667. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bolivia is the older society: 5.7% aged 65+ against 4.0% in Rwanda, while Rwanda has more children (37.0% under 15 vs 29.4%). Rwanda is 50.0× as densely populated (565.6 vs 11.3 people per km²). GDP per capita is 4.6× higher in Bolivia ($5,148 vs $1,124).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bolivia) and 2025 (Rwanda); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
