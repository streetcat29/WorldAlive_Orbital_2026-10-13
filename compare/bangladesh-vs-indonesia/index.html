Indonesia has 1.6× the population of Bangladesh: 285,721,236 vs 175,686,899, a gap of 110.0 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +2,242,079. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Bangladesh would overtake Indonesia in about 113 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2138) — a projection, not a forecast. Bangladesh is 8.9× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 148.6 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 3.6 years longer on average (74.9 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Indonesia ($5,060 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Indonesia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
