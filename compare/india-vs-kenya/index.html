India has 25.4× the population of Kenya: 1,463,865,525 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 1.41 billion. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +12,987,174. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 24.2%). India is 5.1× as densely populated (483.7 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in India live 8.4 years longer on average (72.2 vs 63.8).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
