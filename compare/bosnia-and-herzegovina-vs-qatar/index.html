Bosnia and Herzegovina has 6% more people than Qatar: 3,140,095 vs 2,972,215, a gap of 167,880. Qatar is growing faster (+3.92% vs −0.77% a year), adding about 116,653 people a year against −24,066. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Qatar would overtake Bosnia and Herzegovina in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Bosnia and Herzegovina is the older society: 22.9% aged 65+ against 1.7% in Qatar, while Qatar has more children (15.0% under 15 vs 12.8%). Qatar is 3.7× as densely populated (231.2 vs 62.2 people per km²). People in Qatar live 4.5 years longer on average (82.5 vs 78.0). GDP per capita is 7.0× higher in Qatar ($72,525 vs $10,382).
