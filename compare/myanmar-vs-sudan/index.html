Myanmar has 6% more people than Sudan: 54,850,648 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 3.2 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +351,682. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Myanmar in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Myanmar is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 24.1%). Myanmar is 3.1× as densely populated (82.9 vs 26.8 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Myanmar ($1,489 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
