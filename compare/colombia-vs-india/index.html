India has 27.4× the population of Colombia: 1,463,865,525 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 1.41 billion. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +12,987,174. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 20.0%). India is 10.3× as densely populated (483.7 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 5.7 years longer on average (77.9 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (India); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
