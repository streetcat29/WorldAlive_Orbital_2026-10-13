India has 17.5× the population of Germany: 1,463,865,525 vs 83,491,249, a gap of 1.38 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −25,340. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 13.9%). India is 2.0× as densely populated (483.7 vs 238.4 people per km²). People in Germany live 8.6 years longer on average (80.8 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 22.4× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Germany) and 2025 (India); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
