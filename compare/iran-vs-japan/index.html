Japan has 1.3× the population of Iran: 123,366,734 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 30.9 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iran would overtake Japan in about 20 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2045) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 6.1× as densely populated (341.5 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Japan live 6.2 years longer on average (84.0 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 9.2× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
