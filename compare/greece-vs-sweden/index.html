Sweden has about the same population as Greece: 10,596,620 vs 10,413,962, a gap of 182,658. Sweden is growing faster (+0.25% vs +0.08% a year), adding about 26,945 people a year against +8,832. Greece is the older society: 24.4% aged 65+ against 20.9% in Sweden, while Sweden has more children (16.7% under 15 vs 12.9%). Greece is 3.1× as densely populated (80.7 vs 25.9 people per km²). People in Sweden live 2.2 years longer on average (84.1 vs 81.8). GDP per capita is 2.3× higher in Sweden ($63,133 vs $26,948).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Greece) and 2025 (Sweden); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
