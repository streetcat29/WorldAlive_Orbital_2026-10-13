Nepal has 55.9× the population of Maldives: 29,618,118 vs 529,676, a gap of 29.1 million. Maldives is growing faster (+0.35% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 1,880 people a year against −32,918. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Nepal is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Maldives. Maldives is 8.5× as densely populated (1,765.1 vs 207.1 people per km²). People in Maldives live 10.6 years longer on average (81.3 vs 70.6). GDP per capita is 9.5× higher in Maldives ($14,615 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Maldives) and 2025 (Nepal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
