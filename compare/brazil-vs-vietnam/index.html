Brazil has 2.1× the population of Vietnam: 212,812,405 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 111.2 million. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against +815,392. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 19.4%). Vietnam is 12.7× as densely populated (320.2 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Brazil live 1.3 years longer on average (76.0 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $5,066).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
