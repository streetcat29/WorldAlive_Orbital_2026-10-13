Cape Verde has 13% more people than Brunei: 527,326 vs 466,330, a gap of 60,996. Brunei is growing faster (+0.78% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 3,623 people a year against +2,455. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Brunei would overtake Cape Verde in about 39 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2064) — a projection, not a forecast. Cape Verde is 1.5× as densely populated (129.6 vs 87.1 people per km²). GDP per capita is 5.6× higher in Brunei ($32,235 vs $5,796).
