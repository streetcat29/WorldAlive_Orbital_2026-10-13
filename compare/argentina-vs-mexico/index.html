Mexico has 2.9× the population of Argentina: 131,946,900 vs 45,851,378, a gap of 86.1 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against +155,482. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Argentina is the older society: 12.6% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 21.0%). Mexico is 4.0× as densely populated (66.7 vs 16.6 people per km²). People in Argentina live 2.3 years longer on average (77.5 vs 75.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Argentina) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
