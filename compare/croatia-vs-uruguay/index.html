Croatia has 15% more people than Uruguay: 3,876,200 vs 3,384,688, a gap of 491,512. Croatia is growing faster (+0.26% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 10,013 people a year against −1,899. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Croatia is the older society: 23.6% aged 65+ against 16.3% in Uruguay, while Uruguay has more children (17.9% under 15 vs 13.7%). Croatia is 3.6× as densely populated (69.0 vs 19.4 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Croatia) and 2025 (Uruguay); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
