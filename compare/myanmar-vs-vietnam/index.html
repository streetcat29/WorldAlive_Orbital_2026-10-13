Vietnam has 1.9× the population of Myanmar: 101,598,527 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 46.7 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.64% vs +0.60% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 22.9%). Vietnam is 3.9× as densely populated (320.2 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Vietnam live 7.6 years longer on average (74.7 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 3.4× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
