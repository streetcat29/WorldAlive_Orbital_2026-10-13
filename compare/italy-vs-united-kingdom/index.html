United Kingdom has 1.2× the population of Italy: 69,487,000 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 10.6 million. United Kingdom is growing faster (+0.30% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 206,306 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 19.7% in United Kingdom, while United Kingdom has more children (17.0% under 15 vs 11.7%). United Kingdom is 1.4× as densely populated (283.2 vs 199.5 people per km²). People in Italy live 2.6 years longer on average (84.0 vs 81.4). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $43,309).
