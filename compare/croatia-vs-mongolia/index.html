Croatia has 9% more people than Mongolia: 3,876,200 vs 3,568,978, a gap of 307,222. Mongolia is growing faster (+1.25% vs +0.26% a year), adding about 44,466 people a year against +10,013. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Mongolia would overtake Croatia in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Croatia is the older society: 23.6% aged 65+ against 5.4% in Mongolia, while Mongolia has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 13.7%). Croatia is 30.9× as densely populated (69.0 vs 2.2 people per km²). People in Croatia live 6.5 years longer on average (78.9 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 3.8× higher in Croatia ($27,104 vs $7,108).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Croatia) and 2025 (Mongolia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
