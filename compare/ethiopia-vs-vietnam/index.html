Ethiopia has 1.3× the population of Vietnam: 135,472,051 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 33.9 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +612,685. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 22.9%). Vietnam is 2.8× as densely populated (320.2 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in Vietnam live 7.1 years longer on average (74.7 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
