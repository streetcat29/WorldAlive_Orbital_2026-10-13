DR Congo has 11% more people than Vietnam: 112,832,473 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 11.2 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +612,685. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 22.9%). Vietnam is 6.9× as densely populated (320.2 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Vietnam live 12.7 years longer on average (74.7 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 6.3× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
