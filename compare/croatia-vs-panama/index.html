Panama has 1.2× the population of Croatia: 4,571,189 vs 3,876,200, a gap of 694,989. Panama is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.26% a year), adding about 55,953 people a year against +10,013. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Croatia is the older society: 23.6% aged 65+ against 9.7% in Panama, while Panama has more children (24.5% under 15 vs 13.7%). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Croatia ($27,104 vs $19,790).
