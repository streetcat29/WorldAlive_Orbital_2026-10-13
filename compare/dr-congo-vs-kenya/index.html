DR Congo has 2.0× the population of Kenya: 112,832,473 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 55.3 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +1.93% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +1,110,192. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Kenya is 2.0× as densely populated (95.3 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Kenya live 1.8 years longer on average (63.8 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 2.9× higher in Kenya ($2,363 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
