Burkina Faso has 11% more people than Sri Lanka: 24,074,580 vs 21,756,000, a gap of 2.3 million. Burkina Faso is growing faster (+2.21% vs −0.73% a year), adding about 531,626 people a year against −159,415. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Sri Lanka is the older society: 12.5% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Burkina Faso, while Burkina Faso has more children (41.2% under 15 vs 21.7%). Sri Lanka is 4.2× as densely populated (356.2 vs 84.2 people per km²). People in Sri Lanka live 16.4 years longer on average (77.7 vs 61.3). GDP per capita is 4.4× higher in Sri Lanka ($5,002 vs $1,148).
