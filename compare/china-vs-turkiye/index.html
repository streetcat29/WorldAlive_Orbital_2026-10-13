China vs Türkiye

China has 16.4× the population of Türkiye: 1,406,585,000 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 1.32 billion. Türkiye is growing faster (+0.42% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 360,651 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 1.4× as densely populated (150.3 vs 110.9 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $13,862).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
