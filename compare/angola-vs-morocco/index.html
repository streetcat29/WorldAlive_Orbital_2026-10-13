Angola has about the same population as Morocco: 39,040,039 vs 38,430,770, a gap of 609,269. Angola is growing faster (+3.00% vs +0.91% a year), adding about 1,171,595 people a year against +351,197. Morocco is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 2.9% in Angola, while Angola has more children (44.1% under 15 vs 25.2%). Morocco is 2.9× as densely populated (84.5 vs 29.5 people per km²). People in Morocco live 10.7 years longer on average (75.5 vs 64.8). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Morocco ($4,672 vs $3,129).
