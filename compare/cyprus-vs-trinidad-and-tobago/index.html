Cyprus has about the same population as Trinidad and Tobago: 1,370,754 vs 1,367,764, a gap of 2,990. Cyprus is growing faster (+0.91% vs −0.04% a year), adding about 12,529 people a year against −569. Cyprus is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 12.9% in Trinidad and Tobago, while Trinidad and Tobago has more children (17.5% under 15 vs 16.1%). Trinidad and Tobago is 1.8× as densely populated (266.6 vs 145.6 people per km²). People in Cyprus live 8.2 years longer on average (81.8 vs 73.6). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Cyprus ($41,783 vs $18,967).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cyprus) and 2025 (Trinidad and Tobago); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
