Guinea-Bissau has 1.2× the population of Equatorial Guinea: 2,249,515 vs 1,938,431, a gap of 311,084. Equatorial Guinea is growing faster (+2.40% vs +2.16% a year), adding about 46,468 people a year against +48,686. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Equatorial Guinea would overtake Guinea-Bissau in about 64 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2089) — a projection, not a forecast. GDP per capita is 5.9× higher in Equatorial Guinea ($6,615 vs $1,124).
