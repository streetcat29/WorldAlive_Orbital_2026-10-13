France has 1.2× the population of Italy: 68,720,337 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 9.8 million. France is growing faster (+0.25% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 168,891 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 22.5% in France, while France has more children (16.2% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 1.6× as densely populated (199.5 vs 126.9 people per km²).
