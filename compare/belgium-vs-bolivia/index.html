Bolivia has 5% more people than Belgium: 12,581,843 vs 11,941,781, a gap of 640,062. Bolivia is growing faster (+1.35% vs +0.70% a year), adding about 169,667 people a year against +83,462. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Belgium is the older society: 21.0% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Bolivia, while Bolivia has more children (29.4% under 15 vs 15.7%). Belgium is 34.2× as densely populated (386.3 vs 11.3 people per km²). People in Belgium live 13.6 years longer on average (82.3 vs 68.7). GDP per capita is 11.8× higher in Belgium ($60,750 vs $5,148).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Belgium) and 2025 (Bolivia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
