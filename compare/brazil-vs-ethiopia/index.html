Brazil has 1.6× the population of Ethiopia: 212,812,405 vs 135,472,051, a gap of 77.3 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +815,392. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ethiopia would overtake Brazil in about 21 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2046) — a projection, not a forecast. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 19.4%). Ethiopia is 4.5× as densely populated (114.0 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Brazil live 8.4 years longer on average (76.0 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 11.5× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Ethiopia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
