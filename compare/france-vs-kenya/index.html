France has 1.2× the population of Kenya: 68,720,337 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 11.2 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +168,891. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake France in about 11 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2036) — a projection, not a forecast. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 16.2%). France is 1.3× as densely populated (126.9 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in France live 19.1 years longer on average (83.0 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 20.7× higher in France ($48,986 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (France) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
