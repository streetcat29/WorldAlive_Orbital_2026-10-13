Mexico has 7% more people than Japan: 131,946,900 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 8.6 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 5.1× as densely populated (341.5 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Japan live 8.8 years longer on average (84.0 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $13,889).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
