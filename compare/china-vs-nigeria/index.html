China has 5.9× the population of Nigeria: 1,406,585,000 vs 237,527,782, a gap of 1.17 billion. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Nigeria would overtake China in about 80 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2105) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 15.4%). Nigeria is 1.7× as densely populated (250.2 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in China live 23.4 years longer on average (78.0 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 11.3× higher in China ($13,862 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
