Pakistan has 4.8× the population of Colombia: 255,219,554 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 201.8 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +1.01% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +542,012. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 20.0%). Pakistan is 6.8× as densely populated (321.1 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 10.1 years longer on average (77.9 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $1,596).
