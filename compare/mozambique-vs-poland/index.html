Poland has about the same population as Mozambique: 36,435,861 vs 35,631,653, a gap of 804,208. Mozambique is growing faster (+2.85% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 1,014,184 people a year against −123,164. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Mozambique would overtake Poland in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Poland is the older society: 20.8% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Mozambique, while Mozambique has more children (44.2% under 15 vs 14.5%). Poland is 2.8× as densely populated (119.8 vs 42.8 people per km²). People in Poland live 14.6 years longer on average (78.4 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 45.3× higher in Poland ($28,420 vs $627).
