United States has 1.4× the population of Nigeria: 341,784,857 vs 237,527,782, a gap of 104.3 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +1,785,717. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Nigeria would overtake United States in about 24 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2049) — a projection, not a forecast. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 17.1%). Nigeria is 6.8× as densely populated (250.2 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 24.3 years longer on average (78.9 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 73.5× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nigeria) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
