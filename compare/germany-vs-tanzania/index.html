Germany has 1.2× the population of Tanzania: 83,491,249 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 12.9 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against −25,340. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Germany in about 6 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2031) — a projection, not a forecast. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 13.9%). Germany is 3.2× as densely populated (238.4 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in Germany live 13.6 years longer on average (80.8 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 45.9× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Germany) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
