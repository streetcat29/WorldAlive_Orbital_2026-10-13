Philippines has 2.3× the population of Sudan: 116,786,962 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 65.1 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +0.81% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +947,122. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Philippines in about 52 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2077) — a projection, not a forecast. Philippines is the older society: 5.7% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 27.1%). Philippines is 14.4× as densely populated (385.3 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Philippines live 3.4 years longer on average (69.9 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 3.6× higher in Philippines ($4,171 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Philippines) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
