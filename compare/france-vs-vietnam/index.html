Vietnam has 1.5× the population of France: 101,598,527 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 32.9 million. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against +168,891. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 16.2%). Vietnam is 2.5× as densely populated (320.2 vs 126.9 people per km²). People in France live 8.2 years longer on average (83.0 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 9.7× higher in France ($48,986 vs $5,066).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (France) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
