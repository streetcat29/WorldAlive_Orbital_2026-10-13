France has 1.3× the population of Colombia: 68,720,337 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 15.3 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +168,891. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake France in about 33 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2058) — a projection, not a forecast. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 16.2%). France is 2.7× as densely populated (126.9 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in France live 5.1 years longer on average (83.0 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 5.7× higher in France ($48,986 vs $8,562).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (France); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
