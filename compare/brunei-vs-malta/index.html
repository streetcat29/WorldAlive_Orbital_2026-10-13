Malta has 1.2× the population of Brunei: 579,704 vs 466,330, a gap of 113,374. Malta is growing faster (+1.89% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 10,960 people a year against +3,623. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Malta is the older society: 20.5% aged 65+ against 7.2% in Brunei, while Brunei has more children (20.6% under 15 vs 12.9%). Malta is 19.8× as densely populated (1,727.3 vs 87.1 people per km²). People in Malta live 7.5 years longer on average (83.0 vs 75.5). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Malta ($47,907 vs $32,235).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brunei) and 2025 (Malta); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
