India has 11.9× the population of Japan: 1,463,865,525 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 1.34 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 11.2%). India is 1.4× as densely populated (483.7 vs 341.5 people per km²). People in Japan live 11.8 years longer on average (84.0 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 13.3× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
