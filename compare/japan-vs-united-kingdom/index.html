Japan has 1.8× the population of United Kingdom: 123,366,734 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 53.9 million. United Kingdom is growing faster (+0.30% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 206,306 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, United Kingdom would overtake Japan in about 73 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2098) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 19.7% in United Kingdom, while United Kingdom has more children (17.0% under 15 vs 11.2%). People in Japan live 2.6 years longer on average (84.0 vs 81.4). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $35,951).
