Guam has 1.2× the population of Kiribati: 168,999 vs 136,488, a gap of 32,511. Kiribati is growing faster (+1.45% vs +0.73% a year), adding about 1,984 people a year against +1,226. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kiribati would overtake Guam in about 29 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2054) — a projection, not a forecast. Guam is the older society: 13.1% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Kiribati, while Kiribati has more children (34.4% under 15 vs 25.9%). Guam is 1.9× as densely populated (308.3 vs 163.6 people per km²). People in Guam live 10.8 years longer on average (77.4 vs 66.6). GDP per capita is 16.3× higher in Guam ($41,833 vs $2,559).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Guam) and 2025 (Kiribati); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
