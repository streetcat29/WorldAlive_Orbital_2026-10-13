Uruguay has 6% more people than Puerto Rico: 3,384,688 vs 3,184,835, a gap of 199,853. Uruguay is shrinking more slowly (−0.06% vs −0.55% a year), adding about -1,899 people a year against −17,637. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Puerto Rico is the older society: 25.3% aged 65+ against 16.3% in Uruguay, while Uruguay has more children (17.9% under 15 vs 11.2%). Puerto Rico is 18.7× as densely populated (361.2 vs 19.4 people per km²). People in Puerto Rico live 3.6 years longer on average (81.9 vs 78.3). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Puerto Rico ($40,620 vs $25,216).
