Sudan has about the same population as Uganda: 51,662,147 vs 51,384,894, a gap of 277,253. Uganda is growing faster (+2.70% vs +2.38% a year), adding about 1,388,391 people a year against +1,227,656. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Uganda would overtake Sudan in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Sudan is the older society: 3.4% aged 65+ against 2.2% in Uganda, while Uganda has more children (43.1% under 15 vs 40.2%). Uganda is 9.1× as densely populated (242.7 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Uganda live 2.0 years longer on average (68.5 vs 66.5).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sudan) and 2025 (Uganda); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
