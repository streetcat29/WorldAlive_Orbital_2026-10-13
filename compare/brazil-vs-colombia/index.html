Brazil has 4.0× the population of Colombia: 212,812,405 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 159.4 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +815,392. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 19.4%). Colombia is 1.9× as densely populated (47.2 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Colombia live 1.9 years longer on average (77.9 vs 76.0). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $8,562).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Colombia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
