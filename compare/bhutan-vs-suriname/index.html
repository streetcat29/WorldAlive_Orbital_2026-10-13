Bhutan has 1.2× the population of Suriname: 796,682 vs 639,850, a gap of 156,832. Suriname is growing faster (+0.85% vs +0.65% a year), adding about 5,442 people a year against +5,175. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Suriname would overtake Bhutan in about 109 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2134) — a projection, not a forecast. Suriname is the older society: 8.2% aged 65+ against 6.6% in Bhutan. Bhutan is 5.3× as densely populated (20.6 vs 3.9 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Suriname ($7,070 vs $4,493).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bhutan) and 2025 (Suriname); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
