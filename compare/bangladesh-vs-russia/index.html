Bangladesh has 1.2× the population of Russia: 175,686,899 vs 143,513,328, a gap of 32.2 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against −156,235. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 17.0%). Bangladesh is 150.2× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 1.5 years longer on average (74.9 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 6.8× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
