Japan has 1.2× the population of Vietnam: 123,366,734 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 21.8 million. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Vietnam would overtake Japan in about 18 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2043) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 11.2%). People in Japan live 9.3 years longer on average (84.0 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $5,066).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
