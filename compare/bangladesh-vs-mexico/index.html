Bangladesh has 1.3× the population of Mexico: 175,686,899 vs 131,946,900, a gap of 43.7 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +1,090,386. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 24.1%). Bangladesh is 19.8× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 66.7 people per km²). GDP per capita is 5.3× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
