Slovakia has about the same population as Mauritania: 5,413,813 vs 5,315,065, a gap of 98,748. Mauritania is growing faster (+2.78% vs −0.15% a year), adding about 147,703 people a year against −8,250. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Mauritania would overtake Slovakia in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Slovakia is the older society: 19.0% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Mauritania, while Mauritania has more children (42.4% under 15 vs 15.6%). Slovakia is 23.2× as densely populated (112.9 vs 4.9 people per km²). People in Slovakia live 9.7 years longer on average (78.4 vs 68.7). GDP per capita is 13.0× higher in Slovakia ($28,544 vs $2,198).
