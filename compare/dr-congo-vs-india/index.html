India has 13.0× the population of DR Congo: 1,463,865,525 vs 112,832,473, a gap of 1.35 billion. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +12,987,174. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, DR Congo would overtake India in about 111 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2136) — a projection, not a forecast. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 24.2%). India is 10.4× as densely populated (483.7 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in India live 10.2 years longer on average (72.2 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in India ($2,702 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (India); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
