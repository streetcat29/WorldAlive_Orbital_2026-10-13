Italy has 14% more people than Sudan: 58,915,656 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 7.3 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against −37,036. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Italy in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 7.4× as densely populated (199.5 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Italy live 17.4 years longer on average (84.0 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 37.2× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
