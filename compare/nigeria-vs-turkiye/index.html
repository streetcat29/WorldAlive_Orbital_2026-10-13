Nigeria vs Türkiye

Nigeria has 2.8× the population of Türkiye: 237,527,782 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 151.6 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 21.0%). Nigeria is 2.3× as densely populated (250.2 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 22.8 years longer on average (77.4 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 15.2× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nigeria) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
