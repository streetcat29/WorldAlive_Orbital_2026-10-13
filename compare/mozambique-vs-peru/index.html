Mozambique has 3% more people than Peru: 35,631,653 vs 34,576,665, a gap of 1.1 million. Mozambique is growing faster (+2.85% vs +1.04% a year), adding about 1,014,184 people a year against +360,692. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Peru is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Mozambique, while Mozambique has more children (44.2% under 15 vs 23.6%). Mozambique is 1.6× as densely populated (42.8 vs 26.4 people per km²). People in Peru live 14.1 years longer on average (77.9 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 15.4× higher in Peru ($9,684 vs $627).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mozambique) and 2025 (Peru); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
