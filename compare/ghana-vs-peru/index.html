Ghana has about the same population as Peru: 35,064,272 vs 34,576,665, a gap of 487,607. Ghana is growing faster (+1.83% vs +1.04% a year), adding about 642,712 people a year against +360,692. Peru is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Ghana, while Ghana has more children (35.4% under 15 vs 23.6%). Ghana is 5.6× as densely populated (148.5 vs 26.4 people per km²). People in Peru live 12.2 years longer on average (77.9 vs 65.7). GDP per capita is 3.0× higher in Peru ($9,684 vs $3,257).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ghana) and 2025 (Peru); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
