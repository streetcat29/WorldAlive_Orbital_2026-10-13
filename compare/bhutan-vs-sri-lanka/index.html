Sri Lanka has 27.3× the population of Bhutan: 21,756,000 vs 796,682, a gap of 21.0 million. Bhutan is growing faster (+0.65% vs −0.73% a year), adding about 5,175 people a year against −159,415. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Sri Lanka is the older society: 12.5% aged 65+ against 6.6% in Bhutan. Sri Lanka is 17.3× as densely populated (356.2 vs 20.6 people per km²). People in Sri Lanka live 4.4 years longer on average (77.7 vs 73.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bhutan) and 2025 (Sri Lanka); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
