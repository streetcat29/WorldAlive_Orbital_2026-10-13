Türkiye has about the same population as Germany: 85,878,556 vs 83,491,249, a gap of 2.4 million. Türkiye is growing faster (+0.42% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 360,651 people a year against −25,340. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 13.9%). Germany is 2.1× as densely populated (238.4 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in Germany live 3.4 years longer on average (80.8 vs 77.4). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $18,599).
