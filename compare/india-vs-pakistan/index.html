India has 5.7× the population of Pakistan: 1,463,865,525 vs 255,219,554, a gap of 1.21 billion. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.89% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +12,987,174. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. India is the older society: 7.4% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 24.2%). India is 1.5× as densely populated (483.7 vs 321.1 people per km²). People in India live 4.4 years longer on average (72.2 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in India ($2,702 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
