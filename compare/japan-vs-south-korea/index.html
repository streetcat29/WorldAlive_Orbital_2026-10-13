Japan has 2.4× the population of South Korea: 123,366,734 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 71.7 million. South Korea is shrinking more slowly (−0.13% vs −0.49% a year), adding about -66,458 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 20.3% in South Korea. South Korea is 1.6× as densely populated (529.8 vs 341.5 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
