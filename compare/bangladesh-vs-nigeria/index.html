Nigeria has 1.4× the population of Bangladesh: 237,527,782 vs 175,686,899, a gap of 61.8 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +1.22% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +2,137,485. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bangladesh is the older society: 6.7% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 27.6%). Bangladesh is 5.3× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 250.2 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 20.3 years longer on average (74.9 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Bangladesh ($2,597 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
