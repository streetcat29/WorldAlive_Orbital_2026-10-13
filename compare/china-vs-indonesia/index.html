China has 4.9× the population of Indonesia: 1,406,585,000 vs 285,721,236, a gap of 1.12 billion. Indonesia is growing faster (+0.78% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 2,242,079 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Indonesia would overtake China in about 167 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2192) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Indonesia, while Indonesia has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 15.4%). People in China live 6.7 years longer on average (78.0 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in China ($13,862 vs $5,060).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Indonesia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
