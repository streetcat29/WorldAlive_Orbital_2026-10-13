Kenya has 5% more people than Myanmar: 57,532,493 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 2.7 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Myanmar is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 24.1%). People in Myanmar live 3.3 years longer on average (67.1 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Kenya ($2,363 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
