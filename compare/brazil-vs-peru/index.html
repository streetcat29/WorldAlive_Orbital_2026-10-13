Brazil has 6.2× the population of Peru: 212,812,405 vs 34,576,665, a gap of 178.2 million. Peru is growing faster (+1.04% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 360,692 people a year against +815,392. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Peru, while Peru has more children (23.6% under 15 vs 19.4%). People in Peru live 1.9 years longer on average (77.9 vs 76.0).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Peru); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
