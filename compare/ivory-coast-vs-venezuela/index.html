Ivory Coast has 15% more people than Venezuela: 32,711,547 vs 28,516,896, a gap of 4.2 million. Ivory Coast is growing faster (+2.40% vs +0.39% a year), adding about 786,702 people a year against +111,571. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Venezuela is the older society: 10.0% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Ivory Coast, while Ivory Coast has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 24.9%). Ivory Coast is 3.1× as densely populated (98.0 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Venezuela live 10.6 years longer on average (72.7 vs 62.1).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ivory Coast) and 2025 (Venezuela); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
