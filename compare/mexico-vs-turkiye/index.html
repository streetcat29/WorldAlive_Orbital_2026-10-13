Mexico has 1.5× the population of Türkiye: 131,946,900 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 46.1 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 21.0%). Türkiye is 1.7× as densely populated (110.9 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 2.2 years longer on average (77.4 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $13,889).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
