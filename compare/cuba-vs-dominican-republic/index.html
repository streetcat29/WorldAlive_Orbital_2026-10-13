Dominican Republic has 5% more people than Cuba: 11,520,487 vs 10,937,203, a gap of 583,284. Dominican Republic is growing faster (+0.81% vs −0.39% a year), adding about 93,307 people a year against −42,497. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Cuba is the older society: 17.0% aged 65+ against 8.2% in Dominican Republic, while Dominican Republic has more children (26.2% under 15 vs 15.0%). Dominican Republic is 2.2× as densely populated (235.1 vs 106.2 people per km²). People in Cuba live 4.4 years longer on average (78.3 vs 73.9).
