Trinidad and Tobago has 9% more people than Eswatini: 1,367,764 vs 1,256,174, a gap of 111,590. Eswatini is growing faster (+1.07% vs −0.04% a year), adding about 13,423 people a year against −569. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Eswatini would overtake Trinidad and Tobago in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Trinidad and Tobago is the older society: 12.9% aged 65+ against 4.3% in Eswatini, while Eswatini has more children (33.0% under 15 vs 17.5%). Trinidad and Tobago is 3.7× as densely populated (266.6 vs 71.5 people per km²). People in Trinidad and Tobago live 9.4 years longer on average (73.6 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 4.6× higher in Trinidad and Tobago ($18,967 vs $4,108).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Eswatini) and 2025 (Trinidad and Tobago); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
