Republic of the Congo has 6% more people than Singapore: 6,484,437 vs 6,111,175, a gap of 373,262. Republic of the Congo is growing faster (+2.36% vs +1.22% a year), adding about 153,273 people a year against +74,771. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Singapore is the older society: 14.2% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Republic of the Congo, while Republic of the Congo has more children (39.9% under 15 vs 11.7%). Singapore is 455.2× as densely populated (8,241.8 vs 18.1 people per km²). People in Singapore live 17.3 years longer on average (83.3 vs 66.0). GDP per capita is 39.3× higher in Singapore ($98,814 vs $2,515).
