Burundi has 1.2× the population of South Sudan: 14,390,003 vs 12,188,788, a gap of 2.2 million. Burundi is growing faster (+2.41% vs +2.03% a year), adding about 346,352 people a year against +247,884. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Burundi is 29.3× as densely populated (533.1 vs 18.2 people per km²). People in Burundi live 6.1 years longer on average (63.8 vs 57.7). GDP per capita is 4.6× higher in South Sudan ($1,080 vs $234).
