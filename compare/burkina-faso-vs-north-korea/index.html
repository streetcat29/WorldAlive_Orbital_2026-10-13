North Korea has 10% more people than Burkina Faso: 26,571,036 vs 24,074,580, a gap of 2.5 million. Burkina Faso is growing faster (+2.21% vs +0.27% a year), adding about 531,626 people a year against +72,311. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Burkina Faso would overtake North Korea in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. North Korea is the older society: 12.7% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Burkina Faso, while Burkina Faso has more children (41.2% under 15 vs 18.9%). North Korea is 2.6× as densely populated (219.4 vs 84.2 people per km²). People in North Korea live 12.5 years longer on average (73.7 vs 61.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Burkina Faso) and 2025 (North Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
