Singapore has 7% more people than Liberia: 6,111,175 vs 5,731,206, a gap of 379,969. Liberia is growing faster (+2.09% vs +1.22% a year), adding about 119,629 people a year against +74,771. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Liberia would overtake Singapore in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Singapore is the older society: 14.2% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Liberia, while Liberia has more children (39.1% under 15 vs 11.7%). Singapore is 144.5× as densely populated (8,241.8 vs 57.0 people per km²). People in Singapore live 21.0 years longer on average (83.3 vs 62.3). GDP per capita is 108.0× higher in Singapore ($98,814 vs $915).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Liberia) and 2025 (Singapore); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
