Poland has about the same population as Malaysia: 36,435,861 vs 35,977,838, a gap of 458,023. Malaysia is growing faster (+1.17% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 422,638 people a year against −123,164. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Malaysia would overtake Poland in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast.
