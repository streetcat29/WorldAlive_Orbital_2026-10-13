Central African Republic has about the same population as Ireland: 5,513,282 vs 5,484,367, a gap of 28,915. Central African Republic is growing faster (+3.37% vs +1.63% a year), adding about 185,684 people a year against +89,300. Ireland is the older society: 16.2% aged 65+ against 2.2% in Central African Republic, while Central African Republic has more children (48.7% under 15 vs 18.1%). Ireland is 9.3× as densely populated (77.1 vs 8.3 people per km²). People in Ireland live 25.3 years longer on average (83.0 vs 57.7). GDP per capita is 236.6× higher in Ireland ($131,592 vs $556).
