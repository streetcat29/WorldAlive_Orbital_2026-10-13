Philippines has 2.0× the population of Italy: 116,786,962 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 57.9 million. Philippines is growing faster (+0.81% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 947,122 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 11.7%). Philippines is 1.9× as densely populated (385.3 vs 199.5 people per km²). People in Italy live 14.0 years longer on average (84.0 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 10.4× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $4,171).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Philippines); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
