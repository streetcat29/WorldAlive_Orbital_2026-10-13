Moldova has 11% more people than Slovenia: 2,360,527 vs 2,130,986, a gap of 229,541. Slovenia is growing faster (+0.17% vs −1.75% a year), adding about 3,589 people a year against −41,414. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Slovenia would overtake Moldova in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Slovenia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 16.9% in Moldova, while Moldova has more children (19.5% under 15 vs 14.5%). People in Slovenia live 11.0 years longer on average (82.3 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 4.3× higher in Slovenia ($37,376 vs $8,622).
