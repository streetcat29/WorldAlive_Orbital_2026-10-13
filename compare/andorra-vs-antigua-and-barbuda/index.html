Antigua and Barbuda has 14% more people than Andorra: 94,209 vs 82,904, a gap of 11,305. Andorra is growing faster (+1.17% vs +0.46% a year), adding about 972 people a year against +438. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Andorra would overtake Antigua and Barbuda in about 18 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2043) — a projection, not a forecast. Andorra is the older society: 16.6% aged 65+ against 12.3% in Antigua and Barbuda, while Antigua and Barbuda has more children (17.5% under 15 vs 11.6%). People in Andorra live 6.4 years longer on average (84.2 vs 77.8). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Andorra ($54,292 vs $24,819).
