Belgium has 4% more people than Dominican Republic: 11,941,781 vs 11,520,487, a gap of 421,294. Dominican Republic is growing faster (+0.81% vs +0.70% a year), adding about 93,307 people a year against +83,462. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Dominican Republic would overtake Belgium in about 32 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2057) — a projection, not a forecast. Belgium is the older society: 21.0% aged 65+ against 8.2% in Dominican Republic, while Dominican Republic has more children (26.2% under 15 vs 15.7%). Belgium is 1.6× as densely populated (386.3 vs 235.1 people per km²). People in Belgium live 8.4 years longer on average (82.3 vs 73.9). GDP per capita is 5.5× higher in Belgium ($60,750 vs $11,059).
