Nigeria has 1.7× the population of Russia: 237,527,782 vs 143,513,328, a gap of 94.0 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against −156,235. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 17.0%). Nigeria is 28.5× as densely populated (250.2 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 18.8 years longer on average (73.4 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 14.3× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nigeria) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
