Curaçao has 14% more people than Kiribati: 156,263 vs 136,488, a gap of 19,775. Kiribati is growing faster (+1.45% vs +0.19% a year), adding about 1,984 people a year against +296. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kiribati would overtake Curaçao in about 11 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2036) — a projection, not a forecast. Curaçao is the older society: 17.3% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Kiribati, while Kiribati has more children (34.4% under 15 vs 14.6%). Curaçao is 2.1× as densely populated (348.3 vs 163.6 people per km²). People in Curaçao live 10.4 years longer on average (77.0 vs 66.6). GDP per capita is 8.9× higher in Curaçao ($22,833 vs $2,559).
