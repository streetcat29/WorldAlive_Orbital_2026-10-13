Colombia has 1.5× the population of Peru: 53,425,635 vs 34,576,665, a gap of 18.8 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+1.01% vs +1.04% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Colombia is 1.8× as densely populated (47.2 vs 26.4 people per km²).
