Cuba has about the same population as Tajikistan: 10,937,203 vs 10,786,734, a gap of 150,469. Tajikistan is growing faster (+1.83% vs −0.39% a year), adding about 197,606 people a year against −42,497. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tajikistan would overtake Cuba in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Cuba is the older society: 17.0% aged 65+ against 4.1% in Tajikistan, while Tajikistan has more children (35.9% under 15 vs 15.0%). Cuba is 1.4× as densely populated (106.2 vs 74.9 people per km²). People in Cuba live 6.3 years longer on average (78.3 vs 71.9). GDP per capita is 5.9× higher in Cuba ($9,605 vs $1,637).
