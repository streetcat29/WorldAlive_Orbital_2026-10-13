Japan has 1.7× the population of Tanzania: 123,366,734 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 52.8 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Japan in about 17 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2042) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 4.5× as densely populated (341.5 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in Japan live 16.8 years longer on average (84.0 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 27.3× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
