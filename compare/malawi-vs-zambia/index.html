Malawi has about the same population as Zambia: 22,216,120 vs 21,913,874, a gap of 302,246. Zambia is growing faster (+2.77% vs +2.56% a year), adding about 607,255 people a year against +568,034. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Zambia would overtake Malawi in about 6 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2031) — a projection, not a forecast. Malawi is 8.0× as densely populated (223.8 vs 27.9 people per km²). People in Malawi live 1.0 years longer on average (67.6 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Zambia ($1,318 vs $672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Malawi) and 2025 (Zambia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
