Morocco has 4% more people than Saudi Arabia: 38,430,770 vs 36,973,555, a gap of 1.5 million. Saudi Arabia is growing faster (+4.63% vs +0.91% a year), adding about 1,712,320 people a year against +351,197. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Saudi Arabia would overtake Morocco in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Morocco is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Saudi Arabia. Morocco is 5.4× as densely populated (84.5 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Saudi Arabia live 3.5 years longer on average (79.0 vs 75.5). GDP per capita is 7.4× higher in Saudi Arabia ($34,537 vs $4,672).
