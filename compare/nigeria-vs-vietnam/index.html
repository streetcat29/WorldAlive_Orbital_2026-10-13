Nigeria has 2.3× the population of Vietnam: 237,527,782 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 135.9 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +612,685. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 22.9%). People in Vietnam live 20.1 years longer on average (74.7 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 4.1× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nigeria) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
