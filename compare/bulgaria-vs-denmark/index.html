Bulgaria has 7% more people than Denmark: 6,433,302 vs 6,009,169, a gap of 424,133. Denmark is growing faster (+0.54% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 32,263 people a year against −8,114. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Denmark would overtake Bulgaria in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Bulgaria is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 21.1% in Denmark, while Denmark has more children (15.6% under 15 vs 14.4%). Denmark is 2.5× as densely populated (148.7 vs 59.4 people per km²). People in Denmark live 6.5 years longer on average (82.3 vs 75.8). GDP per capita is 3.8× higher in Denmark ($76,970 vs $20,328).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bulgaria) and 2025 (Denmark); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
