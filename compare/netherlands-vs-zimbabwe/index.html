Netherlands has 7% more people than Zimbabwe: 18,087,633 vs 16,950,795, a gap of 1.1 million. Zimbabwe is growing faster (+1.88% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 319,413 people a year against +94,394. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Zimbabwe would overtake Netherlands in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Netherlands is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Zimbabwe, while Zimbabwe has more children (40.3% under 15 vs 14.9%). Netherlands is 12.6× as densely populated (531.0 vs 42.2 people per km²). People in Netherlands live 18.9 years longer on average (82.0 vs 63.1). GDP per capita is 24.4× higher in Netherlands ($73,684 vs $3,021).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Netherlands) and 2025 (Zimbabwe); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
