Burundi has 14% more people than Bolivia: 14,390,003 vs 12,581,843, a gap of 1.8 million. Burundi is growing faster (+2.41% vs +1.35% a year), adding about 346,352 people a year against +169,667. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bolivia is the older society: 5.7% aged 65+ against 2.5% in Burundi, while Burundi has more children (44.0% under 15 vs 29.4%). Burundi is 47.2× as densely populated (533.1 vs 11.3 people per km²). People in Bolivia live 4.9 years longer on average (68.7 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 22.0× higher in Bolivia ($5,148 vs $234).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bolivia) and 2025 (Burundi); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
