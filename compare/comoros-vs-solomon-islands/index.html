Comoros has 5% more people than Solomon Islands: 882,847 vs 838,645, a gap of 44,202. Solomon Islands is growing faster (+2.35% vs +1.85% a year), adding about 19,676 people a year against +16,370. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Solomon Islands would overtake Comoros in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Comoros is 16.0× as densely populated (457.0 vs 28.6 people per km²). People in Solomon Islands live 3.7 years longer on average (70.7 vs 67.0).
