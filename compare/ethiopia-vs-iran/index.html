Ethiopia has 1.5× the population of Iran: 135,472,051 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 43.1 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +853,875. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 22.0%). Ethiopia is 2.0× as densely populated (114.0 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 10.3 years longer on average (77.9 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 4.2× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
