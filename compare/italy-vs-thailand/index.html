Thailand has 1.2× the population of Italy: 71,619,863 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 12.7 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (−0.06% vs −0.07% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 16.0% in Thailand, while Thailand has more children (14.4% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 1.4× as densely populated (199.5 vs 140.3 people per km²). People in Italy live 7.4 years longer on average (84.0 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 5.4× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
