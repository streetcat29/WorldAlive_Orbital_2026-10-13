Indonesia has 3.1× the population of Iran: 285,721,236 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 193.3 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against +2,242,079. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Indonesia, while Indonesia has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 22.0%). Indonesia is 2.7× as densely populated (148.6 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 6.6 years longer on average (77.9 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Indonesia ($5,060 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
