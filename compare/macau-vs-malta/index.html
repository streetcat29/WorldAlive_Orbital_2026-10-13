Macau has 1.2× the population of Malta: 685,900 vs 579,704, a gap of 106,196. Malta is growing faster (+1.89% vs −0.16% a year), adding about 10,960 people a year against −1,099. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Malta would overtake Macau in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Malta is the older society: 20.5% aged 65+ against 15.1% in Macau, while Macau has more children (13.7% under 15 vs 12.9%). Macau is 11.9× as densely populated (20,569.7 vs 1,727.3 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Macau ($75,902 vs $47,907).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Macau) and 2025 (Malta); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
