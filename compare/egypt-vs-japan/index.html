Japan has 4% more people than Egypt: 123,366,734 vs 118,365,995, a gap of 5.0 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Egypt would overtake Japan in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 3.0× as densely populated (341.5 vs 115.1 people per km²). People in Japan live 12.2 years longer on average (84.0 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 11.7× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
