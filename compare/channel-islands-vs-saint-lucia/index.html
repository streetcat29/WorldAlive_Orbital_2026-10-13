Saint Lucia has 7% more people than Channel Islands: 180,149 vs 168,466, a gap of 11,683. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.20% vs +0.23% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. GDP per capita is 5.1× higher in Channel Islands ($74,586 vs $14,746).
