India has 6.9× the population of Brazil: 1,463,865,525 vs 212,812,405, a gap of 1.25 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +815,392. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 19.4%). India is 19.1× as densely populated (483.7 vs 25.3 people per km²). People in Brazil live 3.8 years longer on average (76.0 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 4.0× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (India); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
