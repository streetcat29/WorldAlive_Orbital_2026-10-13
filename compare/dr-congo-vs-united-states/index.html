United States has 3.0× the population of DR Congo: 341,784,857 vs 112,832,473, a gap of 229.0 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +1,785,717. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, DR Congo would overtake United States in about 41 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2066) — a projection, not a forecast. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 17.1%). People in United States live 16.8 years longer on average (78.9 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 111.6× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
