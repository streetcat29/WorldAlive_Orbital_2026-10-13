Brazil has 4.1× the population of Sudan: 212,812,405 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 161.2 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +0.38% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +815,392. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Brazil in about 71 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2096) — a projection, not a forecast. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 19.4%). People in Brazil live 9.5 years longer on average (76.0 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 9.2× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Sudan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
