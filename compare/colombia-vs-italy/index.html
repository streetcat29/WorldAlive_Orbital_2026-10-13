Italy has 10% more people than Colombia: 58,915,656 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 5.5 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against −37,036. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Colombia would overtake Italy in about 9 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2034) — a projection, not a forecast. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 10.2% in Colombia, while Colombia has more children (20.0% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 4.2× as densely populated (199.5 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Italy live 6.0 years longer on average (84.0 vs 77.9). GDP per capita is 5.1× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $8,562).
