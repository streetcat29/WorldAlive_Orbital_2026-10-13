Eritrea has 13% more people than Puerto Rico: 3,607,003 vs 3,184,835, a gap of 422,168. Eritrea is growing faster (+2.00% vs −0.55% a year), adding about 72,116 people a year against −17,637. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Puerto Rico is the older society: 25.3% aged 65+ against 4.3% in Eritrea, while Eritrea has more children (37.6% under 15 vs 11.2%). Puerto Rico is 12.6× as densely populated (361.2 vs 28.6 people per km²). People in Puerto Rico live 13.0 years longer on average (81.9 vs 68.9). GDP per capita is 59.0× higher in Puerto Rico ($40,620 vs $689).
