São Tomé and Príncipe has 1.3× the population of Saint Lucia: 240,254 vs 180,149, a gap of 60,105. São Tomé and Príncipe is growing faster (+1.98% vs +0.23% a year), adding about 4,765 people a year against +405. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Saint Lucia is the older society: 10.0% aged 65+ against 3.9% in São Tomé and Príncipe, while São Tomé and Príncipe has more children (37.5% under 15 vs 17.2%). People in Saint Lucia live 2.9 years longer on average (72.8 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 3.6× higher in Saint Lucia ($14,746 vs $4,084).
