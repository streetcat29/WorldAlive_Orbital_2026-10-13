Gibraltar has 11% more people than Marshall Islands: 40,126 vs 36,282, a gap of 3,844. Gibraltar is growing faster (+2.01% vs −3.43% a year), adding about 805 people a year against −1,244. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Gibraltar is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 4.9% in Marshall Islands, while Marshall Islands has more children (33.9% under 15 vs 17.6%). Gibraltar is 17.8× as densely populated (3,847.1 vs 215.7 people per km²). People in Gibraltar live 16.6 years longer on average (83.6 vs 67.1).
