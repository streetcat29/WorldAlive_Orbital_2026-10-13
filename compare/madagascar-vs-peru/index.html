Peru has 6% more people than Madagascar: 34,576,665 vs 32,740,678, a gap of 1.8 million. Madagascar is growing faster (+2.40% vs +1.04% a year), adding about 785,059 people a year against +360,692. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Madagascar would overtake Peru in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Peru is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Madagascar, while Madagascar has more children (38.9% under 15 vs 23.6%). Madagascar is 2.0× as densely populated (53.6 vs 26.4 people per km²). People in Peru live 14.1 years longer on average (77.9 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 16.2× higher in Peru ($9,684 vs $599).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Madagascar) and 2025 (Peru); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
