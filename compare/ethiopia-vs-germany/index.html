Ethiopia has 1.6× the population of Germany: 135,472,051 vs 83,491,249, a gap of 52.0 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against −25,340. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 13.9%). Germany is 2.1× as densely populated (238.4 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in Germany live 13.2 years longer on average (80.8 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 64.9× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (Germany); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
