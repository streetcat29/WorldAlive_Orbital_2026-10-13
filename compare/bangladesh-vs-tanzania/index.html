Bangladesh has 2.5× the population of Tanzania: 175,686,899 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 105.1 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +1.22% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +2,137,485. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Bangladesh in about 56 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2081) — a projection, not a forecast. Bangladesh is the older society: 6.7% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 27.6%). Bangladesh is 17.5× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 7.7 years longer on average (74.9 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Bangladesh ($2,597 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
