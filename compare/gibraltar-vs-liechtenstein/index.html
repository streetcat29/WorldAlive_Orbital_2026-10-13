Liechtenstein has about the same population as Gibraltar: 41,024 vs 40,126, a gap of 898. Gibraltar is growing faster (+2.01% vs +1.41% a year), adding about 805 people a year against +578. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Gibraltar would overtake Liechtenstein in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast.
