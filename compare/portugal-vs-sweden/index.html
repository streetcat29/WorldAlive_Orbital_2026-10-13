Portugal has about the same population as Sweden: 10,804,871 vs 10,596,620, a gap of 208,251. Portugal is growing faster (+1.03% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 110,756 people a year against +26,945. Portugal is the older society: 24.9% aged 65+ against 20.9% in Sweden, while Sweden has more children (16.7% under 15 vs 12.7%). Portugal is 4.5× as densely populated (115.5 vs 25.9 people per km²). People in Sweden live 1.7 years longer on average (84.1 vs 82.4). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Sweden ($63,133 vs $32,082).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Portugal) and 2025 (Sweden); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
