Singapore has about the same population as Denmark: 6,111,175 vs 6,009,169, a gap of 102,006. Singapore is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.54% a year), adding about 74,771 people a year against +32,263. Denmark is the older society: 21.1% aged 65+ against 14.2% in Singapore. Singapore is 55.4× as densely populated (8,241.8 vs 148.7 people per km²). People in Singapore live 1.1 years longer on average (83.3 vs 82.3). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Singapore ($98,814 vs $76,970).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Denmark) and 2025 (Singapore); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
