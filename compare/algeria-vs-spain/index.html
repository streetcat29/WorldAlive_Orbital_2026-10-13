Spain has 4% more people than Algeria: 49,355,143 vs 47,435,312, a gap of 1.9 million. Algeria is growing faster (+1.32% vs +1.03% a year), adding about 625,105 people a year against +508,918. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Algeria would overtake Spain in about 14 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2039) — a projection, not a forecast. Spain is the older society: 21.6% aged 65+ against 6.8% in Algeria, while Algeria has more children (29.9% under 15 vs 12.6%). Spain is 5.0× as densely populated (96.8 vs 19.4 people per km²). People in Spain live 7.4 years longer on average (83.9 vs 76.5). GDP per capita is 6.4× higher in Spain ($38,627 vs $6,051).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Algeria) and 2025 (Spain); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
