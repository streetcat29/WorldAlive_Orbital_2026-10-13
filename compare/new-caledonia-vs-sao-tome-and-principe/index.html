New Caledonia has 1.2× the population of São Tomé and Príncipe: 295,333 vs 240,254, a gap of 55,079. São Tomé and Príncipe is growing faster (+1.98% vs +0.92% a year), adding about 4,765 people a year against +2,706. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, São Tomé and Príncipe would overtake New Caledonia in about 19 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2044) — a projection, not a forecast. New Caledonia is the older society: 11.8% aged 65+ against 3.9% in São Tomé and Príncipe, while São Tomé and Príncipe has more children (37.5% under 15 vs 21.1%). São Tomé and Príncipe is 15.2× as densely populated (240.5 vs 15.9 people per km²). People in New Caledonia live 9.0 years longer on average (78.9 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 7.2× higher in New Caledonia ($29,213 vs $4,084).
