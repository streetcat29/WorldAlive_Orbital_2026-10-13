China has 25.6× the population of Myanmar: 1,406,585,000 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 1.35 billion. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 1.8× as densely populated (150.3 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in China live 10.9 years longer on average (78.0 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 9.3× higher in China ($13,862 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
