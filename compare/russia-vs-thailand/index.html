Russia has 2.0× the population of Thailand: 143,513,328 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 71.9 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (−0.11% vs −0.07% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 16.0% in Thailand. Thailand is 16.0× as densely populated (140.3 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Thailand live 3.1 years longer on average (76.6 vs 73.4). GDP per capita is 2.2× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Russia) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
