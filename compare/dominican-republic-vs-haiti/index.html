Haiti has 3% more people than Dominican Republic: 11,906,095 vs 11,520,487, a gap of 385,608. Haiti is growing faster (+1.13% vs +0.81% a year), adding about 134,293 people a year against +93,307. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Dominican Republic is the older society: 8.2% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Haiti, while Haiti has more children (30.8% under 15 vs 26.2%). Haiti is 1.8× as densely populated (422.3 vs 235.1 people per km²). People in Dominican Republic live 8.7 years longer on average (73.9 vs 65.1). GDP per capita is 4.1× higher in Dominican Republic ($11,059 vs $2,694).
