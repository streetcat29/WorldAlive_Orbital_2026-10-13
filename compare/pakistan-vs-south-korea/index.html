Pakistan has 4.9× the population of South Korea: 255,219,554 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 203.5 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 1.7× as densely populated (529.8 vs 321.1 people per km²). People in South Korea live 15.8 years longer on average (83.6 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 22.7× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Pakistan) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
