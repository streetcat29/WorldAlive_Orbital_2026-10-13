Liberia has 4% more people than Central African Republic: 5,731,206 vs 5,513,282, a gap of 217,924. Central African Republic is growing faster (+3.37% vs +2.09% a year), adding about 185,684 people a year against +119,629. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Central African Republic would overtake Liberia in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Liberia is the older society: 3.3% aged 65+ against 2.2% in Central African Republic, while Central African Republic has more children (48.7% under 15 vs 39.1%). Liberia is 6.9× as densely populated (57.0 vs 8.3 people per km²). People in Liberia live 4.7 years longer on average (62.3 vs 57.7). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Liberia ($915 vs $556).
