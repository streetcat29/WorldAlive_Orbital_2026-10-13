Albania has 10% more people than Slovenia: 2,349,580 vs 2,130,986, a gap of 218,594. Slovenia is growing faster (+0.17% vs −1.17% a year), adding about 3,589 people a year against −27,388. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Slovenia would overtake Albania in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Slovenia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 17.6% in Albania, while Albania has more children (16.6% under 15 vs 14.5%). People in Slovenia live 2.5 years longer on average (82.3 vs 79.8). GDP per capita is 2.9× higher in Slovenia ($37,376 vs $12,998).
