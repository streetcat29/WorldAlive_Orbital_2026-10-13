Brazil has 7.5× the population of Venezuela: 212,812,405 vs 28,516,896, a gap of 184.3 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.38% vs +0.39% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Brazil is the older society: 11.5% aged 65+ against 10.0% in Venezuela, while Venezuela has more children (24.9% under 15 vs 19.4%). People in Brazil live 3.3 years longer on average (76.0 vs 72.7). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in Brazil ($10,713 vs $3,495).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brazil) and 2025 (Venezuela); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
