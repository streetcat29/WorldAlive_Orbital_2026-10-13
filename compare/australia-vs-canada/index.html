Australia vs Canada

Canada has 1.5× the population of Australia: 41,651,653 vs 27,614,411, a gap of 14.0 million. Australia is growing faster (+1.53% vs +0.94% a year), adding about 423,354 people a year against +391,155. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Australia would overtake Canada in about 69 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2094) — a projection, not a forecast. Canada is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 18.1% in Australia, while Australia has more children (17.7% under 15 vs 15.0%). Canada is 1.3× as densely populated (4.6 vs 3.5 people per km²).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Australia) and 2025 (Canada); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
