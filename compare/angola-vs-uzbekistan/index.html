Angola has 5% more people than Uzbekistan: 39,040,039 vs 37,053,428, a gap of 2.0 million. Angola is growing faster (+3.00% vs +1.88% a year), adding about 1,171,595 people a year against +698,104. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Uzbekistan is the older society: 6.1% aged 65+ against 2.9% in Angola, while Angola has more children (44.1% under 15 vs 31.3%). Uzbekistan is 2.7× as densely populated (80.9 vs 29.5 people per km²). People in Uzbekistan live 7.7 years longer on average (72.5 vs 64.8). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Uzbekistan ($3,968 vs $3,129).
