Papua New Guinea has about the same population as Sweden: 10,762,817 vs 10,596,620, a gap of 166,197. Papua New Guinea is growing faster (+1.75% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 187,947 people a year against +26,945. Sweden is the older society: 20.9% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Papua New Guinea, while Papua New Guinea has more children (33.1% under 15 vs 16.7%). People in Sweden live 17.8 years longer on average (84.1 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 20.9× higher in Sweden ($63,133 vs $3,020).
