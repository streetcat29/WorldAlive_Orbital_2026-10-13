Cape Verde has 1.2× the population of Belize: 527,326 vs 422,924, a gap of 104,402. Belize is growing faster (+1.39% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 5,893 people a year against +2,455. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Belize would overtake Cape Verde in about 24 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2049) — a projection, not a forecast. Cape Verde is the older society: 7.2% aged 65+ against 5.2% in Belize, while Belize has more children (26.3% under 15 vs 24.7%). Cape Verde is 7.2× as densely populated (129.6 vs 18.0 people per km²). People in Cape Verde live 2.5 years longer on average (76.2 vs 73.7). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Belize ($7,865 vs $5,796).
