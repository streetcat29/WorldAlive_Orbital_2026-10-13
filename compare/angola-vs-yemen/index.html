Yemen has 7% more people than Angola: 41,773,878 vs 39,040,039, a gap of 2.7 million. Angola is growing faster (+3.00% vs +2.89% a year), adding about 1,171,595 people a year against +1,208,013. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Angola would overtake Yemen in about 62 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2087) — a projection, not a forecast. Yemen is 2.5× as densely populated (74.6 vs 29.5 people per km²). People in Yemen live 4.6 years longer on average (69.4 vs 64.8). GDP per capita is 4.9× higher in Angola ($3,129 vs $634).
