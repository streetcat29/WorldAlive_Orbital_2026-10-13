Canada has 8% more people than Morocco: 41,651,653 vs 38,430,770, a gap of 3.2 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.94% vs +0.91% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Canada is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Morocco, while Morocco has more children (25.2% under 15 vs 15.0%). Morocco is 18.5× as densely populated (84.5 vs 4.6 people per km²). People in Canada live 6.6 years longer on average (82.1 vs 75.5). GDP per capita is 11.9× higher in Canada ($55,698 vs $4,672).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Canada) and 2025 (Morocco); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
