Equatorial Guinea has 5% more people than Latvia: 1,938,431 vs 1,847,785, a gap of 90,646. Equatorial Guinea is growing faster (+2.40% vs −0.99% a year), adding about 46,468 people a year against −18,249. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Latvia is the older society: 22.2% aged 65+ against 3.8% in Equatorial Guinea, while Equatorial Guinea has more children (37.1% under 15 vs 15.2%). Equatorial Guinea is 2.2× as densely populated (65.9 vs 30.3 people per km²). People in Latvia live 12.5 years longer on average (76.4 vs 63.9). GDP per capita is 4.0× higher in Latvia ($26,312 vs $6,615).
