Egypt has 2.5× the population of Algeria: 118,365,995 vs 47,435,312, a gap of 70.9 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +1.32% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +625,105. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Algeria is the older society: 6.8% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 29.9%). Egypt is 5.9× as densely populated (115.1 vs 19.4 people per km²). People in Algeria live 4.7 years longer on average (76.5 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Algeria ($6,051 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Algeria) and 2025 (Egypt); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
