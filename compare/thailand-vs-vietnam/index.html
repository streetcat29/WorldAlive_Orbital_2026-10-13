Vietnam has 1.4× the population of Thailand: 101,598,527 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 30.0 million. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 14.4%). Vietnam is 2.3× as densely populated (320.2 vs 140.3 people per km²). People in Thailand live 1.8 years longer on average (76.6 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $5,066).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Thailand) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
