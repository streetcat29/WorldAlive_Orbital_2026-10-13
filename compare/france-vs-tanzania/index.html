Tanzania has about the same population as France: 70,545,865 vs 68,720,337, a gap of 1.8 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +0.25% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +168,891. France is the older society: 22.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 16.2%). France is 1.7× as densely populated (126.9 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in France live 15.8 years longer on average (83.0 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 37.1× higher in France ($48,986 vs $1,319).
