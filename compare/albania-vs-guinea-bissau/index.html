Albania has 4% more people than Guinea-Bissau: 2,349,580 vs 2,249,515, a gap of 100,065. Guinea-Bissau is growing faster (+2.16% vs −1.17% a year), adding about 48,686 people a year against −27,388. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Guinea-Bissau would overtake Albania in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Albania is the older society: 17.6% aged 65+ against 3.2% in Guinea-Bissau, while Guinea-Bissau has more children (38.3% under 15 vs 16.6%). People in Albania live 15.5 years longer on average (79.8 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 11.6× higher in Albania ($12,998 vs $1,124).
