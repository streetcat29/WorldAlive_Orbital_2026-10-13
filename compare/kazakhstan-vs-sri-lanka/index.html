Sri Lanka has 4% more people than Kazakhstan: 21,756,000 vs 20,843,754, a gap of 912,246. Kazakhstan is growing faster (+1.21% vs −0.73% a year), adding about 252,709 people a year against −159,415. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kazakhstan would overtake Sri Lanka in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Sri Lanka is the older society: 12.5% aged 65+ against 9.0% in Kazakhstan, while Kazakhstan has more children (29.1% under 15 vs 21.7%). Sri Lanka is 47.3× as densely populated (356.2 vs 7.5 people per km²). People in Sri Lanka live 3.1 years longer on average (77.7 vs 74.5). GDP per capita is 2.9× higher in Kazakhstan ($14,692 vs $5,002).
