China has 15.2× the population of Iran: 1,406,585,000 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 1.31 billion. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Iran, while Iran has more children (22.0% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 2.7× as densely populated (150.3 vs 55.8 people per km²). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in China ($13,862 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Iran); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
