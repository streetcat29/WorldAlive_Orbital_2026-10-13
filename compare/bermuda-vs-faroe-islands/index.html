Bermuda has 1.2× the population of Faroe Islands: 64,555 vs 54,900, a gap of 9,655. Faroe Islands is growing faster (+0.47% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 261 people a year against −81. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Faroe Islands would overtake Bermuda in about 27 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2052) — a projection, not a forecast. Bermuda is the older society: 22.6% aged 65+ against 17.8% in Faroe Islands, while Faroe Islands has more children (19.9% under 15 vs 13.4%). Bermuda is 30.2× as densely populated (1,198.1 vs 39.7 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Bermuda ($142,250 vs $74,175).
