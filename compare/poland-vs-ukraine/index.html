Ukraine has 7% more people than Poland: 38,980,376 vs 36,435,861, a gap of 2.5 million. Ukraine is growing faster (+2.92% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 1,136,565 people a year against −123,164. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Poland is the older society: 20.8% aged 65+ against 19.0% in Ukraine. Poland is 1.8× as densely populated (119.8 vs 65.1 people per km²). People in Poland live 3.7 years longer on average (78.4 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 4.8× higher in Poland ($28,420 vs $5,866).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Poland) and 2025 (Ukraine); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
