Thailand has 1.4× the population of South Korea: 71,619,863 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 19.9 million. Thailand is shrinking more slowly (−0.07% vs −0.13% a year), adding about -48,132 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 16.0% in Thailand, while Thailand has more children (14.4% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 3.8× as densely populated (529.8 vs 140.3 people per km²). People in South Korea live 7.1 years longer on average (83.6 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 4.5× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Korea) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
