India has 26.7× the population of Myanmar: 1,463,865,525 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 1.41 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. India is 5.8× as densely populated (483.7 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in India live 5.1 years longer on average (72.2 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 1.8× higher in India ($2,702 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
