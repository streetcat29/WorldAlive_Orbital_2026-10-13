United States has 3.4× the population of Vietnam: 341,784,857 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 240.2 million. Vietnam is growing faster (+0.60% vs +0.52% a year), adding about 612,685 people a year against +1,785,717. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 9.5% in Vietnam, while Vietnam has more children (22.9% under 15 vs 17.1%). Vietnam is 8.7× as densely populated (320.2 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 4.2 years longer on average (78.9 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 17.8× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $5,066).
