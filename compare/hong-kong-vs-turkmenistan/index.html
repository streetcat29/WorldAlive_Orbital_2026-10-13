Turkmenistan has about the same population as Hong Kong: 7,618,847 vs 7,498,900, a gap of 119,947. Turkmenistan is growing faster (+1.65% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 125,375 people a year against −25,158. Hong Kong is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Turkmenistan, while Turkmenistan has more children (31.2% under 15 vs 10.3%). Hong Kong is 458.0× as densely populated (7,177.2 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Hong Kong live 15.2 years longer on average (85.4 vs 70.2). GDP per capita is 8.7× higher in Hong Kong ($56,983 vs $6,540).
