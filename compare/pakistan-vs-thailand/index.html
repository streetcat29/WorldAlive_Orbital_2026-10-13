Pakistan has 3.6× the population of Thailand: 255,219,554 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 183.6 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 14.4%). Pakistan is 2.3× as densely populated (321.1 vs 140.3 people per km²). People in Thailand live 8.8 years longer on average (76.6 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 5.0× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Pakistan) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
