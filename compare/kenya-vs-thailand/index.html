Thailand has 1.2× the population of Kenya: 71,619,863 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 14.1 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against −48,132. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Thailand in about 11 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2036) — a projection, not a forecast. Thailand is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 14.4%). Thailand is 1.5× as densely populated (140.3 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in Thailand live 12.7 years longer on average (76.6 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 3.4× higher in Thailand ($8,057 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Thailand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
