Mexico has 1.4× the population of Iran: 131,946,900 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 39.5 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against +1,090,386. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. People in Iran live 2.6 years longer on average (77.9 vs 75.3). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $3,924).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
