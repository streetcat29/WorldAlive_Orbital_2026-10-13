Palau has 1.5× the population of Nauru: 17,663 vs 12,025, a gap of 5,638. Nauru is growing faster (+0.65% vs −0.18% a year), adding about 78 people a year against −32. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Nauru would overtake Palau in about 46 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2071) — a projection, not a forecast. Palau is the older society: 11.8% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Nauru, while Nauru has more children (37.2% under 15 vs 17.9%). Nauru is 15.4× as densely populated (593.8 vs 38.5 people per km²). People in Palau live 7.1 years longer on average (69.4 vs 62.3). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Palau ($19,532 vs $14,640).
