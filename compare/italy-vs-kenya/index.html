Italy has about the same population as Kenya: 58,915,656 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 1.4 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against −37,036. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Italy in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 2.1× as densely populated (199.5 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in Italy live 20.1 years longer on average (84.0 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 18.3× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $2,363).
