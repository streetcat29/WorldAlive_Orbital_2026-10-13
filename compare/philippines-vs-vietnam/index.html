Philippines has 15% more people than Vietnam: 116,786,962 vs 101,598,527, a gap of 15.2 million. Philippines is growing faster (+0.81% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 947,122 people a year against +612,685. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Vietnam is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 22.9%). People in Vietnam live 4.8 years longer on average (74.7 vs 69.9).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Philippines) and 2025 (Vietnam); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
