Bangladesh has 2.1× the population of Germany: 175,686,899 vs 83,491,249, a gap of 92.2 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against −25,340. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 13.9%). Bangladesh is 5.5× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 238.4 people per km²). People in Germany live 5.9 years longer on average (80.8 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 23.3× higher in Germany ($60,496 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Germany); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
