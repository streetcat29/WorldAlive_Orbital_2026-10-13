Türkiye has 1.5× the population of Kenya: 85,878,556 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 28.3 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +360,651. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Türkiye in about 27 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2052) — a projection, not a forecast. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 21.0%). People in Türkiye live 13.6 years longer on average (77.4 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 7.9× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
