Tanzania has 1.3× the population of Myanmar: 70,545,865 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 15.7 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Myanmar is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 24.1%).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
