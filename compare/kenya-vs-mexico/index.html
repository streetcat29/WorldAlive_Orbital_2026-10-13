Mexico has 2.3× the population of Kenya: 131,946,900 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 74.4 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.83% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +1,090,386. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Mexico in about 75 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2100) — a projection, not a forecast. Mexico is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 24.1%). Kenya is 1.4× as densely populated (95.3 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Mexico live 11.4 years longer on average (75.3 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 5.9× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Kenya) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
