Ethiopia has 1.9× the population of United Kingdom: 135,472,051 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 66.0 million. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 2.5× as densely populated (283.2 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 13.8 years longer on average (81.4 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 61.8× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ethiopia) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
