Finland has about the same population as Central African Republic: 5,646,436 vs 5,513,282, a gap of 133,154. Central African Republic is growing faster (+3.37% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 185,684 people a year against +26,587. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Central African Republic would overtake Finland in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Finland is the older society: 24.2% aged 65+ against 2.2% in Central African Republic, while Central African Republic has more children (48.7% under 15 vs 14.3%). Finland is 2.2× as densely populated (18.4 vs 8.3 people per km²). People in Finland live 24.7 years longer on average (82.3 vs 57.7). GDP per capita is 101.0× higher in Finland ($56,149 vs $556).
