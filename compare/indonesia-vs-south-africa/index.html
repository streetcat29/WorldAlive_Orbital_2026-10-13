Indonesia vs South Africa

Indonesia has 4.4× the population of South Africa: 285,721,236 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 221.0 million. South Africa is growing faster (+1.15% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 744,395 people a year against +2,242,079. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Indonesia is 2.9× as densely populated (148.6 vs 52.1 people per km²). People in Indonesia live 5.0 years longer on average (71.3 vs 66.3). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $5,060).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (South Africa); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
