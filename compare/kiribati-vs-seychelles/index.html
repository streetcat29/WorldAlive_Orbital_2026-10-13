Kiribati has 11% more people than Seychelles: 136,488 vs 122,730, a gap of 13,758. Kiribati is growing faster (+1.45% vs +1.13% a year), adding about 1,984 people a year against +1,384. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Seychelles is the older society: 8.7% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Kiribati, while Kiribati has more children (34.4% under 15 vs 19.8%). Seychelles is 1.6× as densely populated (260.4 vs 163.6 people per km²). People in Seychelles live 9.7 years longer on average (76.3 vs 66.6). GDP per capita is 7.6× higher in Seychelles ($19,449 vs $2,559).
