China has 12.0× the population of Philippines: 1,406,585,000 vs 116,786,962, a gap of 1.29 billion. Philippines is growing faster (+0.81% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 947,122 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 5.7% in Philippines, while Philippines has more children (27.1% under 15 vs 15.4%). Philippines is 2.6× as densely populated (385.3 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in China live 8.1 years longer on average (78.0 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in China ($13,862 vs $4,171).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Philippines); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
