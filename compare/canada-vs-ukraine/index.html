Canada has 7% more people than Ukraine: 41,651,653 vs 38,980,376, a gap of 2.7 million. Ukraine is growing faster (+2.92% vs +0.94% a year), adding about 1,136,565 people a year against +391,155. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ukraine would overtake Canada in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Canada is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 19.0% in Ukraine. Ukraine is 14.3× as densely populated (65.1 vs 4.6 people per km²). People in Canada live 7.4 years longer on average (82.1 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 9.5× higher in Canada ($55,698 vs $5,866).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Canada) and 2025 (Ukraine); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
