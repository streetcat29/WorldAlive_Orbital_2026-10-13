Costa Rica has 1.3× the population of Georgia: 5,152,950 vs 3,935,766, a gap of 1.2 million. Georgia is growing faster (+3.18% vs +0.45% a year), adding about 125,219 people a year against +23,092. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Georgia would overtake Costa Rica in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Georgia is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 12.8% in Costa Rica. Costa Rica is 1.5× as densely populated (100.0 vs 65.0 people per km²). People in Costa Rica live 6.3 years longer on average (81.0 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Costa Rica ($19,970 vs $9,692).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Costa Rica) and 2025 (Georgia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
