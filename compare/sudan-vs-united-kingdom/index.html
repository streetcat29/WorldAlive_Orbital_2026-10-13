United Kingdom has 1.3× the population of Sudan: 69,487,000 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 17.8 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +206,306. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake United Kingdom in about 14 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2039) — a projection, not a forecast. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 10.6× as densely populated (283.2 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 14.9 years longer on average (81.4 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 49.5× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $1,165).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sudan) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
