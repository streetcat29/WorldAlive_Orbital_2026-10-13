China has 10.7× the population of Mexico: 1,406,585,000 vs 131,946,900, a gap of 1.27 billion. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico, while Mexico has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 2.3× as densely populated (150.3 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in China live 2.8 years longer on average (78.0 vs 75.3).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
