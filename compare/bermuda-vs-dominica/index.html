Dominica has about the same population as Bermuda: 65,871 vs 64,555, a gap of 1,316. Bermuda is shrinking more slowly (−0.13% vs −0.51% a year), adding about -81 people a year against −333. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Bermuda would overtake Dominica in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Bermuda is the older society: 22.6% aged 65+ against 13.2% in Dominica, while Dominica has more children (17.6% under 15 vs 13.4%). Bermuda is 13.5× as densely populated (1,198.1 vs 88.7 people per km²). People in Bermuda live 11.2 years longer on average (82.5 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 12.9× higher in Bermuda ($142,250 vs $10,989).
