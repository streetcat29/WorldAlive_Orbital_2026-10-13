Romania has 4% more people than Ecuador: 19,020,271 vs 18,289,896, a gap of 730,375. Ecuador is growing faster (+0.85% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 155,074 people a year against −31,507. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ecuador would overtake Romania in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Romania is the older society: 20.1% aged 65+ against 8.6% in Ecuador, while Ecuador has more children (23.9% under 15 vs 15.6%). People in Ecuador live 1.1 years longer on average (77.6 vs 76.5). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Romania ($22,538 vs $7,125).
