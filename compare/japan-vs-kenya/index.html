Japan has 2.1× the population of Kenya: 123,366,734 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 65.8 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Japan in about 31 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2056) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 3.6× as densely populated (341.5 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in Japan live 20.2 years longer on average (84.0 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 15.2× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Japan) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
