Mexico has 4.6× the population of Venezuela: 131,946,900 vs 28,516,896, a gap of 103.4 million. Mexico is growing faster (+0.83% vs +0.39% a year), adding about 1,090,386 people a year against +111,571. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Venezuela is the older society: 10.0% aged 65+ against 8.5% in Mexico. Mexico is 2.1× as densely populated (66.7 vs 32.1 people per km²). People in Mexico live 2.6 years longer on average (75.3 vs 72.7). GDP per capita is 4.0× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $3,495).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Mexico) and 2025 (Venezuela); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
