Uzbekistan has about the same population as Poland: 37,053,428 vs 36,435,861, a gap of 617,567. Uzbekistan is growing faster (+1.88% vs −0.34% a year), adding about 698,104 people a year against −123,164. Poland is the older society: 20.8% aged 65+ against 6.1% in Uzbekistan, while Uzbekistan has more children (31.3% under 15 vs 14.5%). Poland is 1.5× as densely populated (119.8 vs 80.9 people per km²). People in Poland live 5.9 years longer on average (78.4 vs 72.5). GDP per capita is 7.2× higher in Poland ($28,420 vs $3,968).
