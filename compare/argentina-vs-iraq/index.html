Iraq has about the same population as Argentina: 47,020,774 vs 45,851,378, a gap of 1.2 million. Iraq is growing faster (+2.10% vs +0.34% a year), adding about 989,089 people a year against +155,482. Argentina is the older society: 12.6% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Iraq, while Iraq has more children (36.0% under 15 vs 21.0%). Iraq is 6.2× as densely populated (103.8 vs 16.6 people per km²). People in Argentina live 5.1 years longer on average (77.5 vs 72.4). GDP per capita is 2.8× higher in Argentina ($14,898 vs $5,410).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Argentina) and 2025 (Iraq); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
