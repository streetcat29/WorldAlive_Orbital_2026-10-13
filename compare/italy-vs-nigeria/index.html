Nigeria has 4.0× the population of Italy: 237,527,782 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 178.6 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 11.7%). People in Italy live 29.3 years longer on average (84.0 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 35.4× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Italy) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
