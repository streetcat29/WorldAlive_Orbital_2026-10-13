Iran has 1.7× the population of Myanmar: 92,417,681 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 37.6 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Iran is the older society: 8.6% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 22.0%). Myanmar is 1.5× as densely populated (82.9 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 10.8 years longer on average (77.9 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Iran ($3,924 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Iran) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
