Nigeria has 2.1× the population of DR Congo: 237,527,782 vs 112,832,473, a gap of 124.7 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs +2.06% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against +4,898,468. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, DR Congo would overtake Nigeria in about 65 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2090) — a projection, not a forecast. Nigeria is 5.4× as densely populated (250.2 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in DR Congo live 7.4 years longer on average (62.1 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Nigeria ($1,224 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Nigeria); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
