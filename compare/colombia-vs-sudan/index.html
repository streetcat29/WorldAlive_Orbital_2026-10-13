Colombia has 3% more people than Sudan: 53,425,635 vs 51,662,147, a gap of 1.8 million. Sudan is growing faster (+2.38% vs +1.01% a year), adding about 1,227,656 people a year against +542,012. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Sudan would overtake Colombia in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 3.4% in Sudan, while Sudan has more children (40.2% under 15 vs 20.0%). Colombia is 1.8× as densely populated (47.2 vs 26.8 people per km²). People in Colombia live 11.4 years longer on average (77.9 vs 66.5). GDP per capita is 7.4× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $1,165).
