Chad has 6% more people than Chile: 21,003,705 vs 19,859,921, a gap of 1.1 million. Chad is growing faster (+3.41% vs +0.48% a year), adding about 716,671 people a year against +95,379. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Chile is the older society: 14.6% aged 65+ against 2.1% in Chad, while Chad has more children (46.0% under 15 vs 16.5%). Chile is 1.7× as densely populated (26.5 vs 15.3 people per km²). People in Chile live 26.1 years longer on average (81.4 vs 55.2). GDP per capita is 17.6× higher in Chile ($17,995 vs $1,022).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chad) and 2025 (Chile); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
