Finland has about the same population as Oman: 5,646,436 vs 5,494,691, a gap of 151,745. Oman is growing faster (+3.96% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 217,398 people a year against +26,587. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Oman would overtake Finland in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Finland is the older society: 24.2% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Oman, while Oman has more children (24.3% under 15 vs 14.3%). People in Finland live 2.1 years longer on average (82.3 vs 80.2). GDP per capita is 2.8× higher in Finland ($56,149 vs $19,947).
