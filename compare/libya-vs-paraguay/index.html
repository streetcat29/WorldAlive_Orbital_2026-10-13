Libya has 6% more people than Paraguay: 7,458,555 vs 7,013,078, a gap of 445,477. Paraguay is growing faster (+1.20% vs +1.04% a year), adding about 84,431 people a year against +77,938. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Paraguay would overtake Libya in about 39 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2064) — a projection, not a forecast. Paraguay is the older society: 6.7% aged 65+ against 5.2% in Libya. Paraguay is 4.2× as densely populated (17.3 vs 4.2 people per km²). People in Paraguay live 2.9 years longer on average (74.0 vs 71.1).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Libya) and 2025 (Paraguay); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
