Faroe Islands has 1.2× the population of Turks and Caicos Islands: 54,900 vs 46,855, a gap of 8,045. Turks and Caicos Islands is growing faster (+0.69% vs +0.47% a year), adding about 321 people a year against +261. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Turks and Caicos Islands would overtake Faroe Islands in about 75 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2100) — a projection, not a forecast. Faroe Islands is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 11.7% in Turks and Caicos Islands. People in Faroe Islands live 4.9 years longer on average (83.1 vs 78.2). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Faroe Islands ($74,175 vs $37,507).
