Israel has 11% more people than Switzerland: 10,122,800 vs 9,092,436, a gap of 1.0 million. Israel is growing faster (+1.20% vs +0.96% a year), adding about 121,324 people a year against +87,271. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Switzerland is the older society: 20.4% aged 65+ against 12.7% in Israel, while Israel has more children (27.2% under 15 vs 14.9%). Israel is 2.0× as densely populated (455.1 vs 225.0 people per km²). People in Switzerland live 1.2 years longer on average (84.4 vs 83.2). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Switzerland ($114,769 vs $60,337).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Israel) and 2025 (Switzerland); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
