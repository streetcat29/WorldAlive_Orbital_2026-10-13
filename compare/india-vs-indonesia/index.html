India has 5.1× the population of Indonesia: 1,463,865,525 vs 285,721,236, a gap of 1.18 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +2,242,079. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. India is 3.3× as densely populated (483.7 vs 148.6 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in Indonesia ($5,060 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Indonesia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
