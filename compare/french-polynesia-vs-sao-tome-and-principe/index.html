French Polynesia has 1.2× the population of São Tomé and Príncipe: 282,465 vs 240,254, a gap of 42,211. São Tomé and Príncipe is growing faster (+1.98% vs +0.23% a year), adding about 4,765 people a year against +659. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, São Tomé and Príncipe would overtake French Polynesia in about 9 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2034) — a projection, not a forecast. French Polynesia is the older society: 11.9% aged 65+ against 3.9% in São Tomé and Príncipe, while São Tomé and Príncipe has more children (37.5% under 15 vs 18.5%). São Tomé and Príncipe is 3.0× as densely populated (240.5 vs 81.0 people per km²). People in French Polynesia live 14.3 years longer on average (84.2 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 5.5× higher in French Polynesia ($22,440 vs $4,084).
