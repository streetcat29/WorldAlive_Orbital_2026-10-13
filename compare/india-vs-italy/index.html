India has 24.8× the population of Italy: 1,463,865,525 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 1.40 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 11.7%). India is 2.4× as densely populated (483.7 vs 199.5 people per km²). People in Italy live 11.7 years longer on average (84.0 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 16.0× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Italy); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
