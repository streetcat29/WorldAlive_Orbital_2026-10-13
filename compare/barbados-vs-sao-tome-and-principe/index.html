Barbados has 1.2× the population of São Tomé and Príncipe: 282,623 vs 240,254, a gap of 42,369. São Tomé and Príncipe is growing faster (+1.98% vs +0.06% a year), adding about 4,765 people a year against +156. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, São Tomé and Príncipe would overtake Barbados in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Barbados is the older society: 17.1% aged 65+ against 3.9% in São Tomé and Príncipe, while São Tomé and Príncipe has more children (37.5% under 15 vs 17.1%). Barbados is 2.7× as densely populated (656.6 vs 240.5 people per km²). People in Barbados live 6.4 years longer on average (76.3 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 6.9× higher in Barbados ($28,365 vs $4,084).
