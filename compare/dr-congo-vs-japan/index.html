Japan has 9% more people than DR Congo: 123,366,734 vs 112,832,473, a gap of 10.5 million. DR Congo is growing faster (+3.20% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 3,613,456 people a year against −607,141. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, DR Congo would overtake Japan in about 2 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2027) — a projection, not a forecast. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 3.1% in DR Congo, while DR Congo has more children (45.9% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 7.3× as densely populated (341.5 vs 46.7 people per km²). People in Japan live 22.0 years longer on average (84.0 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 44.6× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $807).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (DR Congo) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
