Liberia has about the same population as Norway: 5,731,206 vs 5,610,870, a gap of 120,336. Liberia is growing faster (+2.09% vs +0.69% a year), adding about 119,629 people a year against +38,724. Norway is the older society: 19.1% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Liberia, while Liberia has more children (39.1% under 15 vs 15.9%). Liberia is 3.8× as densely populated (57.0 vs 15.2 people per km²). People in Norway live 20.8 years longer on average (83.2 vs 62.3). GDP per capita is 103.3× higher in Norway ($94,594 vs $915).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Liberia) and 2025 (Norway); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
