Türkiye has 1.7× the population of South Korea: 85,878,556 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 34.2 million. Türkiye is growing faster (+0.42% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 360,651 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 10.6% in Türkiye, while Türkiye has more children (21.0% under 15 vs 10.2%). South Korea is 4.8× as densely populated (529.8 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in South Korea live 6.2 years longer on average (83.6 vs 77.4). GDP per capita is 1.9× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $18,599).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Korea) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
