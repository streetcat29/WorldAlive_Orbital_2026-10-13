Nigeria has 3.4× the population of United Kingdom: 237,527,782 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 168.0 million. Nigeria is growing faster (+2.06% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 4,898,468 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Nigeria, while Nigeria has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 17.0%). People in United Kingdom live 26.8 years longer on average (81.4 vs 54.6). GDP per capita is 47.1× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $1,224).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nigeria) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
