Germany has 1.2× the population of United Kingdom: 83,491,249 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 14.0 million. United Kingdom is growing faster (+0.30% vs −0.03% a year), adding about 206,306 people a year against −25,340. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, United Kingdom would overtake Germany in about 56 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2081) — a projection, not a forecast. Germany is the older society: 23.7% aged 65+ against 19.7% in United Kingdom, while United Kingdom has more children (17.0% under 15 vs 13.9%).
