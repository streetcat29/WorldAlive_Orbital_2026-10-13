China has 10.4× the population of Ethiopia: 1,406,585,000 vs 135,472,051, a gap of 1.27 billion. Ethiopia is growing faster (+2.55% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 3,455,994 people a year against −2,387,972. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Ethiopia would overtake China in about 86 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2111) — a projection, not a forecast. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Ethiopia, while Ethiopia has more children (38.8% under 15 vs 15.4%). China is 1.3× as densely populated (150.3 vs 114.0 people per km²). People in China live 10.4 years longer on average (78.0 vs 67.6). GDP per capita is 14.9× higher in China ($13,862 vs $933).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Ethiopia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
