Egypt has 2.2× the population of Myanmar: 118,365,995 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 63.5 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.64% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +351,682. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Myanmar is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 24.1%). Egypt is 1.4× as densely populated (115.1 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Egypt live 4.7 years longer on average (71.8 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Egypt ($3,086 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Myanmar); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
