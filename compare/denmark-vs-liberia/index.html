Denmark has 5% more people than Liberia: 6,009,169 vs 5,731,206, a gap of 277,963. Liberia is growing faster (+2.09% vs +0.54% a year), adding about 119,629 people a year against +32,263. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Liberia would overtake Denmark in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Denmark is the older society: 21.1% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Liberia, while Liberia has more children (39.1% under 15 vs 15.6%). Denmark is 2.6× as densely populated (148.7 vs 57.0 people per km²). People in Denmark live 19.9 years longer on average (82.3 vs 62.3). GDP per capita is 84.1× higher in Denmark ($76,970 vs $915).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Denmark) and 2025 (Liberia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
