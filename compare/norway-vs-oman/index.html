Norway has about the same population as Oman: 5,610,870 vs 5,494,691, a gap of 116,179. Oman is growing faster (+3.96% vs +0.69% a year), adding about 217,398 people a year against +38,724. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Oman would overtake Norway in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. Norway is the older society: 19.1% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Oman, while Oman has more children (24.3% under 15 vs 15.9%). People in Norway live 2.9 years longer on average (83.2 vs 80.2). GDP per capita is 4.7× higher in Norway ($94,594 vs $19,947).
