Ireland has about the same population as New Zealand: 5,484,367 vs 5,324,700, a gap of 159,667. Ireland is growing faster (+1.63% vs +0.65% a year), adding about 89,300 people a year against +34,814. New Zealand is the older society: 17.6% aged 65+ against 16.2% in Ireland, while Ireland has more children (18.1% under 15 vs 17.9%). Ireland is 3.9× as densely populated (77.1 vs 19.7 people per km²). People in Ireland live 1.0 years longer on average (83.0 vs 82.0). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in Ireland ($131,592 vs $49,591).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ireland) and 2025 (New Zealand); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
