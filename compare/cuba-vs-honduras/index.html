Honduras has about the same population as Cuba: 11,005,850 vs 10,937,203, a gap of 68,647. Honduras is growing faster (+1.65% vs −0.39% a year), adding about 181,638 people a year against −42,497. Cuba is the older society: 17.0% aged 65+ against 4.5% in Honduras, while Honduras has more children (30.3% under 15 vs 15.0%). People in Cuba live 5.2 years longer on average (78.3 vs 73.0). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in Cuba ($9,605 vs $3,598).
