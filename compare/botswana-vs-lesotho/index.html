Botswana has 8% more people than Lesotho: 2,562,122 vs 2,363,325, a gap of 198,797. Botswana is growing faster (+1.61% vs +1.10% a year), adding about 41,314 people a year against +26,045. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Lesotho is 17.4× as densely populated (76.1 vs 4.4 people per km²). People in Botswana live 11.5 years longer on average (69.3 vs 57.8). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in Botswana ($7,778 vs $1,089).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Botswana) and 2025 (Lesotho); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
