United States has 4.8× the population of Thailand: 341,784,857 vs 71,619,863, a gap of 270.2 million. United States is growing faster (+0.52% vs −0.07% a year), adding about 1,785,717 people a year against −48,132. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United States is the older society: 18.4% aged 65+ against 16.0% in Thailand. Thailand is 3.8× as densely populated (140.3 vs 36.8 people per km²). People in United States live 2.3 years longer on average (78.9 vs 76.6). GDP per capita is 11.2× higher in United States ($90,027 vs $8,057).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Thailand) and 2025 (United States); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
