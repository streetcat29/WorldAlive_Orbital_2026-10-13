Bangladesh has 3.3× the population of Colombia: 175,686,899 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 122.3 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +1.01% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +542,012. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 20.0%). Bangladesh is 28.0× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 3.0 years longer on average (77.9 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Colombia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
