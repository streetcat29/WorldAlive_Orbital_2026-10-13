Saint Martin has 2.6× the population of Tuvalu: 24,941 vs 9,492, a gap of 15,449. Tuvalu is shrinking more slowly (−1.61% vs −4.65% a year), adding about -153 people a year against −1,161. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tuvalu would overtake Saint Martin in about 32 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2057) — a projection, not a forecast. Saint Martin is 1.7× as densely populated (550.3 vs 327.2 people per km²). People in Saint Martin live 13.2 years longer on average (80.4 vs 67.3). GDP per capita is 3.6× higher in Saint Martin ($21,668 vs $6,041).
