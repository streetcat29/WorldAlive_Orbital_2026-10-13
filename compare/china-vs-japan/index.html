China has 11.4× the population of Japan: 1,406,585,000 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 1.28 billion. China is shrinking more slowly (−0.17% vs −0.49% a year), adding about -2,387,972 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 14.9% in China, while China has more children (15.4% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 2.3× as densely populated (341.5 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in Japan live 6.0 years longer on average (84.0 vs 78.0). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $13,862).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
