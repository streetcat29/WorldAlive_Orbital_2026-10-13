Cambodia has 5% more people than Zimbabwe: 17,847,982 vs 16,950,795, a gap of 897,187. Zimbabwe is growing faster (+1.88% vs +1.18% a year), adding about 319,413 people a year against +210,416. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Zimbabwe would overtake Cambodia in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Cambodia is the older society: 6.4% aged 65+ against 3.6% in Zimbabwe, while Zimbabwe has more children (40.3% under 15 vs 29.5%). Cambodia is 2.3× as densely populated (98.7 vs 42.2 people per km²). People in Cambodia live 7.8 years longer on average (70.8 vs 63.1).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cambodia) and 2025 (Zimbabwe); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
