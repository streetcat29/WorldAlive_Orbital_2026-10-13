Kyrgyzstan has 5% more people than Nicaragua: 7,343,064 vs 7,007,502, a gap of 335,562. Kyrgyzstan is growing faster (+1.66% vs +1.31% a year), adding about 122,207 people a year against +91,963. People in Nicaragua live 2.7 years longer on average (75.1 vs 72.4).
