Egypt has 1.7× the population of United Kingdom: 118,365,995 vs 69,487,000, a gap of 48.9 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.30% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +206,306. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. United Kingdom is the older society: 19.7% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 17.0%). United Kingdom is 2.5× as densely populated (283.2 vs 115.1 people per km²). People in United Kingdom live 9.6 years longer on average (81.4 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 18.7× higher in United Kingdom ($57,602 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (United Kingdom); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
