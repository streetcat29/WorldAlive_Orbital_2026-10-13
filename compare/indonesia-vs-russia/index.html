Indonesia has 2.0× the population of Russia: 285,721,236 vs 143,513,328, a gap of 142.2 million. Indonesia is growing faster (+0.78% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 2,242,079 people a year against −156,235. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Russia is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Indonesia, while Indonesia has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 17.0%). Indonesia is 16.9× as densely populated (148.6 vs 8.8 people per km²). People in Russia live 2.2 years longer on average (73.4 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 3.5× higher in Russia ($17,547 vs $5,060).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Russia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
