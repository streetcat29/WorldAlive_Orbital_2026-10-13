Morocco has 4% more people than Uzbekistan: 38,430,770 vs 37,053,428, a gap of 1.4 million. Uzbekistan is growing faster (+1.88% vs +0.91% a year), adding about 698,104 people a year against +351,197. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Uzbekistan would overtake Morocco in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Morocco is the older society: 8.5% aged 65+ against 6.1% in Uzbekistan, while Uzbekistan has more children (31.3% under 15 vs 25.2%). People in Morocco live 3.0 years longer on average (75.5 vs 72.5).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Morocco) and 2025 (Uzbekistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
