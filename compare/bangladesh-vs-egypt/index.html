Bangladesh has 1.5× the population of Egypt: 175,686,899 vs 118,365,995, a gap of 57.3 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs +1.22% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against +2,137,485. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Egypt would overtake Bangladesh in about 116 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2141) — a projection, not a forecast. Bangladesh is the older society: 6.7% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 27.6%). Bangladesh is 11.5× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 115.1 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 3.1 years longer on average (74.9 vs 71.8).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Egypt); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
