Myanmar vs Türkiye

Türkiye has 1.6× the population of Myanmar: 85,878,556 vs 54,850,648, a gap of 31.0 million. Myanmar is growing faster (+0.64% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 351,682 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Myanmar, while Myanmar has more children (24.1% under 15 vs 21.0%). Türkiye is 1.3× as densely populated (110.9 vs 82.9 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 10.3 years longer on average (77.4 vs 67.1). GDP per capita is 12.5× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $1,489).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Myanmar) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
