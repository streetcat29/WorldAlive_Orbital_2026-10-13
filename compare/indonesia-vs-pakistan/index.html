Indonesia has 12% more people than Pakistan: 285,721,236 vs 255,219,554, a gap of 30.5 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +2,242,079. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Pakistan would overtake Indonesia in about 15 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2040) — a projection, not a forecast. Indonesia is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 24.2%). Pakistan is 2.2× as densely populated (321.1 vs 148.6 people per km²). People in Indonesia live 3.5 years longer on average (71.3 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Indonesia ($5,060 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
