Indonesia has 2.2× the population of Mexico: 285,721,236 vs 131,946,900, a gap of 153.8 million. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.78% vs +0.83% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Indonesia is 2.2× as densely populated (148.6 vs 66.7 people per km²). People in Mexico live 4.0 years longer on average (75.3 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 2.7× higher in Mexico ($13,889 vs $5,060).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Mexico); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
