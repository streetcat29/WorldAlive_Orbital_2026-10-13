Chad has about the same population as Kazakhstan: 21,003,705 vs 20,843,754, a gap of 159,951. Chad is growing faster (+3.41% vs +1.21% a year), adding about 716,671 people a year against +252,709. Kazakhstan is the older society: 9.0% aged 65+ against 2.1% in Chad, while Chad has more children (46.0% under 15 vs 29.1%). Chad is 2.0× as densely populated (15.3 vs 7.5 people per km²). People in Kazakhstan live 19.3 years longer on average (74.5 vs 55.2). GDP per capita is 14.4× higher in Kazakhstan ($14,692 vs $1,022).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Chad) and 2025 (Kazakhstan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
