Indonesia has 2.3× the population of Japan: 285,721,236 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 162.4 million. Indonesia is growing faster (+0.78% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 2,242,079 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Indonesia, while Indonesia has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 11.2%). Japan is 2.3× as densely populated (341.5 vs 148.6 people per km²). People in Japan live 12.7 years longer on average (84.0 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 7.1× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $5,060).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
