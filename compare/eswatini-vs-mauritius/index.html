Eswatini has about the same population as Mauritius: 1,256,174 vs 1,243,741, a gap of 12,433. Eswatini is growing faster (+1.07% vs −0.16% a year), adding about 13,423 people a year against −2,036. Mauritius is the older society: 14.1% aged 65+ against 4.3% in Eswatini, while Eswatini has more children (33.0% under 15 vs 14.5%). Mauritius is 8.7× as densely populated (625.3 vs 71.5 people per km²). People in Mauritius live 9.5 years longer on average (73.8 vs 64.3). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Mauritius ($12,991 vs $4,108).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Eswatini) and 2025 (Mauritius); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
