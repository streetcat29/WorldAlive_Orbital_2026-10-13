Bangladesh has 1.5× the population of Philippines: 175,686,899 vs 116,786,962, a gap of 58.9 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs +0.81% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against +947,122. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Bangladesh is 3.4× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 385.3 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 5.0 years longer on average (74.9 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 1.6× higher in Philippines ($4,171 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Philippines); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
