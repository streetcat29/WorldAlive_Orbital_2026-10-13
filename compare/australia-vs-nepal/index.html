Nepal has 7% more people than Australia: 29,618,118 vs 27,614,411, a gap of 2.0 million. Australia is growing faster (+1.53% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 423,354 people a year against −32,918. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Australia would overtake Nepal in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Australia is the older society: 18.1% aged 65+ against 6.6% in Nepal, while Nepal has more children (28.1% under 15 vs 17.7%). Nepal is 59.8× as densely populated (207.1 vs 3.5 people per km²). People in Australia live 12.4 years longer on average (83.1 vs 70.6). GDP per capita is 42.4× higher in Australia ($65,130 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Australia) and 2025 (Nepal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
