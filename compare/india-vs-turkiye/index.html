India has 17.0× the population of Türkiye: 1,463,865,525 vs 85,878,556, a gap of 1.38 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against +360,651. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 21.0%). India is 4.4× as densely populated (483.7 vs 110.9 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 5.2 years longer on average (77.4 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 6.9× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
