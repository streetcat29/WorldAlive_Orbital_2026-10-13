Switzerland has 6% more people than Togo: 9,092,436 vs 8,591,626, a gap of 500,810. Togo is growing faster (+2.18% vs +0.96% a year), adding about 187,090 people a year against +87,271. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Togo would overtake Switzerland in about 5 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2030) — a projection, not a forecast. Switzerland is the older society: 20.4% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Togo, while Togo has more children (40.4% under 15 vs 14.9%). Switzerland is 1.5× as densely populated (225.0 vs 151.2 people per km²). People in Switzerland live 21.5 years longer on average (84.4 vs 62.9). GDP per capita is 82.9× higher in Switzerland ($114,769 vs $1,384).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Switzerland) and 2025 (Togo); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
