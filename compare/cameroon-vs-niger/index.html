Cameroon has 7% more people than Niger: 29,879,337 vs 27,917,831, a gap of 2.0 million. Niger is growing faster (+3.22% vs +2.56% a year), adding about 899,764 people a year against +765,311. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Niger would overtake Cameroon in about 10 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2035) — a projection, not a forecast. Cameroon is 2.9× as densely populated (60.0 vs 20.7 people per km²). People in Cameroon live 2.5 years longer on average (64.0 vs 61.4). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Cameroon ($1,972 vs $775).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cameroon) and 2025 (Niger); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
