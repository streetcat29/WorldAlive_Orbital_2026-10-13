Malaysia has 4% more people than Peru: 35,977,838 vs 34,576,665, a gap of 1.4 million. Malaysia is growing faster (+1.17% vs +1.04% a year), adding about 422,638 people a year against +360,692. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Peru is the older society: 9.5% aged 65+ against 8.0% in Malaysia. Malaysia is 4.0× as densely populated (106.9 vs 26.4 people per km²). People in Peru live 1.1 years longer on average (77.9 vs 76.8). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Malaysia ($13,125 vs $9,684).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Malaysia) and 2025 (Peru); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
