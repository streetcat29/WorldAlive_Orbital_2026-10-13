Guatemala has 5% more people than Cambodia: 18,687,881 vs 17,847,982, a gap of 839,899. Guatemala is growing faster (+1.52% vs +1.18% a year), adding about 283,664 people a year against +210,416. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Cambodia is the older society: 6.4% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Guatemala, while Guatemala has more children (31.0% under 15 vs 29.5%). Guatemala is 1.7× as densely populated (169.1 vs 98.7 people per km²). People in Guatemala live 1.9 years longer on average (72.7 vs 70.8). GDP per capita is 2.3× higher in Guatemala ($6,598 vs $2,872).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cambodia) and 2025 (Guatemala); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
