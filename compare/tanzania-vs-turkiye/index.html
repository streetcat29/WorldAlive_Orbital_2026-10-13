Tanzania vs Türkiye

Türkiye has 1.2× the population of Tanzania: 85,878,556 vs 70,545,865, a gap of 15.3 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +0.42% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +360,651. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Tanzania would overtake Türkiye in about 8 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2033) — a projection, not a forecast. Türkiye is the older society: 10.6% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 21.0%). Türkiye is 1.5× as densely populated (110.9 vs 75.2 people per km²). People in Türkiye live 10.2 years longer on average (77.4 vs 67.2). GDP per capita is 14.1× higher in Türkiye ($18,599 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Tanzania) and 2025 (Türkiye); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
