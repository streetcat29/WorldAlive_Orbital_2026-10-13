São Tomé and Príncipe has 1.4× the population of Guam: 240,254 vs 168,999, a gap of 71,255. São Tomé and Príncipe is growing faster (+1.98% vs +0.73% a year), adding about 4,765 people a year against +1,226. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Guam is the older society: 13.1% aged 65+ against 3.9% in São Tomé and Príncipe, while São Tomé and Príncipe has more children (37.5% under 15 vs 25.9%). People in Guam live 7.5 years longer on average (77.4 vs 69.9). GDP per capita is 10.2× higher in Guam ($41,833 vs $4,084).
