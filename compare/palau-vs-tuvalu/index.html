Palau has 1.9× the population of Tuvalu: 17,663 vs 9,492, a gap of 8,171. Palau is shrinking more slowly (−0.18% vs −1.61% a year), adding about -32 people a year against −153. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Palau is the older society: 11.8% aged 65+ against 7.0% in Tuvalu, while Tuvalu has more children (33.0% under 15 vs 17.9%). Tuvalu is 8.5× as densely populated (327.2 vs 38.5 people per km²). People in Palau live 2.1 years longer on average (69.4 vs 67.3). GDP per capita is 3.2× higher in Palau ($19,532 vs $6,041).
