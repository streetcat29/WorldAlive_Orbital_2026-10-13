Palestine has about the same population as Mauritania: 5,413,596 vs 5,315,065, a gap of 98,531. Mauritania is growing faster (+2.78% vs +2.33% a year), adding about 147,703 people a year against +125,897. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Mauritania would overtake Palestine in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Palestine is 176.0× as densely populated (857.4 vs 4.9 people per km²). GDP per capita is 1.4× higher in Palestine ($3,171 vs $2,198).
