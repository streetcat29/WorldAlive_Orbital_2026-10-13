Indonesia has 5.3× the population of Colombia: 285,721,236 vs 53,425,635, a gap of 232.3 million. Colombia is growing faster (+1.01% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 542,012 people a year against +2,242,079. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is narrowing, but too slowly to close within two centuries. Colombia is the older society: 10.2% aged 65+ against 7.5% in Indonesia, while Indonesia has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 20.0%). Indonesia is 3.2× as densely populated (148.6 vs 47.2 people per km²). People in Colombia live 6.6 years longer on average (77.9 vs 71.3). GDP per capita is 1.7× higher in Colombia ($8,562 vs $5,060).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Colombia) and 2025 (Indonesia); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
