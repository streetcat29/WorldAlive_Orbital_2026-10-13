Aruba has 9% more people than Saint Vincent and the Grenadines: 108,785 vs 99,924, a gap of 8,861. Aruba is growing faster (+0.73% vs −0.69% a year), adding about 793 people a year against −690. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Aruba is the older society: 17.8% aged 65+ against 12.3% in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, while Saint Vincent and the Grenadines has more children (20.9% under 15 vs 16.5%). Aruba is 2.3× as densely populated (596.4 vs 259.8 people per km²). People in Aruba live 5.1 years longer on average (76.5 vs 71.4). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in Aruba ($38,591 vs $12,562).
