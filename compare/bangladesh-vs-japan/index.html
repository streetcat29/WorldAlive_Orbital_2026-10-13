Bangladesh has 1.4× the population of Japan: 175,686,899 vs 123,366,734, a gap of 52.3 million. Bangladesh is growing faster (+1.22% vs −0.49% a year), adding about 2,137,485 people a year against −607,141. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Japan is the older society: 30.0% aged 65+ against 6.7% in Bangladesh, while Bangladesh has more children (27.6% under 15 vs 11.2%). Bangladesh is 3.9× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 341.5 people per km²). People in Japan live 9.1 years longer on average (84.0 vs 74.9). GDP per capita is 13.8× higher in Japan ($35,951 vs $2,597).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Japan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
