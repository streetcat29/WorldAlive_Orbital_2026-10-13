Maldives has about the same population as Cape Verde: 529,676 vs 527,326, a gap of 2,350. Cape Verde is growing faster (+0.47% vs +0.35% a year), adding about 2,455 people a year against +1,880. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Cape Verde would overtake Maldives in about 4 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2029) — a projection, not a forecast. Cape Verde is the older society: 7.2% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Maldives. Maldives is 13.6× as densely populated (1,765.1 vs 129.6 people per km²). People in Maldives live 5.1 years longer on average (81.3 vs 76.2). GDP per capita is 2.5× higher in Maldives ($14,615 vs $5,796).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Cape Verde) and 2025 (Maldives); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
