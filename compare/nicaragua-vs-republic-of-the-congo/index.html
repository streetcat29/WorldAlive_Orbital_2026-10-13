Nicaragua has 8% more people than Republic of the Congo: 7,007,502 vs 6,484,437, a gap of 523,065. Republic of the Congo is growing faster (+2.36% vs +1.31% a year), adding about 153,273 people a year against +91,963. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Republic of the Congo would overtake Nicaragua in about 7 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2032) — a projection, not a forecast. Nicaragua is the older society: 5.7% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Republic of the Congo, while Republic of the Congo has more children (39.9% under 15 vs 28.3%). Nicaragua is 3.1× as densely populated (56.7 vs 18.1 people per km²). People in Nicaragua live 9.1 years longer on average (75.1 vs 66.0). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Nicaragua ($3,173 vs $2,515).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Nicaragua) and 2025 (Republic of the Congo); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
