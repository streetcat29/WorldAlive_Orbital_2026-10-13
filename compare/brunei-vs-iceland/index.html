Brunei has 1.2× the population of Iceland: 466,330 vs 392,404, a gap of 73,926. Iceland is growing faster (+1.51% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 5,943 people a year against +3,623. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iceland would overtake Brunei in about 23 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2048) — a projection, not a forecast. Iceland is the older society: 16.0% aged 65+ against 7.2% in Brunei, while Brunei has more children (20.6% under 15 vs 17.5%). Brunei is 22.8× as densely populated (87.1 vs 3.8 people per km²). People in Iceland live 7.3 years longer on average (82.8 vs 75.5). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in Iceland ($98,323 vs $32,235).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Brunei) and 2025 (Iceland); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
