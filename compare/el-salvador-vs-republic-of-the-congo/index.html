Republic of the Congo has about the same population as El Salvador: 6,484,437 vs 6,365,503, a gap of 118,934. Republic of the Congo is growing faster (+2.36% vs +0.43% a year), adding about 153,273 people a year against +27,369. El Salvador is the older society: 8.3% aged 65+ against 3.1% in Republic of the Congo, while Republic of the Congo has more children (39.9% under 15 vs 24.4%). El Salvador is 16.8× as densely populated (304.5 vs 18.1 people per km²). People in El Salvador live 6.3 years longer on average (72.3 vs 66.0). GDP per capita is 2.3× higher in El Salvador ($5,767 vs $2,515).
