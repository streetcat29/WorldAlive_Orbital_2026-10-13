Indonesia has 5.0× the population of Kenya: 285,721,236 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 228.2 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +0.78% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +2,242,079. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Indonesia in about 140 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2165) — a projection, not a forecast. Indonesia is the older society: 7.5% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 24.2%). Indonesia is 1.6× as densely populated (148.6 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in Indonesia live 7.5 years longer on average (71.3 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 2.1× higher in Indonesia ($5,060 vs $2,363).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Indonesia) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
