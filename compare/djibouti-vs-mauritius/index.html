Mauritius has 5% more people than Djibouti: 1,243,741 vs 1,184,076, a gap of 59,665. Djibouti is growing faster (+1.31% vs −0.16% a year), adding about 15,454 people a year against −2,036. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Djibouti would overtake Mauritius in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Mauritius is the older society: 14.1% aged 65+ against 5.0% in Djibouti, while Djibouti has more children (28.8% under 15 vs 14.5%). Mauritius is 12.6× as densely populated (625.3 vs 49.7 people per km²). People in Mauritius live 7.6 years longer on average (73.8 vs 66.2). GDP per capita is 3.3× higher in Mauritius ($12,991 vs $3,906).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Djibouti) and 2025 (Mauritius); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
