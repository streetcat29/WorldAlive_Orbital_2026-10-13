Tanzania has 9% more people than South Africa: 70,545,865 vs 64,747,319, a gap of 5.8 million. Tanzania is growing faster (+2.86% vs +1.15% a year), adding about 2,014,190 people a year against +744,395. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Africa is the older society: 6.9% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Tanzania, while Tanzania has more children (42.3% under 15 vs 25.7%). Tanzania is 1.4× as densely populated (75.2 vs 52.1 people per km²). GDP per capita is 5.0× higher in South Africa ($6,598 vs $1,319).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (South Africa) and 2025 (Tanzania); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
