Pakistan has 5.4× the population of Algeria: 255,219,554 vs 47,435,312, a gap of 207.8 million. Pakistan is growing faster (+1.56% vs +1.32% a year), adding about 3,981,282 people a year against +625,105. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Algeria is the older society: 6.8% aged 65+ against 4.4% in Pakistan, while Pakistan has more children (36.2% under 15 vs 29.9%). Pakistan is 16.6× as densely populated (321.1 vs 19.4 people per km²). People in Algeria live 8.7 years longer on average (76.5 vs 67.8). GDP per capita is 3.8× higher in Algeria ($6,051 vs $1,596).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Algeria) and 2025 (Pakistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
