India has 4% more people than China: 1,463,865,525 vs 1,406,585,000, a gap of 57.3 million. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.17% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −2,387,972. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. China is the older society: 14.9% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 15.4%). India is 3.2× as densely populated (483.7 vs 150.3 people per km²). People in China live 5.8 years longer on average (78.0 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 5.1× higher in China ($13,862 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (China) and 2025 (India); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
