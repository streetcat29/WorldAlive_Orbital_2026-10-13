Bangladesh has 3.1× the population of Kenya: 175,686,899 vs 57,532,493, a gap of 118.2 million. Kenya is growing faster (+1.93% vs +1.22% a year), adding about 1,110,192 people a year against +2,137,485. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Kenya would overtake Bangladesh in about 157 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2182) — a projection, not a forecast. Bangladesh is the older society: 6.7% aged 65+ against 3.0% in Kenya, while Kenya has more children (36.3% under 15 vs 27.6%). Bangladesh is 13.8× as densely populated (1,319.2 vs 95.3 people per km²). People in Bangladesh live 11.1 years longer on average (74.9 vs 63.8).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Bangladesh) and 2025 (Kenya); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
