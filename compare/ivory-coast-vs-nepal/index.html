Ivory Coast has 10% more people than Nepal: 32,711,547 vs 29,618,118, a gap of 3.1 million. Ivory Coast is growing faster (+2.40% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 786,702 people a year against −32,918. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Nepal is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 2.7% in Ivory Coast, while Ivory Coast has more children (40.5% under 15 vs 28.1%). Nepal is 2.1× as densely populated (207.1 vs 98.0 people per km²). People in Nepal live 8.5 years longer on average (70.6 vs 62.1). GDP per capita is 2.0× higher in Ivory Coast ($3,050 vs $1,536).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Ivory Coast) and 2025 (Nepal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
