Egypt has 2.0× the population of Italy: 118,365,995 vs 58,915,656, a gap of 59.5 million. Egypt is growing faster (+1.56% vs −0.06% a year), adding about 1,841,995 people a year against −37,036. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Italy is the older society: 25.1% aged 65+ against 5.3% in Egypt, while Egypt has more children (31.6% under 15 vs 11.7%). Italy is 1.7× as densely populated (199.5 vs 115.1 people per km²). People in Italy live 12.1 years longer on average (84.0 vs 71.8). GDP per capita is 14.0× higher in Italy ($43,309 vs $3,086).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Egypt) and 2025 (Italy); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
