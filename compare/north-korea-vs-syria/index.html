North Korea has 4% more people than Syria: 26,571,036 vs 25,620,427, a gap of 950,609. Syria is growing faster (+3.77% vs +0.27% a year), adding about 965,638 people a year against +72,311. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Syria would overtake North Korea in about 1 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2026) — a projection, not a forecast. North Korea is the older society: 12.7% aged 65+ against 4.8% in Syria, while Syria has more children (28.4% under 15 vs 18.9%). North Korea is 1.7× as densely populated (219.4 vs 128.5 people per km²). People in North Korea live 1.2 years longer on average (73.7 vs 72.6).
