India has 28.3× the population of South Korea: 1,463,865,525 vs 51,684,564, a gap of 1.41 billion. India is growing faster (+0.89% vs −0.13% a year), adding about 12,987,174 people a year against −66,458. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. South Korea is the older society: 20.3% aged 65+ against 7.4% in India, while India has more children (24.2% under 15 vs 10.2%). People in South Korea live 11.4 years longer on average (83.6 vs 72.2). GDP per capita is 13.4× higher in South Korea ($36,227 vs $2,702).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (India) and 2025 (South Korea); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
