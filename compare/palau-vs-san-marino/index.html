San Marino has 1.9× the population of Palau: 34,109 vs 17,663, a gap of 16,446. San Marino is growing faster (+0.39% vs −0.18% a year), adding about 132 people a year against −32. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. San Marino is 14.6× as densely populated (564.3 vs 38.5 people per km²). People in San Marino live 16.4 years longer on average (85.8 vs 69.4). GDP per capita is 3.1× higher in San Marino ($59,871 vs $19,532).
