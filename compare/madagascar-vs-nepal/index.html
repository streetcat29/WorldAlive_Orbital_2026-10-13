Madagascar has 11% more people than Nepal: 32,740,678 vs 29,618,118, a gap of 3.1 million. Madagascar is growing faster (+2.40% vs −0.11% a year), adding about 785,059 people a year against −32,918. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Nepal is the older society: 6.6% aged 65+ against 3.5% in Madagascar, while Madagascar has more children (38.9% under 15 vs 28.1%). Nepal is 3.9× as densely populated (207.1 vs 53.6 people per km²). People in Nepal live 6.8 years longer on average (70.6 vs 63.8). GDP per capita is 2.6× higher in Nepal ($1,536 vs $599).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Madagascar) and 2025 (Nepal); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
