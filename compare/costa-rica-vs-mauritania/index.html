Mauritania has 3% more people than Costa Rica: 5,315,065 vs 5,152,950, a gap of 162,115. Mauritania is growing faster (+2.78% vs +0.45% a year), adding about 147,703 people a year against +23,092. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. GDP per capita is 9.1× higher in Costa Rica ($19,970 vs $2,198).
