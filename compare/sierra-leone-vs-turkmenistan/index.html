Sierra Leone has 1.2× the population of Turkmenistan: 8,819,794 vs 7,618,847, a gap of 1.2 million. Sierra Leone is growing faster (+2.04% vs +1.65% a year), adding about 179,588 people a year against +125,375. At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Turkmenistan is the older society: 4.8% aged 65+ against 3.3% in Sierra Leone, while Sierra Leone has more children (37.7% under 15 vs 31.2%). Sierra Leone is 7.5× as densely populated (117.2 vs 15.7 people per km²). People in Turkmenistan live 8.2 years longer on average (70.2 vs 62.0). GDP per capita is 7.7× higher in Turkmenistan ($6,540 vs $846).

Country/territory population bases: World Bank 2025 (Sierra Leone) and 2025 (Turkmenistan); growth years 2025 / 2025. Annual source values, not live counts; years may differ. Growth is national, not local.
