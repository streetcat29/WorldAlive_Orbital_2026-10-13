Vietnam has 10% more people than Iran: 101,598,527 vs 92,417,681, a gap of 9.2 million. Iran is growing faster (+0.92% vs +0.60% a year), adding about 853,875 people a year against +612,685. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Iran would overtake Vietnam in about 30 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2055) — a projection, not a forecast. Vietnam is 5.7× as densely populated (320.2 vs 55.8 people per km²). People in Iran live 3.1 years longer on average (77.9 vs 74.7). GDP per capita is 1.3× higher in Vietnam ($5,066 vs $3,924).
