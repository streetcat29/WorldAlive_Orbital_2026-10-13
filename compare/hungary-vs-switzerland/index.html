Hungary has 5% more people than Switzerland: 9,514,251 vs 9,092,436, a gap of 421,815. Switzerland is growing faster (+0.96% vs −0.50% a year), adding about 87,271 people a year against −47,694. Using continuous exponential growth from the stated base year, if these rates held, Switzerland would overtake Hungary in about 3 years (from the 2025 population bases, around 2028) — a projection, not a forecast. Switzerland is 2.1× as densely populated (225.0 vs 105.1 people per km²). People in Switzerland live 7.7 years longer on average (84.4 vs 76.7). GDP per capita is 4.4× higher in Switzerland ($114,769 vs $25,907).
