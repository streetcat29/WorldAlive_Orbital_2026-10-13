Saint Lucia has 1.2× the population of Curaçao: 180,149 vs 156,263, a gap of 23,886. Both are growing at almost the same pace (+0.19% vs +0.23% a year). At these source-year rates the modelled relative population gap is widening. Curaçao is the older society: 17.3% aged 65+ against 10.0% in Saint Lucia, while Saint Lucia has more children (17.2% under 15 vs 14.6%). People in Curaçao live 4.1 years longer on average (77.0 vs 72.8). GDP per capita is 1.5× higher in Curaçao ($22,833 vs $14,746).
